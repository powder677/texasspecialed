Who Should I Contact?

For ARD Meeting Questions

Start with your child's ARD case manager or campus diagnostician.

If unresolved: Contact the Special Education Administration office for district-wide oversight.

Key Department Coordinators

Specialized programs in FBISD are managed by coordinators who ensure high standards for instruction and behavioral support:

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact: Main Office

Focus: Oversees Diagnosticians and LSSPs; primary contact for FIIE timelines.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact: Main Office

Focus: Implementation of HB 3928 and transitioning dyslexia services into the IEP framework.

Autism Specialist

Contact: Main Office

Focus: Assists with functional behavior assessments (FBAs) and state-mandated autism supplements.

Records Clerk

Contact: Main Office

Focus: Handles requests for confidential special education records and IEP files.

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Include your child's name, DOB, and campus in every email
  • Request written responses to all verbal conversations

📝 Documentation

  • Save all email correspondence and IEP records
  • Take notes during phone calls including the date and the person spoken to
  • Use certified mail for formal evaluation requests if necessary
