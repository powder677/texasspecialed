Quick Facts: Evaluations in Fort Bend ISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full Individual Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, guardians, school personnel, or community agencies.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to complete testing and the report.
  • Cost: Free of charge for families residing within district boundaries.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting scheduled within 30 calendar days of report completion.

Common Questions About Fort Bend ISD Evaluations

How do I request a special education evaluation in Fort Bend ISD?

Submit a written request to the campus principal. If your child is enrolled in an FBISD school, address a letter to the principal outlining your concerns. For children not yet in school or attending private schools within district boundaries, contact the Special Education Intake Center at 281-634-1351 or email MRWintake@fortbendisd.gov.

How long does the evaluation process take in Fort Bend ISD?

45 school days for the evaluation report. Once you sign the consent form, the district must complete testing and the written report within 45 school days. The district has an initial 15 school days to respond to your written request with consent forms or a refusal.

What tests are included in a Fort Bend ISD evaluation?

Testing is multidisciplinary. Assessments often include cognitive/IQ tests, academic achievement (reading, math, writing), speech and language evaluations, and behavioral assessments. Dyslexia is now evaluated under the IDEA framework following Texas HB 3928.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. Under Child Find law, Fort Bend ISD must identify and evaluate any child suspected of having a disability. You do not need a medical diagnosis from a physician to request school testing.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) in Fort Bend ISD?

Yes. If you disagree with the results of the district's Full Individual Evaluation (FIE), you have the right to request an IEE at public expense. Contact the Special Education Department at 281-634-1143 for assistance with the IEE request process.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

An ARD meeting within 30 calendar days. The ARD committee will meet to review evaluation results, determine eligibility for special education, and (if eligible) develop an Individualized Education Program (IEP).

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

A FIIE (sometimes called an FIE) is a comprehensive assessment conducted to determine if a child has a disability and if there is a resulting educational need for services.

Evaluations in Fort Bend ISD are conducted by licensed specialists, including Educational Diagnosticians, Licensed Specialists in School Psychology (LSSP), and Speech-Language Pathologists.

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Within 15 school days, the district provides Prior Written Notice (PWN). If testing is agreed upon, they will provide a Consent for Evaluation form.

Step 3: Evaluation Period (45 School Days)

Once you sign consent, the 45-school-day clock begins. Testing must be completed in all areas of suspected disability.

Step 4: Receive and Review Report

The district must provide you with a written copy of the evaluation report. It is recommended to review this report prior to the ARD meeting.

Step 5: Initial ARD Meeting

The ARD committee meets within 30 calendar days of the report completion to determine eligibility.

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Cognitive & Psychological

Who: LSSP or Diagnostician

Assessments of IQ, memory, and functional processing skills.

Academic Achievement

Who: Educational Diagnostician

Measures grade-level skills in reading, math, and writing.

Therapy Screenings

Who: SLP, OT, or PT

Evaluations for speech, fine motor, or gross motor needs.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with the district's evaluation, you can request an IEE at public expense. The district must then either pay for an outside evaluation or file for a due process hearing.

Contact: FBISD Special Education Department at 281-634-1143 for help with the request process.

Early Childhood Evaluations (Ages 3-5)

Fort Bend ISD offers an Early Intervention Academy for students aged 3-5. If you suspect a developmental delay in your preschool-aged child, contact Donielle Walters at 281-634-2728 or email EarlyInterventionAcademy@fortbendisd.gov.
