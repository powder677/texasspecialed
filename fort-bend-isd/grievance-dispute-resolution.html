⚠️ 2026 Timeline Alert

Local grievances with Fort Bend ISD (policy FNG Local) must be filed within 15 district business days of the incident. TEA state-level complaints must be submitted within one calendar year of the alleged violation. Missing these deadlines often results in immediate dismissal of your case.

File a Special Education Complaint in Fort Bend ISD

Navigate the complex dispute resolution process in FBISD. Learn how to escalate concerns regarding evaluations, IEP implementation, and your child's legal rights.

Quick Answer: To resolve a dispute in Fort Bend ISD, you should first attempt to speak with your child's teacher or the campus principal. If informal steps fail, you can file a formal Level One grievance with the campus principal under policy FNG(LOCAL). For legal violations of the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA), you may file a state complaint with the Texas Education Agency (TEA). Fort Bend ISD Special Education is led by Dahria Driskell, Executive Director, and can be reached at (281) 634-1143.

Dispute Resolution Quick Facts

  • Board Policy: FNG(LOCAL) governs parent/student complaints.
  • Primary Contact: Special Education Department at (281) 634-1143.
  • 504 Coordinator: Catharine Jacobson (281-634-5301) for disability discrimination.
  • TEA Deadline: 1 year from the date of violation.
  • FBISD Deadline: 15 district business days for local grievances.

Common Questions About FBISD Complaints

How do I file a grievance with Fort Bend ISD?

A formal grievance must be submitted in writing using the Level One complaint form, available from the Department of Student Affairs. This form is typically turned into the campus principal. In 2026, the district continues to emphasize that retaliation against parents for filing a report of bullying or a general grievance is strictly prohibited.

What is a TEA complaint vs. an OCR complaint?

A TEA Complaint focuses on IDEA and state special education rule violations, such as failure to follow IEP minutes or evaluation timelines. An OCR Complaint is filed with the U.S. Office for Civil Rights for discrimination, harassment, or retaliation based on disability under Section 504. In FBISD, Section 504 concerns are managed by Catharine Jacobson.

What happens if I disagree with an evaluation?

If you disagree with a district evaluation, you have the right to request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at public expense. Fort Bend ISD may either grant the IEE or file for a due process hearing to defend their own evaluation as appropriate. Contact the Special Education Department at (281) 634-1143 to start this process.

The FBISD Local Grievance Pathway

Fort Bend ISD utilizes a three-level administrative process for local complaints:

Level One: Principal Conference

File written notice within 15 district business days. The principal will hold a conference and issue a written response within 10 days of that meeting.

Level Two: Superintendent Appeal

If unsatisfied, appeal to the Superintendent's designee within 10 business days of receiving the Level One decision. A central administrator from the Department of Student Affairs will review the case.

Level Three: Board of Trustees

The final local step is an appeal to the School Board. You must file within 10 business days of the Level Two response.

Documentation for 2026 Disputes

Successful complaints require specific facts. TEA requires at least two facts to support each allegation. We recommend maintaining a "Parent Communication Log" including:

  • Recap emails for every phone call ("Just to confirm our conversation...").
  • Names and titles of all staff involved in disputed decisions.
  • Dates of ARD meetings and specific IEP sections being violated.
