About TexasSpecialEd.com

We're building the most comprehensive free resource for Texas families navigating special education — one district at a time.

Our Mission

TexasSpecialEd.com exists because no parent should have to figure out the special education process alone. Texas has over 1,200 school districts, each with their own leadership, timelines, and local policies. Finding accurate, district-specific information shouldn't require hours of searching through board documents and policy manuals.

We created this site to give every Texas family — regardless of income, education, or location — free access to the practical information they need to advocate for their child. From requesting a first evaluation to preparing for an ARD meeting to filing a grievance, we break down the process in plain language with tools you can actually use.

Every resource on this site is free. No subscriptions, no paywalls, no gated content. Just clear, actionable information designed for parents who need it most.

What We Cover

For each school district in our directory, we provide detailed guides on four core areas of the special education journey:

🔍

Evaluations & Child Find

How to request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE), understand the 45-school-day timeline, and know your options if the district says no.

🤝

ARD Meeting Process

What to expect at an ARD meeting, how to prepare, your right to disagree, and what happens when the committee can't reach consensus.

⚖️

Dispute Resolution

Local grievance procedures (FNG policy), TEA complaints, mediation, and due process hearings — with district-specific timelines and contacts.

👥

Leadership Directory

Names, titles, phone numbers, and email addresses for the special education administrators in your district who can actually help.

What Guides Our Work

Accuracy First

Every district page is researched against the district's official policies, board documents, and publicly available leadership directories. When information changes, we update it.

Plain Language

Special education is full of acronyms and legal jargon — FIIE, PLAAFP, LRE, FNG. We explain every term in parent-friendly language so you never feel lost.

Always Free

We will never charge families for the information on this site. Every checklist, template, guide, and district page is free to access and download.

District-Specific

Generic advice isn't good enough. Every district handles special education differently. Our guides are tailored to the specific policies, contacts, and processes of each district we cover.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who created TexasSpecialEd.com?

TexasSpecialEd.com was created to fill the information gap facing Texas special education families. We saw that parents were spending hours searching for basic information — who to call, what forms to use, what their rights are — and we built a single resource to answer those questions for every major Texas district.

Is this website a substitute for legal advice?

No. TexasSpecialEd.com provides educational information about the special education process in Texas. We are not attorneys, and nothing on this site constitutes legal advice. For specific legal questions about your child's rights, we recommend consulting a licensed special education attorney in your area.

How often is the information updated?

We review and update our district pages on a regular basis — particularly leadership directories, which change as districts hire new administrators. If you spot outdated information, please contact us so we can verify and correct it promptly.

Can I request a district to be added?

Yes. We prioritize new districts based on parent demand. If your district isn't covered yet, send us a message through our contact page and let us know which district you'd like to see added. The more requests a district receives, the higher it moves on our list.
