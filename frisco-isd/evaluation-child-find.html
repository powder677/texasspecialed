Quick Facts: Evaluations in Frisco ISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full (Individual) Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, teachers, or school staff can refer a child.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to completed evaluation.
  • Cost: Free to parents. District covers all testing.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting within 30 days of completed evaluation.

Request a Special Education Evaluation in Frisco ISD

Essential guidance for Frisco, McKinney, and Little Elm families. Learn how to start the evaluation process, understand timelines, and advocate for your child's needs in Frisco Independent School District.

Common Questions About Frisco ISD Evaluations

How do I request a special education evaluation in Frisco ISD?

Submit a written request to your child's Campus Principal or Counselor. If your child is not yet school-aged or is in private school, contact the Special Education Department at 469-633-6000.

Sample request language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], date of birth [DOB], currently enrolled at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Frisco ISD?

45 school days maximum. Once you sign the consent form, Frisco ISD has 45 school days (not calendar days) to complete all testing and provide you with a written report. Note that summer and winter breaks do not count toward this timeline.

What tests are included in a Frisco ISD evaluation?

Varies by suspected disability. Common assessments include cognitive/IQ testing (WISC, Woodcock-Johnson), academic achievement tests, speech/language evaluation, and psychological assessments conducted by an LSSP.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. You do not need a medical diagnosis (like ADHD or autism) to request a school evaluation. If you suspect your child has a disability that impacts their education, you have the right to request testing.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) in Frisco ISD?

Yes, if you disagree with the district's evaluation. An IEE is conducted by a professional not employed by Frisco ISD. Contact the Special Education Director to request district funding for an IEE if you believe the district's results are inaccurate.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

Initial ARD meeting within 30 calendar days. Frisco ISD will schedule a meeting to review results and determine if your child requires an Individualized Education Program (IEP). Learn about ARD meetings →

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

A FIIE is a comprehensive assessment to determine:

  • Does the child have a disability? One of the 13 categories recognized under IDEA.
  • Does the disability impact educational performance? The child must need specially designed instruction.
  • What services and supports are needed? Evaluation data informs the specific goals in the IEP.

13 Disability Categories in Texas Special Education

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment Specific Learning Disability Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment

In Frisco ISD, evaluations are conducted by licensed specialists including Educational Diagnosticians, Licensed Specialists in School Psychology (LSSP), and Speech-Language Pathologists (SLP).

How to Request an Evaluation in Frisco ISD

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Submit your request in writing to ensure the legal timeline is documented.

Where to Send Your Request:

Method: Email or Written Letter

Contact: Dr. Stephanie Cook, Managing Director of Special Education

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Within 15 school days, Frisco ISD will provide a Prior Written Notice. This will include either the consent forms to begin testing or an explanation for refusal.

Step 3: Evaluation Period (45 School Days)

Once you sign consent, Frisco ISD has 45 school days to conduct testing and complete a written report.

The Evaluation Timeline in Frisco ISD

Day 1: Parent Submits Written Request.
~15 School Days: District responds with Consent forms.
Clock Starts: Parent signs and returns Consent.
45 School Days: Testing period and Report completion.
30 Calendar Days: ARD meeting held to review eligibility.

Important: Frisco ISD must provide the report at least 5 school days before the ARD meeting so you have time to review the findings.

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Frisco ISD uses licensed specialists to conduct evaluations in several key areas:

Cognitive/Intellectual

Who: Diagnostician or LSSP

Measures: IQ, reasoning, and problem-solving abilities.

Academic Achievement

Who: Educational Diagnostician

Measures: Reading, math, and writing skills vs. grade-level expectations.

Speech & Language

Who: SLP

Measures: Articulation, receptive/expressive language, and social communication.

Behavioral/Emotional

Who: LSSP

Measures: ADHD symptoms, anxiety, and social-emotional functioning.

After the Evaluation: The ARD Meeting

  1. Receive Report: Obtain the FIE report 5 days before the meeting.
  2. Attend Initial ARD: Meet with the committee within 30 calendar days.
  3. Eligibility Determination: Decide if the child meets Texas criteria for special education.
  4. IEP Development: If eligible, create the education plan together.

✓ Eligible for Special Education

The child requires specially designed instruction via an IEP.

✓ Eligible for Section 504

The child requires accommodations but not specialized instruction.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with Frisco ISD's evaluation results, you have the right to request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at public expense. Contact the Special Education Department to file a formal disagreement and request an IEE.

Early Childhood Evaluations (Ages 3-5)

Frisco ISD identifies children with developmental delays before they enter kindergarten through the Child Find program. If your 3-year-old or 4-year-old shows delays in speech, motor skills, or social development, contact the district office at 469-633-6000 to request a preschool evaluation.
