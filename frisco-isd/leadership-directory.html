Frisco ISD Special Education Leadership & Contact Directory

Direct contact information for Frisco ISD special education administrators, coordinators, and support staff serving Frisco, Plano, Little Elm, and McKinney families.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Who Should I Contact?

For ARD Meeting Questions

Start with your child's Case Manager or Campus Diagnostician.

They handle classroom accommodations, IEP goals, and annual meeting scheduling.

For Evaluation Requests

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director.

Submit a written request for a special education evaluation to the central office.

For Complaints or Grievances

Start: Campus Principal.

Escalate to: Executive Director of Special Education if disagreements are not settled at the campus level.

For General Questions

Special Education Administration Office.

Phone: 469-633-6000.

Address: 5515 Ohio Drive, Frisco, TX 75035.

Executive Leadership

Frisco ISD (FISD) serves over 66,000 students and is one of the fastest-growing districts in DFW. The leadership team provides administrative oversight to ensure every student receives a Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE).

Key Department Coordinators

Frisco ISD uses a specialized structure to provide expertise across disability categories and ensure state mandates are followed.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Ensures dyslexia is treated as a specific learning disability within the ARD process per HB 3928.

Contact: Main Office

Autism Specialist

Provides expertise on communication, social skills, and behavior management for students on the spectrum.

Contact: Main Office

Evaluation Coordinator

Manages diagnosticians and specialists conducting Full and Individual Initial Evaluations (FIIE).

Contact: Main Office

Records Clerk

Responsible for the secure storage and transfer of IEP and ARD documentation.

Contact: Main Office

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Keep initial emails brief and request written responses to verbal conversations

📝 Documentation

  • Save all IEP and ARD documentation provided by the Records Clerk
  • Submit all evaluation requests in writing to the central office
