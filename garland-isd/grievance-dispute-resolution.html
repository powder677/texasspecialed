⚠️ Important Notice About Timelines

Special education complaints have strict legal deadlines. TEA state complaints must be filed within one year of the violation. Local grievances with Garland ISD (Policy FNG Local) must be filed within 15 district business days of the incident or decision. Failure to meet these timelines often results in the immediate dismissal of your complaint.

File a Special Education Complaint in Garland ISD

Understand your dispute resolution options in Garland ISD, which serves families across Garland, Rowlett, and Sachse. Learn the escalation process, how to navigate Board Policy FNG(LOCAL), and access resources for state-level TEA complaints or due process hearings.

Quick Answer: To file a special education complaint in Garland ISD, start with your child’s campus ARD chair or principal to attempt an informal resolution. If the issue remains unresolved, you must submit a formal written Level One complaint form to the campus principal within 15 district business days of the incident. Garland ISD, which serves a large student population in East Dallas, manages these disputes through its Student Services office, which tracks each grievance through a three-level administrative process ending with the Board of Trustees.

Quick Facts: Dispute Resolution in Garland ISD

  • Your Rights: You may disagree with any ARD decision without retaliation under federal law.
  • Initial Step: Attempt informal campus-level resolution with the principal.
  • Escalation: Formal Level One grievance filed via Student Services/Principal.
  • State Pathway: File a TEA complaint within 1 year of the incident.
  • Mediation: Voluntary neutral third-party negotiation (Free via TEA).

Common Questions About Garland ISD Dispute Resolution

How do I file a special education complaint in Garland ISD?

Start at the campus level. Contact your child's ARD committee chair or principal first to attempt informal resolution. If the issue is not resolved within 10-15 business days, escalate by submitting a formal Level One complaint form to the principal. Garland ISD follows a specific grievance process where Student Services tracks these complaints and assigns case reviews to ensure compliance with Board Policy FNG(LOCAL).

What is the difference between a district complaint and a TEA complaint?

District complaints are internal; TEA complaints are state investigations. A district complaint is filed with Garland ISD and follows the district’s three-level appeal process. A TEA (Texas Education Agency) complaint is a formal investigation into legal violations (like missed IEP minutes or evaluation timelines) and has a 60-day investigation window. Garland ISD parents are encouraged to attempt local resolution, but you may file with TEA at any time.

What happens if I miss the 15-day filing deadline in GISD?

The complaint may be dismissed. Garland ISD Policy FNG(LOCAL) is strictly enforced. If you miss the 15-district-business-day window, the district may decline to hear your grievance. However, if the issue involves a violation of federal law, you may still have the option to file a TEA complaint (1-year timeline) or a due process hearing.

Dispute Resolution Escalation Path in Garland ISD

Level 1

Campus-Level Resolution (Start Here)

Contact: ARD Chair, Special Ed Coordinator, or Principal.

Action: Request a staffing or a "reconvene ARD" to discuss concerns.

Tip: Garland ISD encourages parents to bring an advocate if they feel unheard.

Level 2

District-Level Grievance

Method: Submit written Level One form to the Principal.

GISD Student Services: 972-487-3300

Director: Dr. Tanya Vargas Ramos

Process: GISD assigns a central administrator if you appeal to Level Two.

State & Federal Options

TEA Complaint: Filed for violations of law within 1 year.

OCR Complaint: Filed for disability discrimination or retaliation within 180 days.

Mediation: Request via TEA Dispute Resolution portal.

Protection Against Retaliation

Garland ISD is legally prohibited from retaliating against parents or students who file complaints.

Your Rights:

  • ✓ Garland ISD cannot reduce services or change placement as punishment for a grievance.
  • ✓ Staff cannot treat your child differently because you advocated for their rights.
  • ✓ You have the right to document all communication via email to maintain a record.
