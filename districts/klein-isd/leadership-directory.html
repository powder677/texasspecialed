Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Include your student's full name, ID number, and grade level.
  • Request a "read receipt" or written confirmation for evaluation requests.
  • Maintain a "Communication Log" of every email sent to the district.

📞 Documentation Tips

  • After every phone call, send a "follow-up email" summarizing what was discussed.
  • Keep copies of all ARD documents, Progress Reports, and Evaluation summaries in one binder.
  • If you are requesting a change in services, ensure the request is in writing to trigger legal timelines.
