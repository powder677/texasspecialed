The district strictly adheres to Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL) for Child Find timelines and Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) for formal grievance procedures.

The Klein ISD "Special Programs" Model

Klein ISD integrates special education under the Special Programs umbrella, centralizing oversight for IEPs, Section 504, and Dyslexia services at the Teaching & Learning Center. Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), Klein ISD must complete a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) within 45 school days of receiving written parental consent. While general administration is housed on Louetta Rd, primary ARD Committee decisions are delegated to Evaluation & ARD Specialists stationed at specific campuses like Klein Collins High or Lemm Elementary.

A flagship component of the district's local authority is the Bridge Center, located at 7302 Kleingreen Ln. This facility serves adult learners (ages 18-22) and operates as a simulated community environment. For families with younger children, the Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) team manages initial "Child Find" screenings for children aged 3-5, independent of the local elementary school's general registration process.

Dispute Resolution: If a parent disagrees with an ARD Committee decision, Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) dictates the specific multi-level grievance process. Klein ISD requires parents to first attempt resolution with the Campus Principal and the assigned Program Coordinator (e.g., Coordinators for Autism or Behavior Support) before escalating to Dr. Kirsten Allman at the district level.
