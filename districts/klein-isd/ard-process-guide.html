Answer: In Klein ISD, an ARD meeting is the legally required Texas special education meeting where parents and district staff determine a student's eligibility, services, and placement. Under Texas law, parents in Klein ISD must receive at least five school days’ written notice, are mandatory equal members of the committee, and have the right to audio record all proceedings.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to Klein ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: ARD Meetings in Klein ISD

  • Notice Timeline: Minimum 5 school days' written notice required.
  • Recording Rights: Parents may audio record; the district typically records if the parent does.
  • Parent Role: Mandatory, equal member of the ARD committee.
  • Annual Frequency: Required at least once every 12 months.
  • Initial ARD Timing: Must occur within 30 calendar days of a completed Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).

How ARD Meetings in Klein ISD Differ From Other Districts

Klein ISD utilizes specific professional roles and meeting structures designed for compliance and efficiency:

  • Evaluation & ARD Specialists: Unlike many districts where teachers or diagnosticians solely manage paperwork, Klein ISD employs specialized "Evaluation & ARD Specialists" (Diagnosticians) who focus specifically on facilitating the meeting and ensuring legal documentation is precise.
  • Staffing Model: Klein ISD often utilizes a multidisciplinary team approach during meetings, where Licensed Specialists in School Psychology (LSSPs) and speech-language pathologists coordinate closely with the campus specialists to interpret evaluation data.
  • Virtual Accessibility: Klein ISD maintains robust virtual meeting options (typically via Zoom), allowing for flexible participation for working parents while maintaining the integrity of the committee.

How to Prepare for an ARD Meeting in Klein ISD

1. Review Current Progress Data

Access the Klein ISD Skyward parent portal to review current grades, attendance, and progress monitoring reports before the meeting.

2. Request a Draft IEP

Request a "Draft IEP" (including proposed goals and PLAAFPs) from your campus ARD Specialist at least one week before the meeting to avoid surprises during the committee session.

3. Document Parent Concerns

Draft a written "Parent Concerns" statement. Under Texas procedural safeguards, these must be formally recorded in the ARD meeting deliberations.

Common Klein ISD ARD Questions

Can I bring an advocate to a Klein ISD ARD?

Yes, parents have the legal right to bring advocates or experts to any meeting. Klein ISD requires parents to notify the school in advance if they are bringing an advocate or attorney so the district can ensure the proper staff are present.

What if I disagree with the ARD committee?

If consensus is not reached, you may request a "10-day recess" to gather more data or consider alternatives before reconvening. You also have the right to sign "disagree" on the final document.
