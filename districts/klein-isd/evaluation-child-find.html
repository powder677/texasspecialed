Quick Facts: Evaluations in Klein ISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full (Individual) Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, teachers, or school staff can refer a child.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to completed evaluation.
  • Cost: Free to parents; the district covers all assessment costs.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting held within 30 days of report completion.

Common Questions About Klein ISD Evaluations

How do I request a special education evaluation in Klein ISD?

Submit a written request to your campus principal or Dr. Kirsten Allman. Written requests are critical because they trigger the mandatory 15-school-day response period under Texas law.

Sample Language: "I am writing to formally request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], DOB [Date], at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Klein ISD?

45 school days for testing. Once the district receives your signed consent, specialists have 45 school days to complete testing and provide a written report. This is followed by a 30-calendar-day window to hold the ARD meeting.

What tests are included in a Klein ISD evaluation?

Assessments are tailored to suspected disabilities. This may include cognitive testing (IQ), academic achievement in reading and math, speech evaluations, and Functional Behavioral Assessments (FBA). Following Texas HB 3928, dyslexia testing must now be conducted under these special education protocols.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. Under Child Find, Klein ISD must evaluate based on educational need. A medical diagnosis (like ADHD or Autism) can support the request, but the school's assessment determines "educational eligibility".

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE)?

Yes, if you disagree with the district's results. An IEE is an evaluation by a qualified professional not employed by Klein ISD, paid for at public expense.

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

An FIIE is the comprehensive assessment process used in Texas to determine if a student qualifies for special education under federal law (IDEA). It determines:

  • Disability Identification: Does the student meet one of the 13 federal disability categories?
  • Educational Impact: Does the disability interfere with their performance in school?
  • Need for Services: Does the student require specialized instruction or accommodations to succeed?

13 Disability Categories in Texas Special Education

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment Specific Learning Disability Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment

Submit your request in writing to the campus principal to ensure legal timelines are triggered.

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Klein ISD has 15 school days to provide consent forms or a Prior Written Notice explaining why an evaluation is not warranted.

Step 3: Evaluation Period (45 School Days)

The 45-school-day timeline begins only after the district receives your signed consent.

The Evaluation Timeline in Klein ISD

Day 0: Parent submits written request.
Day 15: District provides consent form or refusal.
Clock Starts: Parent signs consent; 45-school-day window begins.
Day 45: Written FIIE report must be completed.
Within 30 Days: Initial ARD meeting must be held.

Note: Timelines are calculated in school days. Summer and district holidays pause the countdown.

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Specialists assess all areas of suspected disability to provide a complete picture of your child:

Psychological/Cognitive

Evaluates intelligence, IQ, and cognitive processing skills.

Academic Achievement

Assesses core grade-level skills in reading, writing, and math.

Social-Emotional

Reviews behavioral health, social skills, and emotional status.

After the Evaluation: The ARD Meeting

Once the evaluation is complete, Klein ISD will schedule an ARD meeting within 30 calendar days.

✓ Determining Eligibility

The ARD committee (including parents) reviews the FIIE report to decide if the student qualifies for special education. If eligible, an IEP is developed to outline specific goals and services.

Prepare for Your ARD Meeting

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with Klein ISD’s evaluation results, you have the right to request an IEE. This allows an outside professional to conduct a second opinion at the district’s expense.

Klein ISD Evaluation Specifics

Dyslexia Assessments (HB 3928)

Under Texas law, all dyslexia evaluations in Klein ISD are now conducted via the special education framework (IDEA). This ensures students with dyslexia receive comprehensive assessments and full legal protections.
