Quick Facts: Evaluations in Socorro ISD

  • Main Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to report completion.
  • Summer Rule: Consent signed 35-45 school days before year-end must be completed by June 30th.
  • Request Method: Written request to Special Education Director or campus administrator.
  • Cost: Free to parents; evaluation report provided at no cost.

Common Questions About Socorro ISD Evaluations

How do I request a special education evaluation in Socorro ISD?

You can make a request verbally or in writing to the director of special education or any administrative employee. However, a written request is highly recommended to document the 15-school-day response deadline.

How long does the evaluation process take in Socorro ISD?

Once you sign the consent form, the district has 45 school days to complete the initial evaluation and written report. This is followed by an ARD meeting within 30 days to review the results.

What if Socorro ISD refuses my request?

If the district decides not to evaluate, they must provide Prior Written Notice within 15 school days explaining why they believe an evaluation is not warranted, along with your Procedural Safeguards.

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

In Socorro ISD, the FIIE is the foundational assessment used by the ARD committee to determine eligibility for special education services.

Specialized Testing Categories

Evaluations include cognitive testing, academic achievement, and social-emotional assessments. Notably, Dyslexia screenings in Socorro ISD must follow the updated Texas HB 3928 requirements, which mandate these occur under IDEA special education protocols.

Socorro ISD Summer Timeline Rule

Texas law provides a specific protection for evaluations requested near the end of the school year:

If Socorro ISD receives consent at least 35 school days but less than 45 school days before the final instructional day, they MUST complete the report and provide it to you by June 30th.
