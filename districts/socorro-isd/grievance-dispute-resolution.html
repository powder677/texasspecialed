⚠️ Important Notice About Timelines

Special education complaints have strict deadlines. TEA complaints must be filed within one year of the alleged violation. Local district grievances in Socorro ISD (FNG Local) must typically be filed within 15 district business days of the incident.

Filing Complaints & Resolving Disputes in Socorro ISD

A complete guide to your options when you disagree with Socorro ISD (SISD) regarding your child's special education services, IEP implementation, or evaluation.

Quick Answer: To resolve a dispute with Socorro ISD, first attempt informal resolution with the campus principal or the Special Education Department at (915) 937-1800. If unresolved, you can file a formal Level One Grievance (FNG Local) at 12440 Rojas Dr. within 15 district business days. For legal violations, you may also file a TEA Complaint or request Special Education Mediation.

Common Reasons for Filing a Complaint

Failure to Provide Services

The IEP lists specific services (speech, OT, etc.) that Socorro ISD is not delivering as written.

Evaluation Delays

SISD has missed the 45-school-day timeline for completing the Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).

Placement Disagreements

You believe your child requires a more or less restrictive setting than what the district is offering.

Procedural Violations

The district failed to provide Prior Written Notice (PWN) or refused to hold an ARD meeting when requested.

Dispute Resolution Hierarchy

Level 1

District Grievance (FNG Local)

Focus: Local policy, staff conduct, or failure to follow the IEP.

Deadline: 15 district business days.

Process: Submit written Level 1 form to SISD Administrative Services.

Level 2

TEA Special Education Complaint

Focus: Violations of special education law (IDEA) or state code.

Deadline: 1 year from the violation.

Outcome: TEA issues a legally binding decision within 60 days.

Level 3

Mediation or Due Process

Mediation: Voluntary negotiation with a neutral TEA-provided mediator.

Due Process: A formal legal hearing similar to a trial regarding placement or FAPE.

Socorro ISD Local Filing Guide

Administrative Services Department

Address: 12440 Rojas Dr., El Paso, TX 79928

Phone: (915) 937-0000

Director of Special Education: Melissa Olivarez (moliva03@sisd.net)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Download the Level 1 Form: Access the official SISD Student/Parent Complaint Form from the district website.
  2. File Within 15 Days: Submit the form via hand-delivery (request a date-stamped receipt) or certified mail.
  3. Attend the Conference: A district administrator will schedule a meeting to hear your concerns.
  4. Receive Decision: You will get a written response within 10 business days of the meeting.

Documentation Checklist

Your ability to resolve a dispute depends on your evidence. Maintain a binder containing:

  • All current and past IEPs and FIEs.
  • Printouts of all email correspondence with teachers and administrators.
  • A phone log documenting the date, time, and summary of every call with SISD staff.
  • Work samples and progress reports showing where your child is struggling.
