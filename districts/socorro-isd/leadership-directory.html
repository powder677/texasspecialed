Who Should I Contact?

For ARD Meeting Questions

Start with your campus ARD committee chair or special education coordinator. If unresolved, contact the Program Coordinator for your child's age group.

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Include student's DOB and case manager name
  • Request written responses to verbal discussions

📞 Phone Contact

  • Call during hours: 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM
  • Leave detailed voicemails with a callback number
  • Follow up calls with a summary email
