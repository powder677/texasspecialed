Socorro ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Socorro ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators serving the El Paso metro area.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

Socorro ISD serves a large and diverse population of over 47,000 students in the El Paso area. The special education leadership team is responsible for implementing specialized instruction and ensuring federal and state compliance across all campuses, from Horizon City to East El Paso.

Key Coordinators

While the Executive Director oversees the entire department, most day-to-day program decisions and specialized support are managed by coordinators. Socorro ISD utilizes these specialists to provide deep expertise in specific disability areas and procedural requirements.

```

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Handles dyslexia evaluations and services under HB 3928, ensuring compliance with the updated Texas Dyslexia Handbook. They coordinate with campus literacy teams to provide evidence-based interventions.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Supports students with autism spectrum disorders, providing guidance on behavioral strategies and classroom environment. They assist ARD committees in developing Autism Supplements and specific behavioral intervention plans (BIP).

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages initial and reevaluation requests, overseeing the Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) timeline. This coordinator ensures that diagnosticians and LSSPs meet state-mandated deadlines for student testing.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

Handles IEP/ARD records requests and transfers for students moving into or out of Socorro ISD. Essential for ensuring your child’s services continue without interruption when moving across district lines.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the main special education office at 915-937-0000 for referral to the appropriate person.

```

Who to Contact When

In Texas, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process is the vehicle for all special education decisions. Use this guide to determine the best point of contact for your specific situation in Socorro ISD.

```

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

When you suspect your child has a disability and needs support, you should submit a written request for evaluation. If you haven't heard back from your local campus, the district coordinator is your next point of contact.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Your child's ARD case manager or campus special education coordinator

For student-specific questions regarding accommodations or progress, your child's campus is always the first stop. This includes requesting a "Parent-Initiated ARD" at any point during the school year.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

If you are moving or need records for a private doctor, contact the district's special education records office directly. You have a legal right to access these files under FERPA.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If issues cannot be resolved at the campus level with the Principal or Diagnostician, the Executive Director is the next level of oversight. They can help facilitate communication and compliance resolution.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

Under Texas HB 3928, dyslexia is now evaluated through special education. If your child was previously under Section 504 and you have questions about the transition to an IEP, reach out here.
```
