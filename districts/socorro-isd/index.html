Navigating Socorro ISD Specialized Services

Socorro Independent School District (SISD) manages special education services through a decentralized model that emphasizes campus-level accountability. Because SISD operates on a year-round academic calendar, the assessment of regression and recoupment for Extended School Year (ESY) services is unique to the district's intersession structure.

Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), SISD is mandated to complete the Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) within 45 school days of signed consent. The district provides specialized settings including FALS (Functional Academic Living Skills) for functional support and PAS (Positive Approach to Student Success) for behavioral intervention. Families transition from Early Childhood services to school-age programs via the assessment team at the District Service Center.

For secondary students, SISD coordinates post-secondary pathways through the Community Education Center. If a dispute arises during an Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) meeting, families should refer to Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) to initiate the grievance process, starting with the Campus Principal before escalating to Dr. Jan Halliday at the district level.
