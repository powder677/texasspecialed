Local Authority Guide: Navigating SISD Special Education

Socorro Independent School District operates a unique year-round academic calendar. Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must identify and evaluate students with disabilities, but families should be aware that SISD's intersession breaks affect how Extended School Year (ESY) services are calculated. Because student breaks are distributed throughout the year, the criteria for "regression and recoupment" are assessed relative to these specific intersession periods.

If you are requesting a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE), the legal 45-school-day timeline begins only after you provide written consent to the campus principal at your child's specific location, such as Socorro High School or Pebble Hills High School. For students with moderate to severe cognitive disabilities, SISD utilizes the FALS (Functional Academic Living Skills) program, while behavioral support is primarily delivered through PAS (Positive Approach to Student Success) units.

Bilingual & Transition Specialization

Due to the El Paso region's demographics, Socorro ISD maintains a robust team of bilingual diagnosticians. This is critical for parents to ensure that evaluations distinguish between a language difference and a true learning disability.

  • Evaluation Rights: Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), you have the right to an evaluation in your child's native language.
  • Secondary Transition: For students aged 14 and older, SISD provides vocational tracks through the Community Education Center, offering pathways in culinary arts and retail.
  • Dispute Resolution: Under Board Policy FNG(LOCAL), any grievances must be initiated at the campus level before escalating to Dr. Adam Starke at the Rojas Drive office.
