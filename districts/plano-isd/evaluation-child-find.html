Quick Facts: Evaluations in Plano ISD

  • Timeline: 15 school days for a response; 45 school days for testing; 30 calendar days for the ARD.
  • Who can request? Parents, doctors, or school staff can refer a child.
  • Cost: Free to parents; Plano ISD covers all assessment costs.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting to review results and determine eligibility.

How to Request a Special Education Evaluation in Plano ISD

Essential guidance for Plano families. Learn the legal timelines, parent rights, and how to start the evaluation process in Plano Independent School District.

Common Questions About Plano ISD Evaluations

```

How do I request a special education evaluation in Plano ISD?

Submit a written request to the school principal and special education office. Briefly describe your concerns regarding academic or behavioral progress.

Sample language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], DOB [Date]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Plano ISD?

The "45-School-Day" rule. Once the district receives your signed written consent, they have 45 school days to complete the testing. The ARD meeting must follow within 30 calendar days.

What happens if my child is absent during testing?

If your child is absent for three or more school days during the testing period, the district may extend the 45-school-day deadline by the number of days they were absent.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. Plano ISD has a Child Find duty to evaluate children suspected of having a disability based on educational need, not just a medical diagnosis.

Can I use an evaluation for the 2026 ESA program?

Yes. Beginning in 2026, parents may request a district evaluation (FIIE) to help calculate an Education Savings Account (ESA) award amount, even if not enrolled in the district.

```

Child Find & Your Rights

Under federal IDEA law, Plano ISD must identify, locate, and evaluate all children with disabilities (birth through age 21).

  • Written Consent: Formal evaluation cannot begin without informed parental consent.
  • Prior Written Notice: If the district refuses your request, they must provide a formal notice and Procedural Safeguards within 15 school days.
  • Report Access: You must receive the written evaluation report at least five school days before the initial ARD meeting.

How to Request an Evaluation in Plano ISD

```

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Send your request to both the campus principal and the Special Education department.

Plano ISD Contact Info:

Special Education Office: 2700 W. 15th Street, Plano, TX 75075

Child Find Help Line: 469-752-8100, Ext. 38050

Step 2: Receive Response (15 School Days)

Plano ISD must respond within 15 school days to provide a consent form or a formal refusal.

Step 3: Evaluation & ARD (45/30 Timeline)

The 45-school-day testing clock starts the school day after consent is received. The ARD meeting occurs within 30 calendar days of the report completion.

```

2026 Special Education Updates

Education Savings Accounts (ESA)

Effective for the 2026-2027 school year, students with disabilities approved for the ESA program may receive funds for private providers. Parents can use district FIIE results to help calculate these awards.

Dyslexia Allotment

As of September 1, 2026, districts like Plano ISD are no longer restricted to reserving only 20% of their dyslexia allotment for private provider contracts.
