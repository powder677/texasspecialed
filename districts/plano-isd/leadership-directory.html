Plano ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Plano ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators serving the DFW metro area.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

The Plano ISD Special Education department is a large administrative body tasked with overseeing the individualized education programs for nearly 50,000 students. At the helm, the Executive Director ensures that the district maintains compliance with both federal IDEA (Individuals with Disabilities Education Act) requirements and the specific Texas Education Code mandates.

Key Coordinators

While the Executive Director oversees the entire department, most day-to-day program decisions and specialized support are managed by coordinators. In a district as large as Plano ISD, these coordinators are often divided by "clusters" (feeder patterns) or by specific disability categories to provide more targeted support to campus teams and parents.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Handles dyslexia evaluations and services under HB 3928, ensuring compliance with the updated Texas Dyslexia Handbook. If your child is struggling with reading, this office manages the transition from 504 services to specialized instruction.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Supports students with autism spectrum disorders, providing guidance on behavioral strategies and classroom environment. They work closely with campus-level staff to implement structured teaching and social skills training.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages initial and reevaluation requests, overseeing the Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) timeline. This is the office that ensures your child's testing is completed within the legal 45-school-day window in Texas.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

Handles IEP/ARD records requests and transfers for students moving into or out of Plano ISD. Essential for parents who need a copy of the latest FIE or ARD document for private therapy or relocation.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the main special education office at 469-752-8100 for referral to the appropriate person based on your child's specific school campus or need.

Who to Contact When

In Texas, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process is the vehicle for all special education decisions. Knowing who to talk to can significantly reduce stress and speed up the resolution of concerns. Use this guide to determine the best point of contact for your specific situation in Plano ISD.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

When you suspect your child has a disability and needs support, you should submit a written request for evaluation. If the campus is not responding, escalating to the district evaluation coordinator is the next step.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Your child's ARD case manager or campus special education coordinator

For student-specific questions regarding accommodations or progress, your child's campus is always the first stop. If you need to schedule an "Emergency ARD," start with the campus diagnostician or teacher of record.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

If you are moving or need records for a private doctor, contact the district's special education records office directly to request your child's file. Under FERPA, the district has 45 days, but Texas law often encourages a faster turnaround.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If issues cannot be resolved at the campus level with the Principal or Diagnostician, the Executive Director is the next level of oversight. This is appropriate for systemic issues or failure to implement an IEP as written.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

Under Texas HB 3928, dyslexia is now evaluated through special education. If your child was previously under Section 504, contact the district about the transition to specialized instruction and the required inclusion of a dyslexia specialist in the ARD.
