Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Include your child's name, DOB, and campus in every email.
  • Submit written requests for initial evaluations to maintain a legal paper trail.

📞 Phone & Records

  • Call during business hours: Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM.
  • Log the date, time, and summary of verbal conversations.
  • Follow up phone calls with a written summary via email.

📝 Documentation

  • Save all IEP documents and Progress Reports in a secure folder.
  • Request IEP records directly from the Records Clerk when moving.
  • Districts have 45 days under FERPA to provide records, though Texas law encourages faster turnarounds.
