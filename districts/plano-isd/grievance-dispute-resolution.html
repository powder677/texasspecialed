Quick Answer: To file a special education complaint in Plano ISD, start with your campus ARD chair or principal. If unresolved, escalate to the district level by submitting a Level One Student/Parent Complaint Form (FNG Local) to the Plano ISD Administration Building. You can also file directly with the Texas Education Agency (TEA) or request mediation. Plano ISD, which serves the North Dallas metro area, requires district-level grievances to be filed within 15 district business days of the incident.

⚠️ Important Notice About Timelines

District grievances in Plano ISD (FNG Local) typically must be filed within 15 district business days of the time you knew or should have known of the issue. TEA complaints must be filed within one year of the alleged violation.

Common Questions About Plano ISD Dispute Resolution

How do I file a special education complaint in Plano ISD?

Start at campus level. Contact your child's ARD committee chair or campus principal first to attempt informal resolution. If the issue is not resolved, escalate by submitting a formal Level One Student/Parent Complaint Form (FNG Local) to the PISD Administration Building. Plano ISD follows a structured grievance officer system to document and respond to these complaints within specific board-mandated windows.

What is the difference between a district complaint and a TEA complaint?

District complaints are internal; TEA complaints are state investigations. A district complaint follows Plano ISD board policy FNG (Local) and is reviewed by district leadership. A TEA complaint triggers an external state-level investigation into legal or procedural violations, such as missed evaluation timelines. In Plano ISD, parents are encouraged to use the internal process first, but may file with TEA at any time.

How long does Plano ISD have to respond to a complaint?

Response times are determined by local policy. Once a formal Level 1 grievance is filed, a district administrator will schedule a conference. After the meeting, the administrator typically has 10 district business days to provide a written response. If you do not receive a response, you may appeal to Level 2 (Superintendent's designee) within 10 business days.
