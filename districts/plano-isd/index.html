Navigating Plano ISD Specialized Services

Plano Independent School District (PISD) is recognized for its robust specialized programming, often serving as a hub for the north DFW region. Leadership is headquartered at the Administration Center on W. 15th St., but programmatic hubs like the Beaty Early Childhood School are dedicated specifically to serving preschool-aged children (ages 3-5) with disabilities.

Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), Plano ISD is mandated to identify and evaluate students with suspected disabilities via the Child Find process. For older students, the district utilizes the Solomon Center for Adult Transition, a specialized campus for students ages 18-21 focused on vocational training and independent living. If your child is in a specialized placement, they may be assigned to RISE (Reaching Independence through Structured Education) or EAC (Essential Academics Class) environments.

Parents should note that the "Lift Off to Learning" Help Line (469-752-4299) is the direct authority for Child Find inquiries. If a disagreement arises during an Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) meeting, families should refer to Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) to initiate the grievance process, starting with the Campus Principal before escalating to Dr. Katrina Hasley.
