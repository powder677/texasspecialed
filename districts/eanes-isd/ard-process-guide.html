Answer: In Eanes ISD, an ARD meeting is the legally required Texas special education meeting where parents and district staff determine eligibility, services, and placement. Parents must receive at least five school days’ written notice, may audio record the meeting, and serve as mandatory equal members of the committee. This applies specifically to Eanes ISD.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to Eanes ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: ARD Meetings in Eanes ISD

  • Notice Timeline: Parents must be provided with written notice at least five school days before a scheduled meeting.
  • Recording Rights: You have a legal right to audio record any ARD meeting in Texas.
  • Parent Role: You are a mandatory equal member of the committee whose input is essential for decision-making.
  • Annual Requirement: The committee must meet at least once every 12 months to review and update the student's program.
  • Initial ARD Timing: An initial ARD meeting to determine eligibility must occur within 30 calendar days of a completed Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).

Common ARD Questions in Eanes ISD

Can I request an ARD meeting at any time in Eanes ISD?

Yes, you can request an ARD meeting at any time to address new concerns or progress issues. Under Texas law, the district should respond to your request to either schedule the meeting or explain why one is not needed.

Who serves as the District Representative in Eanes ISD meetings?

The District Representative is typically a campus administrator, such as a Principal or Assistant Principal. This person has the specific authority to commit Eanes ISD resources and funds to implement the IEP.

What happens if I disagree with the ARD committee's decision?

If a consensus cannot be reached, you have the right to request a "10-day recess" to gather more information and attempt to resolve the disagreement. You may also note your formal disagreement in the ARD minutes.

How to Prepare for an ARD Meeting in Eanes ISD

  1. Review Current Data: Read your child's most recent Full Individual Evaluation (FIE) and current IEP at least two weeks before the meeting.
  2. Request Draft Documents: Ask the Eanes ISD special education teacher for a draft IEP one week before the meeting to avoid surprises.
  3. Document Your Concerns: Write down your "Top 3" concerns or goals to ensure they are shared during the Parent Input section.
  4. Gather External Reports: If applicable, bring copies of private evaluations or medical updates from outside providers for the committee to consider.
  5. Confirm Your Team: Notify the school if you are bringing an advocate or attorney, as Eanes ISD typically requests 48-72 hours' notice for legal counsel.

How ARD Meetings in Eanes ISD May Differ From Other Districts

Eanes ISD is distinguished by its frequent use of **Facilitated ARD** options. For complex or contentious meetings, the district may provide a trained facilitator to help the committee maintain a productive, student-centered dialogue. Additionally, because Eanes ISD encourages student attendance for transition planning as early as age 14, the district places a high priority on fostering student self-advocacy within the meeting structure.
