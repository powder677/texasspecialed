Request a Special Education Evaluation in Eanes ISD

A current 2026 guide for Austin/Westlake families navigating the Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) process.

Child Find & Your Rights

Under federal IDEA law, Eanes ISD has a "Child Find" mandate to locate and evaluate all children with disabilities (birth through age 21). This applies regardless of whether the child is enrolled in EISD, a private school, or is homeschooled.

Key Parent Rights in Eanes ISD:

  • Right to Request: You can request an evaluation at any time in writing if you suspect a disability.
  • 15-Day Response: The district must respond to your written request within 15 school days with a proposal to evaluate or a refusal (Prior Written Notice).
  • Independent Evaluations: If you disagree with the district's FIIE, you may request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at public expense.
  • Native Language: Evaluations must be conducted in the child's native language or preferred mode of communication.

The Eanes ISD Evaluation Workflow

1

Submit a Written Request

In Texas, a written request is the only way to guarantee a 15-school-day response deadline. Address your request to the campus principal or the district's special education office.

2

Consent and the 45-Day Clock

If EISD agrees to evaluate, they will provide a "Notice of Proposal to Evaluate" and a "Consent for Evaluation" form.

The Timeline: Once Eanes ISD receives your signed consent, they have 45 school days to complete the evaluation report. If the child is absent 3+ days, this timeline may be extended.

3

Assessment Domains

The FIIE is comprehensive and assesses all areas of suspected disability. In Eanes ISD, this often includes:

  • Dyslexia: Under Texas HB 3928, dyslexia evaluations are part of the FIIE special education process, ensuring full IDEA safeguards.
  • Developmental Delay: For children ages 3-9, this category replaces NCEC as of the 2025-26 school year.
  • Psychological & Cognitive: IQ and processing testing performed by an LSSP.
4

Eligibility ARD Meeting

Within 30 calendar days of the report's completion, Eanes ISD must hold an Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) committee meeting to determine eligibility and develop an IEP if necessary.

2026 FAQ: Eanes ISD Evaluations

What is the "Developmental Delay" change for 2026?

As of the 2025-26 school year, Texas has phased out "Noncategorical Early Childhood" (NCEC). Children in Eanes ISD ages 3-9 are now evaluated under the broader Developmental Delay category, allowing for services without a specific disability label during early childhood.

Can I use an outside diagnosis for an Eanes evaluation?

Yes. You can provide reports from private doctors or psychologists. While the ARD committee must consider this data, the district is still required to conduct its own FIIE to determine educational eligibility in the school setting.
