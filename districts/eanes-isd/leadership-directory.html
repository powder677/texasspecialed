Campus Special Education Coordinators

For day-to-day questions about your child's services, contact your campus special education coordinator or ARD committee chair.

Note: Most ARD-related questions should start at the campus level. Contact information for campus coordinators is available through your school's main office or the district directory at Eanes ISD Website.

Eanes ISD operates a centralized service model with campus-based ARD teams. For a list of specific campus contacts, call the Special Education office at 512-732-9000.

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Include your child's name, DOB, and campus in every email
  • Keep initial emails brief and specific
  • Request written responses to verbal conversations

📞 Phone Contact

  • Call during business hours: Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM
  • Leave detailed voicemails with callback number and best times
  • Follow up phone calls with written summary via email

📝 Documentation

  • Save all email correspondence in a dedicated folder
  • Take notes during phone calls (date, time, person, summary)
  • Request confirmation of verbal agreements in writing
  • Use certified mail for formal evaluation requests if needed
