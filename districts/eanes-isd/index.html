Eanes ISD Special Education: Westlake Parent Guide

Navigating the ARD process and IEP implementation for the 850+ students receiving specialized instruction in Westlake.

Quick Answer: Special education services in Eanes ISD are centralized through the Special Education Department located at the Camp Craft Road Administration Building, led by Executive Director Molly May. The district operates under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), which mandates a 45-school-day timeline for Full Individual and Initial Evaluations (FIIE) following written parental consent.

Navigating the Eanes ISD Special Education System

For families at Westlake High School, Hill Country Middle School, or any of the six elementary campuses, the special education journey is governed by the Eanes ISD Special Education Operating Guidelines. Unlike larger Austin-area districts, Eanes maintains a highly localized approach where Campus Coordinators play a pivotal role in the Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) committee. Under Board Policy FB(LOCAL), the district ensures that students with disabilities have an equal opportunity to participate in all educational programs and activities.

The district provides a full continuum of services, including Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) at the Eanes Elementary and Valley View sites, and the Adult Transition Services (ATS) program for students aged 18-22. A unique feature of Eanes ISD is the Eanes Parent Enrichment Partnership (E-PEP), which offers specific training for parents to better navigate the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA) requirements within the Westlake community.

If you suspect your child has a disability, Child Find mandates under Board Policy EHBAA(LEGAL) require Eanes ISD to identify and evaluate students. Parents are encouraged to submit a formal written request for evaluation to the Campus Principal or the Special Education Department. Once consent is signed, the district has 45 school days to complete the evaluation and an additional 30 calendar days to hold the initial ARD meeting to determine eligibility for an Individualized Education Program (IEP).
