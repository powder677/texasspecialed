About Eanes ISD Special Education

For families at Westlake High School, Hill Country Middle School, or any of the six elementary campuses, the special education journey is governed by Eanes ISD Operating Guidelines. Eanes maintains a localized approach where Campus Coordinators play a pivotal role in ARD meetings.

Specialized Programs: Eanes ISD provides a full continuum of services, including Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) at Eanes Elementary and Valley View, and the Adult Transition Services (ATS) program for students aged 18-22. The Eanes Parent Enrichment Partnership (E-PEP) offers specific training for Westlake parents.

Child Find: Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must identify and evaluate students suspected of having a disability. Parents are encouraged to submit a formal written request to the Campus Principal or Molly May to initiate the 45-day evaluation timeline.

Understanding Eanes ISD Board Policies

When advocating for your child in Eanes ISD, it is critical to cite the specific Board Policy codes that govern special education services.

  • Evaluations (FIE): Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must provide a Full and Individual Evaluation within 45 school days of receiving your signed written consent.
  • Dispute Resolution: If you disagree with an ARD committee decision, Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) outlines the formal grievance process, starting with a Level One conference with your campus principal.
  • Nondiscrimination: Board Policy FB(LOCAL) ensures equal opportunity for students with disabilities in all educational programs.
