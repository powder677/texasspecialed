File a Special Education Complaint in Eanes ISD

Understand your dispute resolution options in Eanes ISD, serving approximately 7,500-8,500 students in West Austin. Learn the escalation process, timelines, and rights within the district's localized grievance structure.

Quick Answer: To file a special education complaint in Eanes ISD, start with your campus ARD chair or principal. If unresolved, escalate to the district level by submitting a written Level One Grievance under Board Policy FNG(LOCAL). Eanes ISD, located in the West Austin metro area, follows a three-level formal grievance process. You must file within 15 district business days of the incident. The district routes these complaints through the principal's office before potentially moving to the Superintendent’s designee and the Board of Trustees.

Quick Facts: Dispute Resolution in Eanes ISD

  • Your Rights: You can disagree with ARD decisions without retaliation.
  • Start Here: Informal campus resolution with the ARD chair or principal.
  • Escalate To: Formal Level One grievance filed with the Principal.
  • State Option: File with TEA within 1 year of the violation.
  • Legal Option: Due process hearing before an Independent Hearing Officer.

Common Questions About Eanes ISD Dispute Resolution

How do I file a special education complaint in Eanes ISD?

Start at the campus level. Contact your child's ARD committee chair or principal first. If unresolved within 10-15 business days, escalate by filing a written Level One grievance under Policy FNG(LOCAL). Eanes ISD requires these forms to be submitted to the principal or department head. The district will schedule a conference within 10 district business days to document and respond to your complaint.

What is the difference between a district complaint and a TEA complaint?

District complaints are internal; TEA complaints are state investigations. A district complaint follows Eanes ISD's internal FNG policy. A TEA complaint is a formal investigation into violations of IDEA or Texas Administrative Code. In Eanes ISD, parents are encouraged to attempt local resolution, but may file with TEA at any time.

How long does Eanes ISD have to respond to a complaint?

Strict timelines apply. Per Board Policy, a Level One conference is usually held within 10 district business days. A written response is then provided within another 10 district business days. If you do not receive a response, escalate to the Special Education Director or Superintendent's office.

What is mediation and how do I request it in Eanes ISD?

Mediation is voluntary and free. A neutral third party helps parents and the district reach a compromise. To request this in Eanes ISD, you can contact the TEA Dispute Resolution portal. Mediation is binding once a final agreement is signed by both parties.

Dispute Resolution Escalation Path in Eanes ISD

Follow this sequence to resolve special education disagreements in Eanes ISD:

Level 1

Campus Resolution (Start Here)

Contact: ARD Chair, Case Manager, or Principal.

Timeline: Informal; request a reconvene ARD within 10 days.

Action: Document concerns in the "Parent Concerns" section of the IEP.

Level 2

Formal District Grievance (Level One)

Eanes ISD Process:

Method: Submit a written complaint under Policy FNG(LOCAL).

Address: 601 Camp Craft Rd, Austin, TX 78746

Phone: 512-732-9000

Deadline: Must file within 15 district business days.

Level 3

TEA State Complaint

Requirement: Allege a specific violation of IDEA.

Investigation: TEA issues findings within 60 calendar days.

Outcome: If a violation is found, TEA orders corrective action or compensatory services.

Protection Against Retaliation

Eanes ISD families are protected by federal law when advocating for their children. The district cannot:

  • ✓ Reduce IEP services because you filed a grievance.
  • ✓ Exclude you from school activities or meetings.
  • ✓ Treat your child differently or negatively due to your advocacy.

If you believe retaliation is occurring, document incidents and file a complaint with the Office for Civil Rights (OCR) or TEA immediately.
