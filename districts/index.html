About Eanes ISD Special Education

Eanes ISD is widely recognized for its high academic standards and its robust Special Education Department. If you are a parent in the Westlake area, you likely know that Eanes ISD prides itself on providing a "continuum of services." This means the district offers various levels of support, from full inclusion in the general education classroom to specialized self-contained programs for students with more significant needs. The district emphasizes that special education is a service, not a place, ensuring that your child remains integrated with their peers to the maximum extent appropriate.

One of the unique features of Eanes ISD is its focus on collaborative planning. The district often utilizes "Parent Liaisons" or specialized coordinators to help bridge the gap between families and school staff. These professionals can be vital resources for you when navigating complex transitions, such as moving from elementary to Middle School or preparing for post-secondary life through the Eanes ISD 18+ transition programs. The district is also known for its strong emphasis on technology integration, utilizing various assistive technology tools to help students access the TEKS (Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills) curriculum effectively.

In recent years, Eanes ISD has made significant efforts to improve its "Child Find" and evaluation processes. The district is committed to identifying students with disabilities, including those with dyslexia or other specific learning disabilities, as early as possible. If you suspect your child has a disability that impacts their learning, the district is required to conduct a Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) at no cost to you. Recent updates in district policy have also focused on mental health support and behavioral intervention, reflecting a more holistic approach to student success.

As a parent in Eanes ISD, it is important to understand that while the district is high-performing, you are your child's best advocate. Familiarizing yourself with the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process is essential. In Texas, the ARD committee—which includes you—makes all the major decisions about your child’s educational program. Ensuring that your child’s IEP goals are specific, measurable, and aligned with state standards is the key to ensuring they make meaningful progress throughout the school year.
