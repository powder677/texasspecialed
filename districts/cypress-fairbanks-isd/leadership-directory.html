Cypress-Fairbanks ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Cypress-Fairbanks ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators in the Houston metro area.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

The Special Education Department at Cypress-Fairbanks ISD (Cy-Fair) is one of the largest in Texas, serving over 100,000 students across the northwest Houston region. Navigating a district of this size requires knowing exactly who oversees the high-level policy and compliance decisions that affect your child's ARD process.

Key Coordinators

While the Executive Director oversees the entire department, most day-to-day program decisions and specialized support are managed by coordinators. In a district as large as Cy-Fair, leadership is often divided by "clusters" or specific disability categories to ensure that every campus receives specialized oversight.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Handles dyslexia evaluations and services under HB 3928, ensuring compliance with the updated Texas Dyslexia Handbook. They manage the transition of students from Section 504 to Special Education services.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Supports students with autism spectrum disorders, providing guidance on behavioral strategies and classroom environment. They often work with the district's specialized programs to ensure evidence-based practices are implemented.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages initial and reevaluation requests, overseeing the Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) timeline for the thousands of evaluations Cy-Fair conducts annually.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

Handles IEP/ARD records requests and transfers for students moving into or out of Cypress-Fairbanks ISD. Essential for parents needing documentation for private providers or relocation.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the main special education office at 281-897-4000 for referral to the appropriate person based on your child's home campus.

Who to Contact When

In Texas, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process is the vehicle for all special education decisions. Use this guide to determine the best point of contact for your specific situation in Cypress-Fairbanks ISD.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

When you suspect your child has a disability and needs support, you should submit a written request for evaluation to your school principal and copy the district evaluation office.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Your child's ARD case manager or campus special education coordinator

For student-specific questions regarding accommodations or progress, your child's campus is always the first stop. Most Cy-Fair campuses have a dedicated Special Education Liaison.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

If you are moving or need records for a private doctor, contact the district's special education records office directly to request your child's file through the TREx system or via standard records request forms.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If issues cannot be resolved at the campus level with the Principal or Diagnostician, the Executive Director is the next level of oversight for formal dispute resolution.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

Under Texas HB 3928, dyslexia is now evaluated through special education. If your child was previously under Section 504, contact the district about the transition to specialized instruction and the required "qualified member" in your ARD.
