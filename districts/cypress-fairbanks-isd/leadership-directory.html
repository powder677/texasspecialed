Campus Special Education Contacts

Cypress-Fairbanks ISD operates a campus-based ARD model. Your first point of contact should always be the staff members physically located at your child's school.

Note: Every CFISD campus has a Special Education Liaison or Team Leader. For student-specific questions regarding IEP implementation or ARD scheduling, call your school's main office and ask to speak with the Special Education Department Chair.

If you are unable to reach your campus contact, call the central Special Education office at 281-897-4000 and provide your child's name and campus for a referral to the appropriate Regional Coordinator.

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Meeting Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Include your child's full name, date of birth, and ID number if known.
  • Request a "Read Receipt" for important time-sensitive requests like evaluations.
  • Follow up on any verbal agreements with an "as per our conversation" email.

📞 Phone & Records

  • Keep a log of everyone you speak with, including dates and times.
  • If you leave a voicemail, allow 24-48 business hours for a response before following up.
  • For records requests, ask for the Records Clerk at the Jones Road administration office.
