About Cypress-Fairbanks ISD Special Education

Cypress-Fairbanks ISD is one of the largest districts in Texas, serving over 17,000 students in special education. The department is managed by Director Karin Olsen (Instructional Services) and Director Jerretta Temple (Specialized Programs). The district coordinates services through the Mark Henry Administration Building, but emphasizes campus-based support.

CFISD employs numerous Board Certified Behavior Analysts (BCBAs) and behavior coaches who work across campuses to support students with significant behavioral needs. The district also focuses on Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) with specific intake procedures at the Matzke Road facility.

Child Find and Evaluations: Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district uses a tiered intervention model (RTI/MTSS) but must evaluate upon parent request if a disability is suspected. Contact the Special Education office at 281-897-6400 to initiate a referral.

Understanding CFISD Board Policies

When advocating for your child in Cypress-Fairbanks ISD, it is critical to cite the specific Board Policy codes that govern special education services.

  • Evaluations (FIE): Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must provide a Full and Individual Evaluation within 45 school days of receiving your signed written consent.
  • Dispute Resolution: If you disagree with an ARD committee decision, Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) outlines the formal grievance process, starting with a Level One conference with your campus principal.
  • Parent Participation: Board Policy EHBAB(LEGAL) guarantees your right to participate in ARD committee meetings and review all educational records.
