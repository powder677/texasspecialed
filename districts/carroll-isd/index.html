The Carroll ISD Special Education Framework

Carroll Independent School District serves over 8,400 students in Southlake. Navigating the high-performance "Dragon" culture requires a technical understanding of Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), which mandates that the district must identify, locate, and evaluate all children with disabilities within its jurisdiction. In Carroll ISD, this "Child Find" responsibility is centralized at the Administration Center, but initial implementation frequently begins with a request to your child's campus Diagnostician or Licensed Specialist in School Psychology (LSSP).

While Carroll ISD emphasizes a "co-teaching" model to facilitate inclusion within the general education classroom, parents should be aware of the specific "operating guidelines" used in Southlake. Under Board Policy EHBAB(LEGAL), the district must ensure parent participation in every ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) committee meeting. For students requiring more intensive support, specialized programs are integrated across campuses, including the Carroll Senior High transition services and the Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) programs.

When disagreements arise regarding an Individualized Education Program (IEP), Carroll ISD utilizes Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) to manage formal complaints. Before escalating to a Level 1 Grievance, families are encouraged to work directly with the Special Programs Coordinator assigned to their specific campus network to resolve placement or service disputes through data-driven progress monitoring.
