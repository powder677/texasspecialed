Quick Facts: Evaluations in Carroll ISD

  • What is it? FIIE = Full Individual Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, legal guardians, or school staff.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to completed report.
  • Cost: Free. Carroll ISD covers all testing costs.
  • Next Step: Initial ARD meeting within 30 calendar days of evaluation completion.

Essential guidance for Southlake families. Learn how to start the evaluation process, understand Texas timelines, and advocate for your child's needs in Carroll Independent School District.

Common Questions About Carroll ISD Evaluations

```

How do I request a special education evaluation in Carroll ISD?

Submit a written request to the campus principal or the Special Programs office. Using a formal letter ensures you trigger the legal 15-school-day response window. You can contact the Executive Director, Stacie Bonner, for assistance.

Sample language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], DOB [Date], currently enrolled at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Carroll ISD?

45 school days. Once Carroll ISD receives your signed written consent, they have 45 school days (not calendar days) to complete the evaluation report. If a student is absent 3+ days, the district may extend this deadline.

What tests are included in a Carroll ISD evaluation?

The FIIE is comprehensive. Assessments typically cover academic achievement, psychological/cognitive functioning, speech/language skills, and motor skills (OT/PT) if needed. Dyslexia is also evaluated under special education procedures (HB 3928).

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. Under the "Child Find" mandate, Carroll ISD must evaluate any child suspected of having a disability that impacts their education, regardless of a medical diagnosis.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE)?

Yes, if you disagree with the results. If you believe the district's evaluation was incomplete or inaccurate, you have the right to request an IEE at district expense.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

ARD meeting within 30 calendar days. The district will schedule a committee meeting to review results, determine eligibility, and develop an IEP if the child qualifies.

```

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

An FIIE is the comprehensive assessment process used by Carroll ISD to determine:

  • Presence of a Disability: Does the child meet criteria for one of the 13 disability categories?
  • Educational Performance: Does the disability impact their ability to learn?
  • Need for Services: Does the child require specialized instruction (Special Education)?
```

13 Disability Categories in Texas Special Education

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment Specific Learning Disability Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment
```

Step 2: District Response (15 School Days)

Carroll ISD must provide Prior Written Notice agreeing or refusing to evaluate, along with procedural safeguards.

Step 3: Signed Consent

The 45-school-day evaluation clock begins only after the district receives your signed written consent.

What Tests Are Included?

The multidisciplinary team assesses areas of suspected disability, which may include:

Academic

Reading, Math, and Writing achievement.

Psychological

Cognitive and emotional functioning.

Speech

Language and communication skills.

Dyslexia

Evaluated as a specific learning disability under IDEA.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with a Carroll ISD evaluation, you can request an IEE at district expense. The district must then either agree to fund the independent testing or file for a due process hearing to defend its evaluation.

Dispute Resolution Info

Child Find & Private Schools

Carroll ISD is responsible for Child Find for all children within Southlake boundaries, including those in private schools or homeschooled. Contact the Special Programs department at 817-949-7052 for assistance.
