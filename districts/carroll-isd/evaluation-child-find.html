How to Request a Special Education Evaluation in Carroll ISD

A comprehensive guide to starting the special education evaluation process in Carroll ISD, Southlake, Texas.

Child Find & Your Rights

Under federal IDEA law and Texas Education Code, Carroll ISD has a "Child Find" duty to identify, locate, and evaluate all children with disabilities (birth through age 21) who may need special education services. This applies to all children within Southlake boundaries, including those in private schools or homeschools.

You Have the Right To:

  • Request a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) at any time in writing.
  • Receive a response (Prior Written Notice) within 15 school days of your request.
  • Have your child evaluated in all areas of suspected disability, including academic, behavioral, and motor skills.
  • Review evaluation results with a professional who can interpret the findings.
  • Request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at district expense if you disagree with the district's results.

New to Special Education?

You do not need a medical diagnosis (like ADHD or Dyslexia) to request an evaluation. Under Child Find, Carroll ISD must evaluate any child suspected of having a disability that impacts their education, even if they are advancing from grade to grade.

Step-by-Step Evaluation Process in Carroll ISD

1

Submit a Written Request

Send a written evaluation request to Carroll ISD. Written requests trigger legal timelines that verbal requests do not always ensure.

2

District Reviews Your Request

Carroll ISD has 15 school days to respond to your written request. They will provide you with:

  • Prior Written Notice: A formal letter agreeing or refusing to evaluate.
  • Procedural Safeguards: A guide explaining your legal rights under IDEA.
  • Consent for Evaluation: If they agree to evaluate, they must provide an opportunity for you to sign consent.
3

Sign Consent for Evaluation

The evaluation clock does not start until the district receives your signed consent in writing.

Note: In Texas, the district has 45 school days to complete the evaluation after receiving your signature.

Tip: If consent is signed late in the school year (between 35-45 school days before the last day), the district must provide the report by June 30th.
4

Evaluation is Conducted

Carroll ISD’s multidisciplinary team will assess your child. Assessments may include:

  • Academic Achievement (Reading, Math, Writing)
  • Psychological or Cognitive functioning
  • Speech and Language skills
  • Occupational or Physical Therapy needs
  • Dyslexia Evaluation: Under HB 3928, dyslexia must be evaluated as a suspected disability under IDEA (Special Education).
5

Initial ARD Meeting is Scheduled

Within 30 calendar days of the completed evaluation report, the district must hold an Initial ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) meeting.

At this meeting, the committee (including you) will determine if the child is eligible for services based on two prongs: having a disability and a resulting need for specialized instruction.

Common Questions About Evaluations in Carroll ISD

What if my child is in a private school?

Carroll ISD is responsible for "Child Find" for children attending private or parochial schools within Southlake boundaries. You should contact the Special Programs department directly.

Can I request my child's records before the meeting?

Yes. Parents have a right to review educational records. Requests for Special Education records can be made to the Special Programs Department.

Does dyslexia testing fall under 504 or Special Education?

Per Texas HB 3928, dyslexia is defined as a specific learning disability under IDEA. Evaluations for dyslexia must now follow special education (FIIE) procedures to ensure full legal protections.
