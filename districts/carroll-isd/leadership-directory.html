Carroll ISD Special Education Leadership & Contact Directory

Direct contacts for special education services, ARD meetings, evaluations, and support in Carroll ISD, serving the Southlake community.

Who Should I Contact?

For ARD Meeting Questions

Start with your child's ARD case manager or campus diagnostician.

If unresolved: Contact the specific Program Coordinator for your child's campus or grade level.

For Evaluation Requests

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator

Key Coordinators

Coordinators in Carroll ISD provide specialized oversight for specific programs and disability categories.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Focus: Ensuring identification and support align with HB 3928 and the updated Texas Dyslexia Handbook.

Autism Specialist

Focus: Support for sensory needs, behavioral strategies, and social-emotional learning goals.

Evaluation Coordinator

Focus: Oversight of Full and Individual Initial Evaluations (FIIE) to ensure compliance with the 45-school-day timeline.

Records Clerk

Focus: Management of confidential IEP and evaluation records for student transfers and medical requests.

Effective Communication in Carroll ISD

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Clearly state the student's name, campus, and the specific request (e.g., "Request for ARD Meeting").
  • Maintain a professional tone and keep requests focused on educational needs.
  • Keep copies of all sent and received emails in a dedicated folder.

📞 Communication Protocol

  • Follow the "Chain of Command": start with the teacher/case manager before escalating to administration.
  • Always submit formal requests, such as for evaluations or records, in writing.
  • Follow up phone calls with a brief email summary for documentation.
