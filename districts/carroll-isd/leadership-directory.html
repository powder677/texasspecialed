Carroll ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Carroll ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators in Southlake.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

In Carroll ISD, the Special Education Department is focused on the "Dragon Way," ensuring that students with diverse learning needs receive top-tier support. The leadership team manages a comprehensive array of services, from early childhood intervention to post-secondary transition programs, ensuring compliance with state and federal mandates while maintaining the district's high standards for excellence.

Key Coordinators

Navigating special education in a high-performing district like Carroll ISD requires understanding the roles of key coordinators. These professionals oversee specific disability categories and compliance requirements, providing a bridge between the central administration and your child's ARD committee. If you have questions about specialized programming, these are the staff members who guide campus-level implementation.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Oversees the identification and support for students with dyslexia. Under the updated Texas Dyslexia Handbook and HB 3928, this coordinator ensures that dyslexia instruction is provided by qualified providers within the special education framework.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Provides support and specialized strategies for students on the autism spectrum. They assist teachers and parents in developing effective behavioral intervention plans (BIPs) and social-emotional learning goals.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages the diagnostic process for students referred for special education. They track the Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) timelines to ensure that Southlake families receive results within the legal 45-school-day window.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

The primary point of contact for the transfer of IEP and evaluation records. Whether you are moving into Southlake or need records for a private specialist, the Records Clerk manages these confidential data requests.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the main CISD Administration office at 817-949-8200. The district often assigns coordinators based on grade levels (Elementary vs. Secondary).

Who to Contact When

Effective communication is key to a successful ARD process. In Carroll ISD, parents are encouraged to follow the "Chain of Command" to resolve issues efficiently. While the campus is usually the first stop, the central office is available for broader district-wide concerns.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you believe your child needs special education services, you must provide a written request for an evaluation. In Texas, "Child Find" requirements obligate the district to identify and evaluate students suspected of having a disability.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Campus Diagnostician or Your Child's Case Manager

For questions about specific accommodations, modifications, or goals in your child's IEP, contact the staff at your child's school. They facilitate the daily implementation of the ARD committee's decisions.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Special Education Records Clerk

If you are applying for SSI, moving, or visiting a neurologist, you will need a certified copy of your child's special education file. Contact the central office to initiate a records release.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director of Special Education

If you cannot reach an agreement with your campus ARD committee, the Executive Director can help mediate or explain the district's formal grievance and due process procedures.
