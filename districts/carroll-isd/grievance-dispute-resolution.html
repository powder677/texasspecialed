File a Special Education Complaint in Carroll ISD

Carroll ISD (CISD) serves approximately 8,500 students in Southlake and Westlake. For families in the Special Programs department, understanding the formal Board Policy FNG grievance structure is critical to resolving disagreements about IEP implementation, evaluations, or placement.

Quick Answer: To file a special education complaint in Carroll ISD, families should first attempt an informal resolution with the campus principal. If unresolved, you must submit a formal **Level I Grievance Form** to the Office of Student Services or the Special Programs Department. Complaints can be emailed to **Student.Services@southlakecarroll.edu**. Carroll ISD utilizes a structured multi-level appeal process: Level I (Campus/Student Services), Level II (Assistant Superintendent), and Level III (Board of Trustees).

Quick Facts: Dispute Resolution in Carroll ISD

  • Local Policy: Follows FNG(LOCAL) for student/parent grievances.
  • Filing Deadline: Level I must be filed within 60 business days of the incident.
  • Hearing Timeline: A conference is typically set within 10 business days of receipt.
  • Special Ed Lead: Patti Parsons, Executive Director for Special Programs.
  • State Escalation: TEA complaints must be filed within 1 year of the alleged violation.

Common Questions About Carroll ISD Dispute Resolution

How do I file a special education complaint in Carroll ISD?

The district requires a step-by-step approach. **Step 1** is a direct conference with the staff member or principal. If this fails, you may initiate the formal process by emailing the **Level I Grievance Form** to **Student.Services@southlakecarroll.edu**. The district assigns these cases to the Student Services or Special Programs leadership for review.

What is the response time for a district grievance?

Once you file a Level I grievance, Carroll ISD will typically hold a conference within **10 business days**. A written decision is then provided within **20 business days** after the hearing. If you disagree with the decision, you have 20 business days to appeal to Level II.

Do I need an attorney for an ARD dispute in Southlake?

No, you are not required to have an attorney for informal meetings or district grievances. However, if you are filing for a **Due Process Hearing**, which is a formal legal proceeding before an administrative law judge, legal representation is strongly recommended.

Dispute Resolution Escalation Path in Carroll ISD

Level 1

Campus & Student Services

Contact: Campus Principal or Student Services Office.

Action: Submit Level I form within 60 business days.

Level 2

Assistant Superintendent

Contact: Brian Nickel, Assistant Superintendent for Administration.

Action: Appeal Level I decision within 20 business days.

Level 3

Board of Trustees

Action: Final local appeal to the elected Carroll ISD Board.
