Answer: In **Carroll ISD**, an ARD meeting is the legally required Texas special education meeting where parents and district staff determine eligibility, services, and placement. Parents must receive at least **five school days’ written notice**, have the legal right to **audio record** meetings, and are recognized as **equal members** of the committee.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to Carroll ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: ARD Meetings in Carroll ISD

  • Notice Timeline: You must be notified in writing at least 5 school days before the meeting.
  • Recording Rights: You can audio record the meeting; Texas allows this as long as you inform the team.
  • Parent Role: You are a mandated, equal member of the committee with a voice in all decisions.
  • Annual Requirement: The committee must meet at least once per year to update your child's educational program.
  • Right to Support: You may bring an advocate, attorney, or specialist to any Southlake ARD meeting.

Common ARD Questions

How do I request an ARD meeting in Carroll ISD?

Parents can request an ARD at any time to address academic or behavioral concerns. Under Texas law, the district must respond to your request to meet regarding your child's Individualized Education Program (IEP).

What happens if I disagree with the committee?

You have the right to disagree with any part of the IEP and request a "10-day recess". This allows the committee to meet again within 10 school days to try and reach a compromise.

Who from Carroll ISD must attend the meeting?

The committee must include a district representative (administrator), a special education teacher, a general education teacher, and someone who can interpret evaluation data (Diagnostician or LSSP).

How to Prepare for an ARD Meeting

Preparation is the best way to reduce stress and advocate effectively for your child in Southlake schools.

  1. Review Records: Thoroughly review the current IEP, progress reports, and the most recent Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).
  2. Request Draft Goals: Ask the teacher to send home draft IEP goals at least one week before the meeting starts.
  3. Identify Priorities: Create a list of your top 3 concerns or priorities for the upcoming school year.
  4. Gather Outside Data: Collect independent evaluations or letters from private therapists to share with the committee.
  5. Plan for Support: Confirm with any advocates or support persons you have invited to attend with you.

How ARD Meetings in Carroll ISD May Differ From Other Districts

In Carroll ISD, ARD meetings are typically held on the child's home campus and are highly data-driven. Given Southlake's high academic standards, the committee often focuses specifically on how special education services will support the student in meeting the district's rigorous curriculum goals. If you bring an attorney, Carroll ISD typically requests advance notice to ensure their own legal representation is present.
