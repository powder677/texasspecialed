Quick Facts: ARD Meetings in Conroe ISD

  • ARD = Admission, Review, and Dismissal Committee (Texas term for IEP team).
  • Conroe ISD must hold an ARD meeting at least once per year.
  • Parents are equal partners and decision-makers on the committee.
  • You have the right to record the meeting and bring an advocate.
  • Initial ARD meetings occur within 30 days of a completed evaluation.

ARD Meeting Guide for Conroe ISD Parents

Step-by-step guidance for Conroe ISD families navigating the ARD committee process, from preparation to post-meeting follow-up.

What is an ARD Committee? (Texas vs. Other States)

In most of the U.S., a student's special education plan is developed by an "IEP Team." In Texas, we use the term ARD Committee.

Other States:

IEP Team

IEP Meeting

Texas:

ARD Committee

ARD Meeting

ARD stands for:

  • Admission: Admitting a student into special education services.
  • Review: Reviewing the student's progress and the IEP's effectiveness annually.
  • Dismissal: Ending special education services when eligibility requirements are no longer met.

While the terminology is different, the result is the same: the creation of an Individualized Education Program (IEP). In Conroe ISD, this committee is the legal engine behind your child's educational support.

Who Attends ARD Meetings in Conroe ISD?

Required Participants:

Parent(s) or Guardian(s)

You are essential. You provide critical insight into your child's strengths, history, and needs outside of school.

Special Education Teacher

Shares data on progress toward goals and suggests specific instructional modifications.

General Education Teacher

Mandatory if your child is, or may be, participating in the general education environment.

District Representative

An administrator (typically a principal or assistant principal) who can authorize district resources.

Evaluator

A Diagnostician or LSSP who interprets evaluation data and testing results.

The Student

Required when transition services are discussed (age 14+) and encouraged whenever appropriate.

You May Also Invite:

  • Special Education Advocate: To help you navigate legal requirements and stay focused on student needs.
  • Attorney: You have a right to legal counsel. (Note: Notify Conroe ISD in advance if you plan to bring an attorney).
  • Private Specialists: Outside therapists or doctors who have relevant information about your child.
  • Translators: Conroe ISD must provide an interpreter if you are not fluent in English.

How Conroe ISD Conducts ARD Meetings

Conroe ISD uses ARD Facilitators at secondary campuses and Campus Compliance Coordinators at many elementary schools to ensure meetings are productive and compliant. Meetings may be held in person or virtually via platforms like Zoom or Microsoft Teams to accommodate parent schedules.

How to Prepare for Your ARD Meeting in Conroe ISD

2 Weeks Before: Gather Intelligence

  • Review the current IEP and the last three progress reports.
  • Gather recent work samples, report cards, and any private evaluations.
  • Review the most recent Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).
  • Ask your child what is working well and what is difficult for them.

1 Week Before: Document Your Goals

  • Draft a "Parent Concerns" statement to be included in the meeting minutes.
  • List 3-5 specific goals or changes you want to discuss.
  • Request a draft of the proposed IEP from your child's case manager.
  • Confirm the attendance of your advocate or support person.

During the ARD Meeting: What to Expect

Typical Meeting Flow in Conroe ISD:

  1. Introductions: Everyone identifies their role.
  2. Review Data: Discussion of Present Levels of Academic Achievement and Functional Performance (PLAAFP).
  3. Parent Input: Your opportunity to voice concerns and goals.
  4. Goal Development: Reviewing progress and setting new measurable goals.
  5. Services & Accommodations: Determining the "what" and "where" of your child's support.
  6. Assurances & Minutes: Reviewing the "deliberations" (meeting notes) for accuracy.
  7. Signature: Signing the document to indicate agreement or disagreement.

Your Rights During the ARD Meeting:

  • ✓ You can ask for breaks to process information.
  • ✓ You can request a "10-day recess" if the committee reaches a stalemate.
  • ✓ You can audio record the meeting (Texas allows this without school consent).
  • ✓ You can request the meeting be rescheduled if you need more time to review a draft.
  • ✓ You can disagree with any specific part of the IEP.

After the ARD Meeting: Next Steps

Full Agreement

If you sign in agreement, services typically begin within 5-10 school days.

Partial Agreement

You can agree to some parts (like speech therapy) but disagree with others (like placement). The agreed-upon parts can start while you resolve the rest.

Disagreement Options

If you disagree, you have the right to a recess or can pursue mediation or a due process hearing.

Frequency of Meetings

Conroe ISD must meet:

  • Annually: To update the IEP.
  • Every 3 Years: For a full reevaluation (REED).
  • As Needed: You can request an ARD at any time to address new concerns.
