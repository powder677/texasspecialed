Answer: In **Conroe ISD**, an ARD meeting is the legally required Texas special education meeting where parents and district staff determine eligibility, services, and placement. Parents must receive at least **five school days’ written notice**, have the right to **audio record** the meeting, and are recognized as **equal members** of the committee under Texas law.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to Conroe ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: ARD Meetings in Conroe ISD

  • Notice Timeline: Conroe ISD must provide written notice at least 5 school days before the meeting.
  • Recording Rights: You have the right to audio record the meeting; Texas allows this without school consent.
  • Parent Role: You are an equal partner and decision-maker on the ARD committee.
  • Annual Requirement: The district must hold an ARD meeting at least once per year.
  • Initial ARD: Meetings typically occur within 30 days of a completed evaluation.

Common ARD Questions

How do I request an ARD meeting in Conroe ISD?

Parents can request an ARD at any time to address new concerns or review progress. The district must respond to your request to meet regarding your child's Individualized Education Program (IEP).

What happens if I disagree with the committee?

You can request a "10-day recess" if the committee reaches a stalemate. You also have the right to disagree with any part of the IEP and pursue mediation or due process.

Who leads the meetings in Conroe ISD?

Conroe ISD uses ARD Facilitators at secondary campuses and Campus Compliance Coordinators at many elementary schools to guide the process.

How to Prepare for an ARD Meeting

  1. Gather Intelligence: Review the current IEP, the last three progress reports, and the most recent Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).
  2. Document Your Goals: Draft a "Parent Concerns" statement and list 3-5 specific changes you want to discuss.
  3. Request Drafts: Ask the case manager for a draft of the proposed IEP goals at least one week before the meeting.
  4. Verify Attendance: Confirm if you are bringing an advocate and notify the school in advance if bringing an attorney.
  5. Prepare for the Flow: Be ready to discuss the PLAAFP (Present Levels) before moving to goals and services.

How ARD Meetings in Conroe ISD May Differ From Other Districts

Conroe ISD is one of Texas's fastest-growing districts and emphasizes the use of ARD Facilitators to maintain consistency across its many campuses. The district frequently offers virtual meeting options via Zoom or Microsoft Teams to accommodate busy parent schedules. Additionally, Conroe ISD begins formal transition discussions for students at age 14, two years earlier than the federal requirement.
