Quick Facts: Evaluations in Conroe ISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full Individual Initial Evaluation to determine special education eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, legal guardians, or school staff can initiate a referral.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from your signed consent to the finished report.
  • Cost: Evaluations are provided at no cost to families by Conroe ISD.
  • Next Step: An ARD meeting is held within 30 calendar days of the report's completion.

Request a Special Education Evaluation in Conroe ISD

Essential guidance for Conroe and The Woodlands families. Learn how to start the evaluation process, understand timelines, and advocate for your child's needs in Conroe Independent School District.

Common Questions About Conroe ISD Evaluations

How do I request a special education evaluation in Conroe ISD?

Submit a written request to your campus principal. If your child is currently enrolled in a Conroe ISD school, address a letter or email to the principal requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE). If your child is pre-school age, contact the Special Education office directly.

Sample request language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], date of birth [DOB], currently enrolled at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Conroe ISD?

45 school days maximum. Once you sign the official consent form, Conroe ISD has 45 school days (excluding holidays and weekends) to complete all testing. In many cases, CISD specialists aim to complete evaluations within 30-40 school days during standard operations.

What tests are included in a Conroe ISD evaluation?

Assessments are tailored to your child's needs. Common tests include the WISC-V or Woodcock-Johnson for cognitive abilities, achievement tests for academics, and specific assessments for speech, motor skills, or behavior if those are areas of concern.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No medical diagnosis is required. Conroe ISD has a legal duty called "Child Find" to evaluate any child suspected of having a disability that impacts their education, regardless of whether they have a doctor's diagnosis.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) in Conroe ISD?

Yes, if you disagree with the district's findings. If you believe the Conroe ISD evaluation was incomplete or incorrect, you can request an IEE at public expense. You should submit this request in writing to the Executive Director of Special Education.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

An ARD meeting is held within 30 calendar days. The committee (including you) will meet to review the FIE report. You must be provided with a copy of this report at least 5 school days before the meeting occurs.

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

A FIIE (often called an FIE) is a comprehensive assessment conducted by Conroe ISD to answer three critical questions:

  • Does the child have a disability? Determining if the child meets one of the 13 federal eligibility categories.
  • Is there an educational need? Determining if the disability causes the child to need specially designed instruction.
  • What supports are necessary? Identifying the specific goals and accommodations required for the child to succeed.

13 Disability Categories in Texas Special Education

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment Specific Learning Disability Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment

In Conroe ISD, these evaluations are performed by a multidisciplinary team, which may include Educational Diagnosticians, Licensed Specialists in School Psychology (LSSP), and Speech-Language Pathologists.

How to Request an Evaluation in Conroe ISD

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Send your request to the campus principal for school-aged children. For children not yet in school, contact the district special education office.

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Within 15 school days, Conroe ISD will either provide you with "Prior Written Notice" refusing the request or provide you with "Consent for Evaluation" forms. The legal timeline does not start until you sign and return these forms.

Step 3: The 45-Day Evaluation Period

Once consent is received, Conroe ISD specialists will observe your child, review records, and conduct standardized testing. They have 45 school days to complete this phase.

Step 4: The ARD Meeting

Within 30 calendar days of the report being finished, you will meet with the ARD committee to discuss eligibility and create an Individualized Education Program (IEP) if your child qualifies.

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Conroe ISD utilizes various standardized tools and observations based on the student's needs:

Cognitive/IQ

Who: LSSP or Diagnostician

Measures: Verbal reasoning, memory, and processing speed.

Academic Achievement

Who: Educational Diagnostician

Measures: Reading, writing, and math skills against grade-level peers.

Speech & Language

Who: Speech-Language Pathologist

Measures: Articulation, language comprehension, and social communication.

Occupational Therapy

Who: Occupational Therapist

Measures: Fine motor skills, handwriting, and sensory processing.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with the evaluation performed by Conroe ISD, you have the legal right to ask for an IEE at district expense. This is an evaluation conducted by a professional who does not work for the district.

How to Request an IEE in Conroe ISD

Submit a written request to the Special Education Director stating that you disagree with the district's evaluation and are requesting an IEE. You do not have to explain exactly why you disagree, though it can be helpful.

Early Childhood Evaluations (Ages 3-5)

Conroe ISD provides Child Find services for preschool-aged children who may have developmental delays. This program is often referred to as Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE).

Contact: Conroe ISD Child Find Office

Phone: 936-709-7813

If your child is found eligible, they may receive services such as speech therapy or placement in a specialized preschool classroom at no cost to you.
