Key Coordinators & Specialists

In Conroe ISD, specialized coordinators manage the nuanced requirements of specific disability categories and legal mandates.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Focus: Compliance with HB 3928, ensuring students with dyslexia receive "specially designed instruction" within the special education framework.

Autism Specialist

Focus: Assists with behavioral strategies and the implementation of the "Autism Supplement" required during ARD committee meetings.

Evaluation Coordinator

Focus: Supervises diagnosticians and LSSPs to ensure the 45-school-day timeline for Full and Individual Initial Evaluations (FIIE) is strictly maintained.

Records Clerk

Focus: Processing requests for ARD/IEP documentation for families relocating or seeking independent evaluations.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not provided by campus staff, call 936-709-7751 for a direct referral.

Tips for Effective Communication

Understanding the hierarchy of communication in Conroe ISD helps ensure your concerns are heard by the right person within the ARD committee process.

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use clear subject lines: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]".
  • Include your child's name and ID number (if known) in every email.
  • Request written confirmation for any verbal agreements made during phone calls.

📞 Communication Flow

  • Always start at the campus level with the teacher or case manager.
  • Maintain a log of all contacts, including dates, names, and summaries of discussions.
  • Submit all formal requests for evaluations in writing to trigger legal timelines.
