Executive Leadership

Conroe ISD (CISD) is one of the fastest-growing school districts in the Houston metro area, serving over 70,000 students across Montgomery County. The special education leadership team provides the strategic vision and compliance oversight necessary to support a diverse range of student needs. When campus-level resolutions are not sufficient, these executive administrators serve as the primary point of accountability for your child's educational rights.

Key Coordinators

In Conroe ISD, specialized program coordinators manage the nuanced requirements of specific disability categories. They provide training to campus staff and ensure that the district adheres to the latest Texas Education Agency guidelines for specialized instruction.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages the district's response to HB 3928, ensuring students with dyslexia are properly evaluated and receive "specially designed instruction" within the special education framework rather than just standard 504 accommodations.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Focuses on programming for students on the autism spectrum, assisting with behavioral strategies and the implementation of the "Autism Supplement" required during ARD committee meetings.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Supervises the diagnosticians and LSSPs across CISD. This office ensures that the 45-school-day timeline for Full and Individual Initial Evaluations (FIIE) is strictly maintained.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

The central point of contact for obtaining your child's ARD and IEP records. This is critical for families moving into the district or seeking independent medical evaluations.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the main special education office at 936-709-7751 for referral to the appropriate person for your child's specific campus.

Who to Contact When

Understanding the hierarchy of communication in Conroe ISD helps ensure your concerns are heard by the right person. In Texas, all special education decisions happen within the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) committee process.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you believe your child has a disability and requires support, submit a written request for evaluation to the district office and your campus principal to trigger the legal timelines.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Your child's Case Manager or Campus Diagnostician

For questions regarding specific accommodations in the classroom or progress on IEP goals, the staff working directly with your child on campus are your best first stop.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

If you are relocating or seeking a second opinion from a private doctor, contact the district's special education records department to request a complete copy of your child's educational file.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If issues cannot be resolved with the campus principal or the special education department's area coordinators, the Executive Director manages the formal grievance and mediation process.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

With recent changes to the Texas Dyslexia Handbook, many students are transitioning from 504 to Special Education. Contact this office to understand how these changes impact your child's services.
