Quick Facts: Dispute Resolution in Conroe ISD

  • Your Rights: You can disagree with ARD decisions without fear of retaliation under IDEA.
  • Initial Deadline: Formal local grievances must be filed within 15 district business days.
  • Leadership: Special education is overseen by Dr. Kendra Wiggins, Executive Director.
  • Legal Contact: District grievances are filed through the CISD Legal Department in Conroe.
  • State Escalation: TEA complaints must be filed within 1 year of the incident.

Common Questions About Conroe ISD Dispute Resolution

How do I file a special education complaint in Conroe ISD?

Start at campus level. Contact your child's ARD committee chair or campus principal first to attempt informal resolution. If the issue is not resolved, escalate to the district level by submitting a Level One grievance form to the Conroe ISD Legal Department at 3205 W. Davis St., Conroe. Conroe ISD uses a centralized tracking system for complaints to document your concerns and assign an appropriate hearing officer.

What is the difference between a district complaint and a TEA complaint?

District complaints are internal; TEA complaints are external. A district complaint is filed with Conroe ISD under Board Policy FNG(LOCAL). A TEA (Texas Education Agency) complaint is a formal investigation into state or federal law violations. In Conroe ISD, parents are encouraged to reach out to the Special Education Department at 936-709-7813 before filing with TEA to see if a district-level solution can be reached quickly.

How long does Conroe ISD have to respond to my complaint?

Typically 10-15 business days. Once a Level One grievance is filed, Conroe ISD generally schedules a conference within 10 district business days. You will then receive a written response within 10 days of that meeting. If you are unsatisfied, you have 10 business days to appeal to Level Two (Superintendent's Designee).

Dispute Resolution Escalation Path in Conroe ISD

Level 1

Campus-Level Resolution

Contact: ARD Chair, Case Manager, or Principal.

Method: Request an informal meeting or ARD recess to resolve concerns about services or IEP goals.

Level 2

District-Level Grievance

Contact: Conroe ISD Legal Department

Phone: 936-709-7700

Director of Special Education: Dr. Kendra Wiggins

Submit formal forms within 15 days of the issue to the central office on West Davis Street.

Level 3

TEA / Mediation / Due Process

If local levels fail, you may file with TEA or request a state-funded mediator. Due Process hearings are the final legal resort and typically require a special education attorney.
