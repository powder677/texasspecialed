Understanding the Conroe ISD Special Education Model

Conroe ISD utilizes a feeder pattern system where complex evaluations are overseen by the central office at the Deane L. Sadler Administration Building. Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must complete evaluations within 45 school days of receiving written parental consent.

Conroe ISD's specific Operating Guidelines encourage parents to submit requests in writing directly to the campus principal to trigger the official timeline clock. For students with significant needs, the district offers specialized programming like the Social Development Class (SDC) and Life Skills.

A specific resource for families is the use of ARD Facilitators for complex cases. While campus-level staff manage daily compliance, the central administration ensures "Vertical Alignment" as students move through the 348-square-mile district.
