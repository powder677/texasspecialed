About Conroe ISD Special Education

Conroe ISD is a geographically vast district covering 348 square miles. The Department of Special Education, led by Dr. Kendra Wiggins, emphasizes "Vertical Alignment" to ensure consistent support as students transition from elementary to intermediate and high school. The district utilizes a feeder pattern system, often aligning special education coordinators with these zones (e.g., The Woodlands Feeder, Conroe Feeder).

For students with significant needs, Conroe ISD offers specialized programming such as the Social Development Class (SDC) for behavioral challenges and Life Skills courses. Transition services are a priority for students aged 14+, preparing them for post-secondary life.

ARD Facilitators: Conroe ISD frequently employs specialized ARD Facilitators for complex cases. These staff members are trained in special education law and conflict resolution. If your ARD meetings have been unproductive, requesting a trained facilitator is a strategic step available to parents.

Understanding Conroe ISD Board Policies

When advocating for your child in Conroe ISD, it is critical to cite the specific Board Policy codes that govern special education services.

  • Evaluations (FIE): Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must provide a Full and Individual Evaluation within 45 school days of receiving your signed written consent.
  • Dispute Resolution: If you disagree with an ARD committee decision, Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) outlines the formal grievance process, starting with a Level One conference with your campus principal.
  • Parent Participation: Board Policy EHBAB(LEGAL) guarantees your right to participate in ARD committee meetings and review all educational records.
