Common Questions About Katy ISD Dispute Resolution

How do I file a special education complaint in Katy ISD?

Start at the campus level. Contact your child's ARD committee chair or campus principal first. If the issue is not resolved, you must submit a formal Level One grievance form. Katy ISD utilizes a centralized system where forms are submitted via the MyKaty Portal or sent directly to Legal@katyisd.org. KISD's Legal Department tracks these submissions and assigns an administrator to hear the grievance within district timelines.

What if Katy ISD is not following my child's IEP?

Document the missed services immediately. If the district fails to provide services specified in the IEP (like speech therapy or inclusion minutes), you can file a TEA complaint for an IDEA violation. In Katy ISD, parents often find success by first emailing the Executive Director of Special Education, Brian Malechuk, to request an immediate correction before moving to a formal state investigation.

How does the 60-day filing window work in Katy ISD?

Katy ISD is unique in its timeline. While many Texas districts require formal grievances to be filed within 15 days, Katy ISD Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) provides a 60-day window from the time you knew of the issue. This allows parents more time to attempt informal ARD reconvene meetings or department-level mediation before triggering the formal legal grievance process.

Dispute Resolution Escalation Path in Katy ISD

Follow this recommended sequence to resolve special education disputes:

Level 1

Campus-Level Resolution (Start Here)

Contact: ARD Chair, Campus Special Ed Coordinator, or Principal.

Method: Request an informal meeting or an ARD reconvene to discuss concerns.

Timeline: Request a response within 5-10 business days.

Level 2

Formal District Grievance

Method: Submit a Level One form via the MyKaty Portal or email Legal@katyisd.org.

Leadership: Brian Malechuk, Executive Director of Special Education

Phone: 281-396-2630

Address: 6301 S. Stadium Lane, Katy, TX 77494

Timeline: Must file within 60 days of the incident. A conference will be held with a district administrator.

Level 3

Texas Education Agency (TEA) Complaint

File with TEA: TEA Complaints Portal

Timeline: Must file within 1 year of the alleged violation.

Outcome: If TEA finds a violation, Katy ISD must implement corrective actions, such as compensatory services.

Protection Against Retaliation

Katy ISD is prohibited by federal law from retaliating against parents who advocate for their child's rights. Documentation is your best defense.

Your Rights:

  • ✓ KISD cannot reduce services as a "punishment" for a complaint.
  • ✓ School staff cannot exclude you from ARD participation.
  • ✓ District must address grievances objectively regardless of past history.
