Quick Facts: Evaluations in Katy ISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full (Individual) Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, teachers, or school staff can refer a child.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to completed evaluation.
  • Cost: Free to parents; Katy ISD covers all assessment costs.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting within 30 calendar days of completed report.

Common Questions About Katy ISD Evaluations

How do I request a special education evaluation in Katy ISD?

Submit a written request to your child's campus principal. For children not yet in school (ages 3-5), contact the Special Education Department at 281-396-2630.

Sample Language: "I am writing to formally request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], DOB [Date], currently in [Grade] at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Katy ISD?

45 school days maximum. Once you sign and return the consent form, Katy ISD has 45 school days to complete all testing. An ARD meeting must then be held within 30 calendar days.

What tests are included in a Katy ISD evaluation?

Assessments are tailored to suspected disabilities. Common tests include psychological (IQ), academic achievement (reading/math), speech and language, and social-emotional status. HB 3928 requires dyslexia testing to occur through this special education process.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. Medical diagnoses (like ADHD) are not required. Katy ISD assesses for "educational eligibility" to determine if a student needs specialized instruction.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE)?

Yes, if you disagree with the district's results. An IEE is conducted by a qualified professional not employed by Katy ISD, paid for at public expense.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

An Initial ARD meeting is held within 30 days. The committee reviews the report to determine eligibility and develop an Individualized Education Program (IEP) if the student qualifies.

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

An FIIE is a comprehensive assessment used to determine if a child qualifies for special education under federal law (IDEA). It determines:

  • Is there a disability? One of the 13 categories recognized in Texas.
  • Does it impact education? The disability must require specially designed instruction.
  • What supports are needed? Testing data informs the goals and accommodations in the IEP.

13 Disability Categories in Texas Special Education

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment Specific Learning Disability Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment

How to Request an Evaluation in Katy ISD

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Deliver your request in writing to ensure the 15-school-day response timeline begins.

Sample Request Letter

[Date]

[Campus Principal Name]
[Katy ISD School Name]

Dear Principal [Name],

I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child, [Child's Name], DOB [Date]. I am concerned about [brief description: academic progress, behavior, etc.]. 

I understand that the district has 15 school days to provide a response and consent forms.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Within 15 school days, Katy ISD must provide a Prior Written Notice. If they agree, you will sign a consent form.

Step 3: Evaluation Period (45 School Days)

The 45-school-day timeline begins only after the district receives your signed consent.

The Evaluation Timeline in Katy ISD

Day 0: Parent submits written request.

Day 15: District provides consent form or refusal.

Clock Starts: Parent signs consent; 45-school-day testing window begins.

Day 45: Written evaluation report completed.

Within 30 Days: Initial ARD meeting held to review eligibility.

Important: Timelines are based on school days. Summer, winter break, and district holidays do not count toward the 45-day window.

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Qualified Katy ISD specialists conduct evaluations in several key areas based on suspected need:

Psychological

Assesses cognition, intelligence, and processing skills.

Academic

Evaluates reading, math, and writing performance.

Social-Emotional

Reviews behavioral and mental health indicators.

After the Evaluation: The ARD Meeting

Once testing is complete, an ARD meeting must be held within 30 calendar days.

Determining Eligibility

The ARD committee (including parents) reviews data to decide if the student has a disability and needs specialized instruction.

Prepare for Your ARD Meeting

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with the district's evaluation, you may request an IEE at public expense. Katy ISD must either fund the private evaluation or file for a hearing to prove their testing was appropriate.

Katy ISD Evaluation Specifics

Dyslexia Assessments (HB 3928)

In accordance with Texas law, all dyslexia testing in Katy ISD now occurs via the special education framework (IDEA), providing students with more robust legal protections.
