Katy ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Katy ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators serving the Katy and West Houston communities.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

Katy ISD is one of the fastest-growing districts in the Houston metro area. The special education leadership team is tasked with managing high-volume growth while maintaining compliance with Texas Education Agency (TEA) standards. When you have concerns that cannot be resolved at the campus level, these individuals provide the final district-level oversight for your child's program.

Key Coordinators

Because Katy ISD serves nearly 95,000 students, the central special education office uses a specialized coordinator model. These individuals are experts in specific fields—like autism or dyslexia—and ensure that campus staff are following the most current research-based practices and state laws.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Responsible for the district's implementation of HB 3928. They oversee the transition of students with dyslexia from 504 plans to specialized instruction under the special education umbrella.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Directs the support for students with autism, focusing on behavioral strategies, social-emotional learning, and ensuring the "Autism Supplement" is correctly addressed in ARD meetings.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Oversees the team of diagnosticians and LSSPs who perform initial evaluations and reevaluations. They are the point of contact if evaluation timelines are not being met.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

Manages the transfer and release of confidential IEP and ARD records. This is your primary contact when moving into the district from another state or Texas district.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the main special education office at 281-396-6000 for referral to the appropriate person for your specific school feeder pattern.

Who to Contact When

Knowing who to call can save weeks of frustration. In Katy ISD, as in all of Texas, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process is the only legal way to change your child's services. Use the following guide to navigate the system.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you suspect your child has a disability that impacts their learning, submit a formal, written request for an evaluation to the district’s central office and your school principal.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Campus Case Manager or Diagnostician

For day-to-day questions about accommodations, modified curriculum, or meeting dates, your child’s campus-based team is always the first point of contact.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

When you need historical data for a private doctor or are preparing to move, contact the central records office for an official copy of your child's file.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If an issue cannot be resolved through the campus-level ARD committee or with the campus principal, the Executive Director of Special Education provides the next tier of administrative review.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

With the updated Texas Dyslexia Handbook, many services have moved to Special Education. Contact the coordinator if you have questions about your child's current 504 status versus Special Education eligibility.
