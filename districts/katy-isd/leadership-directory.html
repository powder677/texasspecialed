Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]".
  • Include your child's full name, DOB, and campus in every email.
  • Request written responses to verbal conversations to maintain a paper trail.

📞 Phone Contact

  • Call during business hours: Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM.
  • Follow up phone calls with a brief "summary email" to the district staff member.
  • Request confirmation of verbal agreements in writing.
