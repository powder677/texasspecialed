Quick Facts: Evaluations in Killeen ISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full (Individual) Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, teachers, or school staff can refer a child.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to completed evaluation.
  • Cost: Free to parents; the district covers all assessment costs.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting held within 30 days of report completion.

Request a Special Education Evaluation in Killeen ISD

Essential guidance for Killeen, Harker Heights, and Nolanville families. Learn how to start the evaluation process, understand legal timelines, and advocate for your child’s success in Killeen ISD.

Common Questions About Killeen ISD Evaluations

How do I request a special education evaluation in Killeen ISD?

Submit a written request to your child’s Campus Principal or Paula Lawrason. Written requests are essential as they trigger legal timelines under Texas law.

Sample Language: "I am writing to formally request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], DOB [Date], at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Killeen ISD?

45 school days for testing. From the date the district receives your signed consent, Killeen ISD has 45 school days to complete the FIIE. An ARD meeting must then be held within 30 calendar days.

What tests are included in a Killeen ISD evaluation?

Assessments are tailored to the child's specific needs. Testing may include intelligence (IQ) tests, academic achievement, speech/language, motor skills, and Functional Behavioral Assessments (FBA). Dyslexia evaluations are also now conducted through this special education process per HB 3928.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. You do not need a medical diagnosis (like ADHD or Autism) to request school testing. Killeen ISD evaluates for "educational eligibility" to determine if a student requires specialized instruction.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE)?

Yes, if you disagree with the district's results. An IEE is an evaluation by a qualified professional not employed by Killeen ISD, paid for at public expense.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

An Initial ARD meeting is held within 30 days. The committee, including parents, reviews the report to determine eligibility and, if eligible, develops an Individualized Education Program (IEP).

How do I get an evaluation for my 3-year-old?

Contact the Specialized Learning department. Killeen ISD evaluates children starting at age three. You can call 254-336-0361 to begin the referral process for preschoolers.

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

An FIIE is the comprehensive assessment process used in Texas to determine if a student qualifies for special education under federal law (IDEA). It evaluates:

  • Is there a disability? Whether the student meets one of the 13 federal disability categories.
  • Educational Impact: Does the disability impact their ability to perform in a school setting?
  • Need for Services: Does the student require specialized instruction or accommodations?

13 Disability Categories in Texas Special Education

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment Specific Learning Disability Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Killeen ISD has 15 school days to respond with consent forms or a Prior Written Notice explaining a refusal.

Step 3: Evaluation Period (45 School Days)

The 45-school-day timeline begins only after the district receives your signed consent.

The Evaluation Timeline in Killeen ISD

Day 0: Parent submits written request.
Day 15: District provides consent form or refusal.
Clock Starts: Parent signs consent; 45-school-day window begins.
Day 45: Written FIIE report must be completed.
Within 30 Days: Initial ARD meeting must be held.

Note: Absences of more than three days or district holidays may extend the 45-school-day deadline.

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Qualified specialists evaluate your child in all areas of suspected disability:

Cognitive/Intelligence

Measures IQ, processing speed, and problem-solving skills.

Academic Achievement

Assesses performance in reading, writing, and math core competencies.

Social-Emotional

Screens for behavioral health and emotional indicators.

After the Evaluation: The ARD Meeting

Once testing is complete, Killeen ISD will schedule an ARD meeting within 30 calendar days.

✓ Determining Eligibility

The ARD committee (including parents) reviews the report to decide if the child qualifies for special education. If eligible, an IEP is developed to outline specific goals and services.

Prepare for Your ARD Meeting

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with Killeen ISD’s evaluation results, you have the right to request an IEE. This is a second opinion conducted by an outside professional at the district's expense.

Killeen ISD Evaluation Specifics

Dyslexia Assessments (HB 3928)

Under Texas HB 3928, all dyslexia evaluations in Killeen ISD are now handled through the special education (IDEA) framework. This ensures students with dyslexia receive the same robust legal protections and comprehensive assessments as other special education students.
