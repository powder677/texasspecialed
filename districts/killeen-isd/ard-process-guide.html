Answer: In Killeen ISD, an ARD meeting is the legally required Texas special education meeting where parents and district staff determine eligibility, services, and placement for a student. Parents in Killeen ISD must receive at least five school days’ written notice, are legally recognized as equal committee members, and may audio record any session.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to Killeen ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: ARD Meetings in Killeen ISD

  • Notice Timeline: Minimum 5 school days' written notice required.
  • Recording Rights: Parents may audio record; Killeen ISD typically requests advance notice for logistics.
  • Military Support: Specialized resources available for families PCSing to or from Fort Cavazos.
  • Annual Requirement: The committee must meet at least once every 12 months.
  • Initial ARD: Must be held within 30 days of a completed Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).

How ARD Meetings in Killeen ISD May Differ From Other Districts

Due to its unique location serving Fort Cavazos, Killeen ISD has specialized procedures for highly mobile families and specific staffing models:

  • Military Impacted Procedures: Killeen ISD utilizes "Military Liaisons" and EFMP (Exceptional Family Member Program) coordination to expedite the transfer of IEP services for students arriving from out-of-state or overseas.
  • Collaborative Staffing: In Killeen ISD, Educational Diagnosticians and Licensed Specialists in School Psychology (LSSPs) work in a closely coupled "onsite" model to interpret evaluation data during meetings.
  • ARD Committee Managers: The district often employs dedicated ARD Committee Managers to facilitate meetings, ensuring federal and local compliance is met consistently across campuses.

How to Prepare for an ARD Meeting in Killeen ISD

1. Review Data via Parent Portal

Use the Killeen ISD Home Access Center to review current report cards and progress reports before the meeting.

2. Request Draft Documents

Request a "Draft IEP" from your child's case manager at least 3-5 days prior to the ARD to review proposed goals.

3. Document Parent Concerns

Prepare a written statement of your concerns; under Texas law, these must be recorded in the official meeting deliberations.

Common Killeen ISD ARD Questions

Can I bring an advocate to a Killeen ISD ARD?

Yes, parents have the right to bring an advocate or representative to the meeting. Killeen ISD asks for advance notice to ensure the meeting space is adequate.

What happens if I disagree with the ARD committee's decision?

If consensus is not reached, a 10-day recess is typically offered to allow the committee and parents to gather more data before reconvening.
