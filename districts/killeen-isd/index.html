Military-Connected Rights in Killeen ISD

Killeen Independent School District operates with a specific focus on the Interstate Compact on Educational Opportunity for Military Children. Under this compact, if you are a military family transferring from an out-of-state installation to Fort Cavazos, Killeen ISD is legally required to provide comparable services based on your child's current IEP. Per Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must initiate these services immediately while determining if the previous evaluation meets Texas standards.

Initial evaluations and complex re-evaluations are centrally managed at the Special Education Annex on N. 10th Street, rather than the WS Young main administration building. For families enrolled in the Exceptional Family Member Program (EFMP), coordination should begin with the campus Special Education Coordinator, but can be escalated to the Fort Cavazos School Liaison Officer if IEP implementation timelines are not met.

For students with significant behavioral needs, KISD utilizes Behavior Skills Classrooms (BSC) located at targeted campuses across the district. Specialized medical and cognitive support is provided through Skills and Functional Academics units. Furthermore, as the fiscal agent for the Regional Day School Program for the Deaf (RDSPD), Killeen ISD provides the highest level of auditory impairment support in the Central Texas region, serving neighboring districts like Copperas Cove and Temple.
