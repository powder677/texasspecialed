Killeen ISD Special Education Leadership & Contact Directory

Direct contacts for special education services, ARD meetings, evaluations, and support in Killeen ISD, serving military-connected families in the Fort Cavazos and Central Texas area.

Who Should I Contact?

For ARD Meeting Questions

Start with your Campus Special Education Coordinator or Diagnostician. They manage the specific implementation of your child's IEP.

For Evaluation Requests

Contact: KISD Evaluation Coordinator

Phone: 254-336-0366

Submit written requests for FIIE (Full and Individual Initial Evaluation) to this office or your campus principal.

For Complaints or Disputes

Start: Campus Principal

Escalate to: Executive Director of Special Education

View dispute resolution process →

For Records & Transfers

Special Education Records Clerk

Phone: 254-336-0366

Critical for military families arriving at Fort Cavazos to ensure continuity of services.

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]".
  • Include your child's full name, date of birth, and current school in every email.
  • Ask for a "read receipt" or a confirmation of receipt for legal requests.

📞 Military Family Tips

  • If moving to Fort Cavazos, contact the Records Clerk at least 30 days before arrival to ensure IEP records are transferred.
  • Mention your military status to help the district apply MIC3 (Interstate Compact on Educational Opportunity for Military Children) protections.
