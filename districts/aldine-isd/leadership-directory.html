Key Program Coordinators

Aldine ISD utilizes a coordinator-based system to provide specialized oversight across different disability areas and instructional levels.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact: Special Education Department

Focus: Compliance with HB 3928, ensuring dyslexia services are provided through Specially Designed Instruction (SDI).

Autism Specialist

Contact: Behavior Support Team

Programs: Support for the 11 strategies of the Texas Autism Supplement and campus-based ASD programming.

Evaluation & Assessment

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator

Handles: Management of Full and Individual Initial Evaluations (FIIE) and reevaluation timelines.

Special Education Records

Contact: Records Clerk

Focus: Processing requests for ARD documents, IEPs, and evaluation reports for families and medical providers.

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Keep initial emails brief and focused on one specific request or concern.
  • Request written confirmation of any verbal agreements made during phone calls.

📝 Documentation

  • Maintain a "Communication Log" with dates, names, and summaries of all district interactions.
  • Submit formal requests (like Evaluation requests) in writing to both the principal and the special education office.
  • Keep copies of your child's most recent ARD and Evaluation (FIE) in a dedicated binder.
