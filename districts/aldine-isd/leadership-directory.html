Aldine ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Aldine ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators serving the North Houston area.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

Aldine ISD serves nearly 58,000 students in the Houston area. The Special Education Department is dedicated to providing individualized support that empowers students with disabilities to achieve their full potential. The leadership team ensures that the district remains compliant with state and federal regulations while providing specialized training to campus personnel across the district.

Key Coordinators

Aldine ISD utilizes a coordinator-based system to provide specialized oversight for different disability areas and compliance requirements. These professionals are the bridge between the central administration and your child's specific campus programming.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Oversees dyslexia services under the requirements of HB 3928, ensuring students receive evidence-based specially designed instruction through the Special Education department.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Supports students with autism spectrum disorders and provides guidance to ARD committees on the 11 strategies included in the Texas Autism Supplement.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages the evaluation teams responsible for Full and Individual Initial Evaluations (FIIE), ensuring timelines are met and results are clearly communicated to families.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

The primary contact for obtaining copies of your child's IEP and ARD documentation. This is vital for families transitioning between districts or seeking outside services.

Note: If specific names are not listed, call the main special education office at 281-449-1011 for a direct referral to the coordinator responsible for your child's campus.

Who to Contact When

In Texas, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process is the primary way for parents to advocate for their child's education. Knowing who to call in Aldine ISD ensures that your child's needs are addressed promptly and effectively.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you suspect your child has a disability, you should submit a formal written request for evaluation to both your school's principal and the special education administration office.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Campus Diagnostician or Case Manager

For questions regarding your child’s current accommodations, IEP goals, or to request a new ARD meeting, start with the staff at your child’s campus.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

Contact the records office if you need a copy of your child's most recent ARD or evaluation for medical providers or insurance purposes.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If concerns cannot be resolved at the campus level, the Executive Director of Special Education provides the next tier of administrative oversight and conflict resolution.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

Because dyslexia is now evaluated through special education in Texas, this coordinator manages the transition of students from Section 504 to specialized instruction.
