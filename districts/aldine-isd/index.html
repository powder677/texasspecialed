Understanding the Aldine ISD Model

Navigating special education in Aldine ISD requires an understanding of their "Annex" structure. Unlike many districts where SpEd leadership is housed in the main administration building on Thorne Blvd, Aldine’s Department of Special Education functions out of the Aldine Westfield Annex. This is where all district-wide evaluations, IEE (Independent Educational Evaluation) reviews, and due process filings are centralized.

Under Board Policy FNG(LOCAL), parents who disagree with an ARD committee decision are encouraged to use the district's formal grievance process, starting with the campus Principal before escalating to the Executive Director at the Annex.

📍 Evaluation Tip for Parents

To officially "start the clock" on an evaluation, Board Policy EHBAA(LEGAL) suggests providing a written request to the campus diagnostician. In Aldine, ensure you receive a "Notice of Procedural Safeguards" and sign the "Consent for Evaluation" form specifically—verbal requests do not trigger the legal timeline.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I transfer my IEP to Aldine ISD?

When enrolling via the Aldine Enrollment Portal, you must upload your most recent FIE and IEP. Aldine ISD will implement "comparable services" for the first 30 days before holding a Transfer ARD to finalize the placement.

What is the "Lane School" in Aldine?

The Lane School is a specialized campus within Aldine ISD designed for students whose behavioral needs require a more restrictive environment than a typical Structured Learning Classroom (SLC) on a comprehensive campus.
