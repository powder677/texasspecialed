Quick Facts: Evaluations in Aldine ISD

  • What is it? FIIE = Full Individual Initial Evaluation to determine special education eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, legal guardians, or school staff.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to completed report.
  • Cost: $0. Evaluations are provided at no cost to families.
  • Next Step: Initial ARD meeting within 30 days of the evaluation.

Request a Special Education Evaluation in Aldine ISD

Essential guidance for North Houston families. Learn how to start the evaluation process, understand Texas timelines, and advocate for your child's needs in Aldine Independent School District.

Common Questions About Aldine ISD Evaluations

```

How do I request a special education evaluation in Aldine ISD?

Submit a written request to your child's principal. In Aldine ISD, the most direct path is providing a signed letter to the campus leader. For children not yet in school (ages 3-5), contact the Aldine ISD Special Education Department at 281-985-6602.

Sample language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], DOB [Date], currently enrolled at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Aldine ISD?

45 school days maximum. Once you sign the Written Consent form, Aldine ISD has 45 school days (excluding holidays and weekends) to complete all assessments and provide you with a written report.

What tests are included in an Aldine ISD evaluation?

Varies by suspected disability. Common assessments used by the district include cognitive/IQ testing, academic achievement tests (reading, math, writing), and behavioral scales. If autism or ADHD is suspected, a school psychologist (LSSP) will perform specific observations.

Does my child need a medical diagnosis first?

No. Under the "Child Find" mandate, Aldine ISD must evaluate any child suspected of having a disability. A medical diagnosis can be helpful data, but it is not a prerequisite for school-based testing.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE)?

Yes, if you disagree with the district's report. If you believe the Aldine ISD evaluation was insufficient, you can request an IEE. If granted, the district pays for an outside professional to conduct a new evaluation.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

An ARD meeting within 30 calendar days. The district must schedule a committee meeting to review the FIIE report and decide if your child meets Texas eligibility criteria for special education services.

```

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

A FIIE is the legal term for a comprehensive school evaluation in Texas. In Aldine ISD, this process determines:

  • Does the child have a disability? Meeting one of the 13 federal disability categories.
  • Is there an educational need? Does the disability require specialized instruction to make progress?
  • What are the recommendations? What specific supports or accommodations does the child need?
```

13 Disability Categories in Texas

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment Specific Learning Disability Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment
```

How to Request an Evaluation in Aldine ISD

```

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Hand-deliver or email your request to the campus principal. Ensure you get a timestamp or delivery receipt.

Address: 2520 W.W. Thorne Blvd., Houston, TX 77073

Sample Evaluation Request Letter


```

[Date]

To the Principal of [School Name] / Aldine ISD Special Education Dept,

I am writing to formally request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child, [Child's Name], [DOB], currently in [Grade].

I am concerned about my child's progress in [Area of Concern: Reading, Behavior, etc.]. I believe they may have a disability under IDEA.

I look forward to receiving the Notice of Evaluation and consent forms within 15 school days.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[Your Phone]

```

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Within 15 school days, Aldine ISD must provide you with a Notice of Evaluation and a consent form, or a Prior Written Notice (PWN) explaining why they are refusing to test.

Step 3: The 45-Day Evaluation Period

Once you sign consent, the clock starts. Aldine ISD specialists (Diagnosticians, LSSPs, SLPs) will work with your child to complete all required assessments.

```

The Evaluation Timeline in Aldine ISD

Day 1

Parent Submits Request

15-school-day window begins for district response.

Day 0

Parent Signs Consent

45-school-day clock starts. This is the most critical deadline.

Day 45

Evaluation Complete

Report is written and shared with the family.

Within 30 Days

Initial ARD Meeting

Committee determines eligibility and creates the IEP.

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Cognitive/IQ

Who: Diagnostician or LSSP

Measures: Problem-solving, memory, and processing speed.

Academic Achievement

Who: Educational Diagnostician

Measures: Reading fluency, math calculation, and written expression.

Speech & Language

Who: SLP

Measures: Articulation, vocabulary, and social communication.

Behavioral

Who: LSSP

Measures: Emotional regulation, attention (ADHD), and social skills.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with the evaluation findings from Aldine ISD, you have the right to request an IEE. This is a second opinion by an outside expert paid for by the school district.

To request an IEE, contact the Special Education Director in writing stating that you disagree with the district's evaluation and are requesting an IEE at public expense.
