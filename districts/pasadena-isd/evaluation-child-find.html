Quick Facts: Evaluations in Pasadena ISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full (Individual) Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, teachers, or school staff can refer a child.
  • Timeline: 60 calendar days from signed consent to completed evaluation and ARD meeting.
  • Cost: Free to parents; district covers testing.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting within the 60-day window to review results.

Request a Special Education Evaluation in Pasadena ISD

Essential guidance for Pasadena families. Learn how to start the evaluation process, understand timelines, and advocate for your child's needs in Pasadena Independent School District.

Common Questions About Pasadena ISD Evaluations

```

How do I request a special education evaluation in Pasadena ISD?

Submit a written request to the Executive Director of Special Education. You may send it to Michelle LeBleu at 3920 Mickey Gilley Blvd., Pasadena, TX 77505.

Sample language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], date of birth [DOB], currently enrolled at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Pasadena ISD?

60 calendar days maximum. Once Pasadena ISD receives your signed written consent, they have 60 calendar days to complete the evaluation and hold the Initial ARD meeting.

What tests are included in a Pasadena ISD evaluation?

Assessments vary by suspected disability. Testing may include academic, cognitive, speech, OT, PT, or functional behavioral evaluations. This includes dyslexia screening under Texas HB 3928 protocols.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. You do not need a doctor's diagnosis to start the evaluation process in Pasadena ISD. The district evaluates based on educational need under Child Find.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) in Pasadena ISD?

Yes. If you disagree with the district's evaluation results, you have the right to request an IEE at public expense.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

Initial ARD meeting. The ARD committee will meet within the 60-day window to review reports and determine eligibility for services.

```

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

An FIIE is a comprehensive assessment required by federal law to determine eligibility for special education. In Pasadena ISD, this process identifies if your child has a disability and requires specially designed instruction.

How to Request an Evaluation in Pasadena ISD

```

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Send a written evaluation request to the district.

Where to Send Your Request:

Primary Contact: Michelle LeBleu, Executive Director of Special Education

Phone: 713-740-0220

Address: 3920 Mickey Gilley Blvd., Pasadena, TX 77505

Sample Evaluation Request Letter


```

[Date]

Michelle LeBleu
Executive Director of Special Education
Pasadena ISD
3920 Mickey Gilley Blvd.
Pasadena, TX 77505

Dear Ms. LeBleu,

I am writing to formally request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child, [Child's Full Name], date of birth [DOB], currently enrolled at [School Name].

I am concerned about my child's progress in [Academic Area/Behavior/Speech]. I believe [Child's Name] may have a disability that requires special education services under IDEA.

Please provide me with the necessary consent forms to begin this process. I understand that the 60-calendar-day timeline begins once you receive my signed consent.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
Download Fillable Template
```

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Pasadena ISD must provide a response (Prior Written Notice) within 15 school days of your request. If they agree to evaluate, they will provide a Consent for Evaluation form.

Step 3: Evaluation Period (60 Calendar Days)

The 60 calendar day timeline begins once the district receives your signed written consent. Assessments are conducted in all areas of suspected disability.

Step 4: Initial ARD Meeting

Within the 60-day window, the district must complete evaluations and schedule an ARD meeting to review results and determine eligibility.

```

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Evaluations in Pasadena ISD may include:

Academic & Cognitive

Evaluations of intellectual ability and academic achievement.

Speech & Language

Evaluations of communication, articulation, and language processing.

OT & PT

Assessments of fine and gross motor skills.

Specialized Evaluations

Functional Behavioral Assessments (FBA) and dyslexia screenings under IDEA protocols.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with Pasadena ISD's evaluation results, you have the right to request an IEE at public expense. This is an evaluation conducted by a qualified professional not employed by the district.
