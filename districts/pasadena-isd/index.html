Policy Anchor: Special Education in Pasadena ISD

Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), Pasadena ISD must complete a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) within 45 school days of receiving written parental consent. Navigating this process in PISD typically begins with the Intervention Assistance Team (IAT) at the campus level (e.g., Pasadena High School or Sam Rayburn High). While the IAT provides critical support, it does not legally replace your right to request a formal evaluation in writing.

The district utilizes high-authority specialized programs for students with complex needs. If your child requires a highly structured behavioral or developmental environment, the ABLE (Academic and Behavior Learning Environment) program is the primary placement option. For students requiring significant medical or cognitive support, the ACCESS (Alternative Curriculum Centered for Exceptional Student Success) program focuses on functional life skills and sensory stimulation.

When disputes arise regarding an IEP or placement, parents should refer to Board Policy FNG(LOCAL). This policy mandates a specific grievance chain that starts with the campus principal and moves to the Special Programs administration on Cherrybrook Lane. For students aged 14 and older, the PISD Transition Services team provides mandatory coordination for post-secondary employment and living, as required by the Texas Transition and Employment Guide.

Communication Chain of Command

Pasadena ISD separates its leadership to ensure specialized oversight:

  • Instructional Issues: Contact the Coordinator for Programming and Instruction.
  • Testing/Eligibility: Contact the Coordinator for Evaluation and Compliance.
  • Grievances: Follow the steps outlined in Board Policy FNG(LOCAL).
