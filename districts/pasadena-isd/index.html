Navigating Pasadena ISD Specialized Services

Special education in Pasadena ISD is built on a "Special Programs" model that integrates services for students with disabilities, dyslexia, and Section 504 needs. Administrative oversight is centralized at the Mickey Gilley Boulevard office, but programmatic decisions are driven by the campus-based Intervention Assistance Team (IAT). For families seeking an initial evaluation, the IAT process serves as the preliminary step to document the need for a formal referral under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL).

The district utilizes two primary specialized settings: ABLE (Academic and Behavior Learning Environment) for students with high-structure developmental needs, and ACCESS (Alternative Curriculum Centered for Exceptional Student Success) for students requiring intensive functional life skills. In Pasadena ISD, the ARD committee—including the Campus Principal and the Special Education Teacher—determines the least restrictive environment for these placements.

For secondary students, PISD Transition Specialists coordinate post-secondary pathways via the district's Transition and Employment Guide. If a dispute arises regarding an Individualized Education Program (IEP), families should initiate resolution via Board Policy FNG(LOCAL), starting with an informal conference with Basilio Leija Jr. before moving to formal mediation or a TEA due process hearing.
