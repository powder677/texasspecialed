Quick Facts: Dispute Resolution in Pasadena ISD

  • Your Rights: You can disagree with any ARD decision without fear of retaliation from PISD staff.
  • Start Here: Informal campus-level resolution with the ARD chair or Principal is highly recommended.
  • Grievance Deadline: Local FNG complaints must typically be filed within 15 district business days.
  • State Option: TEA complaints focus on legal/procedural violations and have a 1-year statute of limitations.
  • Mediation: A neutral third party from TEA or Region 4 can help facilitate agreements.

Common Questions About Pasadena ISD Dispute Resolution

How do I file a special education complaint in Pasadena ISD?

Start at the campus level. Contact your child's ARD committee chair or campus principal first to attempt informal resolution. If the issue is not resolved within 10-15 business days, escalate to the district level by filing a Level One Student/Parent Complaint Form. This form should be hand-delivered or sent via certified mail to the Associate Superintendent for Campus Development at the Pasadena ISD Administration Building. PISD assigns a specific administrator to hear your grievance and issue a formal written decision.

What is the difference between a district complaint and a TEA complaint?

District complaints are handled internally; TEA complaints trigger state investigation. A district complaint is filed directly with Pasadena ISD under Board Policy FNG (LOCAL). It is a process designed to resolve issues regarding specific staff actions, local policy, or IEP implementation. A TEA (Texas Education Agency) complaint is a formal state-level investigation into violations of federal special education law (IDEA). In Pasadena ISD, parents are not required to exhaust district-level options before filing with TEA, though doing so can often result in a faster local fix.

How long does Pasadena ISD have to respond to a complaint?

Response times follow the PISD local board policy. For formal grievances (FNG Local), the district typically schedules a conference within 10 business days. Following the conference, the district administrator has 10 business days to provide you with a written response. If you are using the informal route by contacting the Special Education Department at (713) 740-0220, response times vary but are generally expected within 2-3 business days.

What happens if I disagree with the ARD committee's decision?

You have several immediate options. If you disagree during an ARD meeting in Pasadena ISD, you can request a "recess" to reconvene within 10 school days, or you can sign the IEP in disagreement. This triggers your right to pursue dispute resolution. If the disagreement involves the appropriateness of the program (FAPE) or placement, you may consider requesting a Due Process Hearing or seeking TEA mediation.

Campus-Level Resolution (Start Here)

Contact: Your child's ARD committee chair, Campus Special Education Coordinator, or the Principal.

Timeline: Informal; request a response within 5 school days.

Goal: To resolve misunderstandings or minor implementation issues immediately at the source.

Level 2

District-Level Complaint

Pasadena ISD Complaint Process:

Method: Submit a Level One Student/Parent Complaint Form (FNG LOCAL).

Contact: Executive Director of Special Education

Phone: (713) 740-0220

Pasadena ISD Complaint System Details

Does Pasadena ISD require campus-level resolution first?

Recommended but not strictly required. Pasadena ISD strongly encourages parents to attempt resolution at the campus level before escalating to the Central Administration building. If you bypass the campus, the Special Education Director's office will often inquire if the principal has been notified to give them an opportunity to address the situation locally first.

How does Pasadena ISD's complaint tracking work?

Formal Documentation. For grievances filed under FNG (LOCAL), Pasadena ISD maintains a formal log. Each submission is date-stamped upon receipt at the Administration Building. For informal calls to the Special Education department, a clerk or coordinator typically documents the concern in a communication log that is shared with the campus leadership for follow-up.

Protection Against Retaliation

Parents who file complaints in Pasadena ISD are protected by federal law from retaliation. This means the district cannot treat your child differently, reduce services, or exclude you from school activities because you advocated for your child's rights.

Your Rights Under IDEA:

  • ✓ Protection from negative treatment by school staff due to advocacy.
  • ✓ The right to record ARD meetings (with 24-hour notice to PISD).
  • ✓ Objective review of your concerns by district-level administrators.
  • ✓ The right to bring an advocate or attorney to any meeting.
