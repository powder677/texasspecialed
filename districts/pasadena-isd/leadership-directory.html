Pasadena ISD Special Education Leadership & Contact Directory

Direct contacts for special education services, ARD meetings, evaluations, and support in Pasadena ISD, serving the Houston metro area.

Who Should I Contact?

For ARD Meeting Questions

Start with your child's ARD case manager or campus special education coordinator.

If unresolved: Contact the district-wide coordinator for your child's programming needs.

For Evaluation Requests

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator

Phone: 713-740-0220

Email: Main Office Inquiry

For Complaints or Disputes

Start: Campus principal

Escalate to: Executive Director of Special Education

View dispute resolution process →

For General Questions

Special Education Main Office

Phone: 713-740-0220

Address: 1515 Cherrybrook Ln, Pasadena, TX 77502

Special Education Department Leadership

Pasadena ISD serves over 47,000 students, ensuring every student with a disability receives a Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE).

PISD Administration

Executive Director of Special Education

District Administration

Phone: 713-740-0220

Office: 1515 Cherrybrook Ln, Pasadena, TX 77502

Oversees: Strategic direction of special education services, compliance with Texas state standards, and administrative reviews.

Key Coordinators & Specialists

While campus teachers are daily contacts, these coordinators manage district-wide resources and training:

Dyslexia Coordinator

Focus: Oversees identification and instruction; ensures adherence to HB 3928.

Contact: 713-740-0220

Autism Specialist

Focus: Expert guidance on evidence-based practices, behavioral support, and sensory modifications.

Contact: 713-740-0220

Evaluation Coordinator

Focus: Oversight of Diagnosticians and LSSPs; monitors Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) timelines.

Contact: 713-740-0220

Records Clerk

Focus: Secure transfer of Special Education records and IEP files between districts.

Contact: 713-740-0220

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]".
  • Submit Suspected Disability/Evaluation requests in writing to the principal or coordinator.
  • Follow up phone calls with a written summary via email.

📞 Phone & Records

  • Call during business hours: Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM.
  • Request IEP records when seeking outside medical consultations.
  • For specific names not listed, call 713-740-0220 for referral.
