Pasadena ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Pasadena ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators serving the Houston metro area.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

Pasadena ISD serves over 47,000 students in the Greater Houston area. The Special Education department is structured to ensure that every student with a disability receives a Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE) through individualized support and compliance with Texas state standards.

Key Coordinators

In Pasadena ISD, leadership is distributed among specialists who focus on specific programming needs. While your child's campus teacher is your daily contact, these coordinators manage the district-wide resources and training that support your child's ARD committee.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Oversees the identification and instruction for students with dyslexia. They ensure the district adheres to HB 3928, which mandates that dyslexia evaluations and services are part of the special education framework in Texas.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Provides expert guidance on evidence-based practices for students on the autism spectrum. This includes behavioral support, social skills curriculum, and sensory environment modifications.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Responsible for the oversight of Educational Diagnosticians and Licensed Specialists in School Psychology (LSSPs). They monitor the timelines for Full and Individual Initial Evaluations (FIIE).

Records Clerk

Contact main office

Manages the secure transfer of Special Education records. If you are transferring into Pasadena ISD from another Houston area district, this office facilitates the receipt of your child's previous IEP.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the main special education office at 713-740-0000 for referral to the appropriate person.

Who to Contact When

Navigating a large district like Pasadena ISD requires knowing the chain of command. The goal is always to resolve concerns at the level closest to the student before escalating to district leadership.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you suspect a disability, you have the right to request an evaluation. Submit your request in writing to the campus principal or the district's evaluation coordinator to start the legal timeline.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Your child's ARD case manager or campus special education coordinator

Your child's teacher or the campus diagnostician is the best person to discuss daily accommodations, progress reports, or scheduling an ARD review.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

Requesting records is a standard part of the process when seeking outside medical consultations or moving. Contact the records office directly for a digital or physical copy of the file.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If a campus-level disagreement regarding services or placement cannot be resolved through an ARD meeting, the Executive Director provides the next level of administrative review.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

With the shift in Texas law, dyslexia services are now under the special education umbrella. Contact this coordinator if you have questions about how your child's 504 plan is transitioning to an IEP.
