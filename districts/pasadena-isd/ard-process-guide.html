Answer: In Pasadena ISD, an ARD meeting is the legally mandated Texas special education process where parents and school staff meet to determine a student's eligibility, services, and placement. Under Texas law, Pasadena ISD must provide parents with at least five school days' written notice, and parents have the right to participate as equal members of the committee.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to Pasadena ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: ARD Meetings in Pasadena ISD

  • Notice Timeline: Minimum of 5 school days' written notice prior to the meeting.
  • Recording Rights: Parents may audio record any ARD meeting in Pasadena ISD.
  • Parental Role: Parents are equal, voting members of the ARD committee.
  • Annual Frequency: A Review ARD must be held at least once every 12 months.
  • Initial ARD: Must occur within 30 calendar days of a completed Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).

Common ARD Questions in Pasadena ISD

Can I request an ARD meeting at any time?

Yes. In Pasadena ISD, parents or school staff may request an ARD committee meeting at any point during the school year to address changes in a student's needs. The district typically responds to such requests by scheduling a meeting or providing a written explanation for refusal within a reasonable timeframe.

What happens if I disagree with the Pasadena ISD ARD committee?

If a consensus is not reached, Pasadena ISD must offer a 10-day recess to allow the committee to gather more data and attempt to reach an agreement. If disagreement persists after the reconvened meeting, the district must provide a Prior Written Notice (PWN) explaining their final decision and your rights to dispute resolution.

Who facilitates ARD meetings in Pasadena ISD?

Meetings are typically facilitated by a campus administrator (Principal or Assistant Principal) acting as the District Representative. These individuals have the authority to commit Pasadena ISD resources to ensure the implementation of the student's IEP.

How to Prepare for an ARD Meeting in Pasadena ISD

Follow these steps to ensure effective participation in the Pasadena ISD special education process.

1. Request Draft Documents

Contact your child’s special education teacher in Pasadena ISD to request draft IEP goals and any new evaluation data at least 3 business days before the meeting. Reviewing these ahead of time allows you to focus on high-level decisions during the actual meeting.

2. Prepare Your Parent Statement

Draft a brief summary of your child's strengths, your current concerns, and your vision for their academic year. This ensures your voice is documented in the "Parent Input" section of the Pasadena ISD ARD paperwork.

3. Organize Recent Private Data

If your child has received outside therapy or medical diagnoses from providers in the Houston area, bring copies of these reports. The Pasadena ISD committee must consider outside evaluations, though they are not legally required to adopt all recommendations.

How ARD Meetings in Pasadena ISD May Differ From Other Districts

Pasadena ISD is a large urban/suburban district in Harris County, which influences how ARD committees are structured compared to smaller rural districts:

  • Staffing Patterns: Pasadena ISD often utilizes a mix of Educational Diagnosticians and Licensed Specialists in School Psychology (LSSPs) to present evaluation data, depending on the disability category being discussed.
  • Meeting Format: While the district offers in-person meetings, virtual options (via platforms like Microsoft Teams) remain common for "Brief ARDs" or administrative updates to accommodate parent schedules.
  • Transition Focus: Because Pasadena ISD has robust career and technical education (CTE) programs, transition planning for students aged 14 and older often involves specific local vocational coordination.
