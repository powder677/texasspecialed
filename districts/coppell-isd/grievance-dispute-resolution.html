Quick Facts: Dispute Resolution in Coppell ISD

  • Internal Deadline: Level One grievances must be filed within 15 days.
  • State Deadline: TEA complaints have a one-year statute of limitations.
  • Contact: Stephanie Flores, Executive Director of Intervention Services (214-496-6955).
  • Section 504: For 504-specific disputes, contact Susann Roden at 214-496-6833.

Common Questions About Coppell ISD Dispute Resolution

How do I escalate a campus-level issue?

If informal discussions with teachers or principals do not resolve the issue, Coppell ISD requires a formal written Level One Grievance. This is typically routed to the Director of Student and Staff Services, who must schedule a conference within 10 district business days.

What if the district is not following the IEP?

Failure to provide services listed in the IEP is a violation of IDEA. Parents can file a TEA Special Education Complaint. TEA will investigate and, if a violation is found, may order Coppell ISD to provide compensatory services or corrective actions.

Dispute Resolution Escalation Path in Coppell ISD

Level 1

Campus Resolution

Meet with the ARD chair or principal. This is the fastest way to resolve service delivery or evaluation delays.

Level 2

District Grievance (Level I-IV)

File a formal complaint with the district office. If the Level One administrator denies relief, you have 10 days to appeal to the next level.

Level 3

State & Federal Action

File with TEA for legal violations or OCR for disability-based discrimination within 180 days.
