⚠️ Important Notice About Timelines

Many complaints have strict filing deadlines. TEA complaints must be filed within one year of the alleged violation. District grievances (Policy FNG) often have even shorter deadlines, typically requiring a Level One filing within 10-15 business days of the incident or 10 days of a response. Don't delay if you believe your child's rights have been violated.

Filing Complaints & Resolving Disputes with Coppell ISD Special Education

A comprehensive guide to your options when you disagree with Coppell ISD about your child's special education services, evaluation, or IEP.

When Should You Consider Filing a Complaint?

Services Not Being Provided

Your child's IEP lists specific services (speech therapy, resource support, etc.) but Coppell ISD is not providing them as written.

Evaluation Delays

Coppell ISD has missed the 60-day evaluation timeline or refused to evaluate without valid justification.

ARD Meeting Issues

Coppell ISD refuses to hold an ARD meeting, excludes you from decisions, or won't consider your input.

Placement Disagreements

You believe your child needs a different educational setting than what Coppell ISD is offering.

Discrimination Concerns

You believe your child is being treated differently because of their disability, including denial of accommodations.

Retaliation

You feel Coppell ISD is retaliating against you or your child because you advocated for their rights.

Before Filing a Formal Complaint:

In many cases, informal resolution is faster and preserves relationships. Consider:

  • Discussing concerns directly with your child's teacher or staff member.
  • Requesting a meeting with the campus principal.
  • Contacting the Executive Director of Intervention Services, Stephanie Flores, at 214-496-6955.
  • Requesting an ARD meeting to address concerns formally.

Document all communication attempts. If informal resolution doesn't work, proceed with formal processes below.

Levels of Dispute Resolution in Texas

Texas provides multiple pathways for resolving special education disputes. Here's the hierarchy from informal to formal:

Level 1

District Grievance Process

Best for: Local campus issues, communication problems, or policy implementation concerns.

Timeline: Typically 10-15 business days per level for filing or response.

Cost: Free.

Binding: No - either party can escalate up to the Board of Trustees.

Learn about Coppell ISD's grievance process ↓
Level 2

TEA Special Education Complaint

Best for: Violations of IDEA, state special education rules, or procedural errors.

Timeline: Must be filed within 1 year of violation; TEA investigates within 60 days.

Cost: Free.

Binding: Yes - if TEA finds a violation, the district must comply with corrective actions.

Learn about TEA complaints ↓
Level 3

Special Education Mediation

Best for: When both parties want to negotiate with a neutral third party.

Timeline: Voluntary; typically scheduled within 2-4 weeks if both parties agree.

Cost: Free (TEA provides and pays the mediator).

Binding: Yes - only if a written agreement is signed by both parties.

Request mediation through TEA: TEA Mediation.

Level 4

Due Process Hearing

Best for: Serious disputes about eligibility, placement, or FAPE (Free Appropriate Public Education).

Timeline: Must be filed within 1 year (state) or 2 years (federal IDEA); hearing within 45 days.

Cost: May require an attorney; each party generally pays their own fees.

Binding: Yes - the hearing officer's decision is legally binding.

Learn about due process ↓
Level 5

Office for Civil Rights (OCR) Complaint

Best for: Discrimination based on disability (Section 504 or ADA violations).

Timeline: Must be filed within 180 days of the alleged discrimination.

Cost: Free.

Binding: Yes - if OCR finds a violation, the district must comply.

Learn about OCR complaints ↓

The Coppell ISD Local Grievance Process

Coppell ISD follows a structured multi-level grievance process for parent and student concerns under Board Policy FNG(Local).

Level One: Initial Grievance

Submit a formal written Level One Grievance/Complaint form to the Director of Student and Staff Services (currently Annelise Ford). A conference will be scheduled, and a written response provided within 10 district business days.

Level Two: Administrative Appeal

If not satisfied with the Level One response, you must file a Level Two appeal within 10 days of the written response.

Level Three: Further Appeal

A Level Three appeal may be filed within 10 days of the Level Two response if relief is not granted.

Level Four: Board of Trustees

The final district level is an appeal to the Coppell ISD Board of Trustees, which must be filed within 10 days of the Level Three response.

Filing a TEA Special Education Complaint

A state complaint is a written allegation that Coppell ISD has violated a requirement of the IDEA or state special education law.

Requirements for Filing:

  • Must be in writing and signed by the complainant.
  • Include specific violations and the facts supporting those violations.
  • Must be sent to the Texas Education Agency AND a copy sent to Coppell ISD.
  • Allegations must have occurred within one calendar year of the filing date.

TEA Complaints Unit: spedcomplaints@tea.texas.gov | Fax: 512-463-9560.

Due Process Hearings

Due process is a formal legal proceeding used for serious disagreements over identification, evaluation, or educational placement.

Resolution Meeting

After a due process request is filed, a resolution meeting must occur within 15 calendar days unless both parties waive it. This is a final opportunity to resolve the issue before a hearing.

Stay Put (Pendency)

Once a due process complaint is filed, the student generally remains in their current educational placement until the legal proceedings are final.

Office for Civil Rights (OCR) Complaints

Complaints regarding discrimination based on disability or Section 504 violations can be filed with the federal Office for Civil Rights.

Typical OCR complaints include:

  • Denial of equal access to programs or activities.
  • Disability-based harassment or bullying.
  • Failure to provide Section 504 accommodations or accessibility.

In Coppell ISD, Section 504 inquiries can also be directed to Susann Roden, Section 504 Specialist, at 214-496-6833.

Documentation: Your Most Important Tool

Success in any dispute resolution process relies on evidence. Maintain an organized file including:

Communication Logs: Keep a record of all emails, phone calls, and meetings with district staff.
Prior Written Notice (PWN): Any document where the district explains why they are refusing or initiating a change.
Meeting Minutes: Ensure ARD committee notes accurately reflect your requests and any district denials.
Student Data: Work samples, progress reports, and outside evaluation reports.
