About Coppell ISD Intervention Services

Coppell ISD operates a decentralized special education model where initial support and Child Find activities are triggered at individual campuses. Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must identify and evaluate all children with suspected disabilities within its boundaries.

A distinctive feature of CISD is the Intervention Services umbrella, which integrates Special Education and Section 504 services. While the district emphasizes inclusive practices for its 11.2% special education population, complex placement decisions for specialized programs—such as the ECSE (Early Childhood Special Education) or vocational transition labs—are coordinated through the Vonita White Administration Building staff.

Dispute Resolution: Under Board Policy FNG(LOCAL), Coppell ISD parents have a specific legal pathway to resolve disputes. If a disagreement occurs during an ARD meeting, families should first engage the campus-level coordinator before filing a formal grievance with Dr. Stephanie Flores' office.

Understanding Coppell ISD Board Policies

When advocating for your child in Coppell ISD, it is critical to cite the specific Board Policy codes that govern special education services.

  • Evaluations (FIE): Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must provide a Full and Individual Evaluation within 45 school days of receiving your signed written consent.
  • Dispute Resolution: If you disagree with an ARD committee decision, Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) outlines the formal grievance process, starting with a Level One conference with your campus principal.
  • Parent Participation: Board Policy EHBAB(LEGAL) guarantees your right to participate in ARD committee meetings and review all educational records.
