Navigating Special Education in Coppell ISD

Coppell ISD operates a decentralized special education model where initial support and Child Find activities are triggered at individual campuses. Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must identify and evaluate all children with suspected disabilities within its boundaries. Families at Coppell High School or Victory Place must submit written requests for evaluation directly to the campus principal to initiate the legal timeline.

A distinctive feature of CISD is the Intervention Services umbrella, which integrates Special Education and Section 504 services. While the district emphasizes inclusive practices for its 11.2% special education population, complex placement decisions for specialized programs—such as the ECSE (Early Childhood Special Education) or vocational transition labs—are coordinated through the Vonita White Administration Building staff.

Under Board Policy FNG(LOCAL), Coppell ISD parents have a specific legal pathway to resolve disputes. If a disagreement occurs during an ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) meeting, families should first engage the campus-level coordinator before filing a formal grievance with Dr. Stephanie Flores' office. Decisions must remain aligned with the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS) while providing the necessary modifications required by the student's Individualized Education Program (IEP).
