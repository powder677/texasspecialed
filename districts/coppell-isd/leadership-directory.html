Coppell ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Coppell ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators in the Dallas metro area.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

The Coppell ISD Special Education Department is led by administrative professionals dedicated to ensuring that every student receives a Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE) in the least restrictive environment. The leadership team oversees programming across all campuses in the Dallas-Fort Worth region, managing compliance with both federal IDEA requirements and Texas-specific education codes.

Key Coordinators

While the Executive Director oversees the entire department, most day-to-day program decisions and specialized support are managed by coordinators. In Coppell ISD, these specialists provide the technical expertise necessary to support diverse learning needs, from early childhood through post-secondary transition. They are the bridge between the central administration and the individual ARD committees at your child's school.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Handles dyslexia evaluations and services under HB 3928, ensuring compliance with the updated Texas Dyslexia Handbook. If your child is struggling with reading or writing, this coordinator ensures the district follows the "Specially Designed Instruction" model now required for dyslexia in Texas.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Supports students with autism spectrum disorders, providing guidance on behavioral strategies and classroom environment. They work closely with teachers to implement evidence-based practices and behavioral intervention plans (BIPs).

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages initial and reevaluation requests, overseeing the Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) timeline. They ensure that the district meets the strict 45-school-day timeline for evaluations following parent consent.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

Handles IEP/ARD records requests and transfers for students moving into or out of Coppell ISD. This is your primary contact when you need a complete file for a private therapist or out-of-district move.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the main special education office at 214-496-6000 for referral to the appropriate person. Many positions are managed by program area rather than by campus.

Who to Contact When

In Texas, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process is the vehicle for all special education decisions. Use this guide to determine the best point of contact for your specific situation in Coppell ISD. Navigating a large district can be overwhelming, so starting at the right level saves time and reduces stress for families.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

When you suspect your child has a disability and needs support, you should submit a written request for evaluation. Once submitted to the administration, the district has 15 school days to provide you with a Prior Written Notice and a chance to provide consent.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Your child's ARD case manager or campus special education coordinator

For student-specific questions regarding accommodations or progress, your child's campus is always the first stop. The Diagnostician or Speech Pathologist assigned to your campus acts as the primary facilitator for these meetings.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

If you are moving or need records for a private doctor, contact the district's special education records office directly to request your child's file. Under FERPA, the district must provide these records, though they may charge a small fee for copies.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If issues cannot be resolved at the campus level with the Principal or Diagnostician, the Executive Director is the next level of oversight. They can help mediate disputes regarding services, placement, or implementation of the IEP.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

Under Texas HB 3928, dyslexia is now evaluated through special education. If your child was previously under Section 504 and you have questions about the transition to specialized instruction and the required "member with dyslexia expertise" in the ARD, contact this office.

Office Locations

The administrative offices for Special Education are centrally located to serve the entire Coppell ISD community. This is where the leadership team and coordinators are based, though they frequently visit campuses throughout the district.
