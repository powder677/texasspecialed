Tips for Effective Communication

Navigating a large district requires a structured approach to ensure your student's needs are documented and addressed.

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use clear subject lines: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]".
  • Include your child's full name and campus in every email.
  • Request written confirmation for verbal agreements made during phone calls.

📞 Communication Protocol

  • Always start at the campus level with the teacher or case manager.
  • Submit all formal evaluation requests in writing to trigger legal 15-day notice periods.
  • Follow the "Chain of Command": Campus Staff → Principal → District Coordinator → Executive Director.
