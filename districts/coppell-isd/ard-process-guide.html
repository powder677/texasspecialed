Answer: In **Coppell ISD**, an ARD meeting is the legally required Texas special education meeting where parents and district staff determine eligibility, services, and placement. Parents must receive at least **five school days’ written notice**, have the right to **audio record** the meeting, and are recognized as **equal members** of the committee under Texas law.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to Coppell ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: ARD Meetings in Coppell ISD

  • Notice Timeline: Coppell ISD must provide written notice at least 5 school days before the meeting.
  • Recording Rights: You have the legal right to audio record your ARD meeting in Texas.
  • Parent Role: You are an equal member of the committee with final say on consent for initial services.
  • Annual Requirement: The committee must meet at least once per year to review and update your child's program.
  • Meeting Request: You can request an ARD at any time, and the district must respond within 5 school days.

Common ARD Questions

How do I request an ARD meeting in Coppell ISD?

Parents may request an ARD committee meeting at any time to discuss educational concerns, placement, or services. In Coppell ISD, requests should be made in writing to a district administrative staff member.

What happens if I disagree with the ARD committee?

If mutual agreement cannot be reached, the meeting is "recessed" and will reconvene within 10 school days to try and reach a compromise. You have the right to sign "In Disagreement" and pursue mediation or a due process hearing.

Can I bring an advocate to my Coppell ISD meeting?

Yes, you have the legal right to bring an advocate, attorney, or any individual with knowledge regarding your child. Coppell ISD typically requests 48-72 hours notice if an attorney will be attending.

How to Prepare for an ARD Meeting

  1. Review Records: Examine the current IEP, progress reports, and the most recent Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).
  2. Draft Parent Statement: Write a summary of your child's strengths and your primary concerns to be included in the IEP.
  3. Request Draft IEP: Ask the teacher for a draft copy of proposed goals at least one week before the meeting.
  4. Finalize Priorities: Identify your "must-have" accommodations or services before the discussion begins.
  5. Confirm Your Team: Ensure any outside providers or advocates are confirmed to attend with you.

How ARD Meetings in Coppell ISD May Differ From Other Districts

Coppell ISD emphasizes a "consensus" model where teams work toward collaborative agreement rather than voting. Meetings are typically held at the student's home campus, though the district offers virtual options via Zoom or Microsoft Teams. For students age 14 and older, Coppell ISD specifically includes the student in discussions regarding transition services and post-secondary goals. Additionally, the district uses a specific **Post-ARD Survey** to collect parent feedback on the committee process.
