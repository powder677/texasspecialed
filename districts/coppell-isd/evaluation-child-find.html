Quick Facts: Evaluations in Coppell ISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full Individual Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, legal guardians, or anyone concerned about a child.
  • Timeline: 15 school days to respond; 45 school days to evaluate after consent.
  • Cost: Provided at no cost to parents by Coppell ISD.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting within 30 calendar days of report completion.

Common Questions About Coppell ISD Evaluations

How do I request a special education evaluation in Coppell ISD?

Submit your request in writing. You should address your request to a district administrative staff member or use the dedicated Child Find email. A formal letter ensures legal timelines are triggered immediately.

Sample request language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], date of birth [DOB], currently residing in Coppell ISD."

How long does the evaluation process take in Coppell ISD?

45 school days for testing. Once the district receives your signed consent, they have 45 school days to complete the FIIE report. Before this starts, the district has 15 school days to respond to your initial written request.

What tests are included in a Coppell ISD evaluation?

Comprehensive multidisciplinary assessment. Testing covers academic, developmental, functional performance, and cognitive functioning. Specialized assessments for Speech, OT, and PT are included if there is a suspected need.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. Coppell ISD must evaluate based on "suspected disability" that impacts education. You do not need a medical diagnosis from a doctor to start the process.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) in Coppell ISD?

Yes, if you disagree with the district results. You have the right to request an IEE at public expense if you believe the school's evaluation was incomplete or inaccurate.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

Initial ARD Meeting. The ARD committee must meet within 30 calendar days of the report date to determine eligibility. If eligible, an Individualized Education Program (IEP) is developed.

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

A FIIE (often shortened to FIE) is a comprehensive district assessment to determine eligibility under IDEA. It aims to confirm:

  • Disability Presence: Does the child meet criteria for one of the 13 federal categories?
  • Educational Impact: Does the disability affect school performance?
  • Service Need: Does the child require specially designed instruction?

13 Disability Categories in Texas Special Education

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment Specific Learning Disability Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment

Under Texas HB 3928, all dyslexia evaluations in Coppell ISD now occur through this special education framework.

How to Request an Evaluation in Coppell ISD

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Address your request to:

Contact: Stephanie Flores, Executive Director of Intervention Services

Email: childfind@coppellisd.com

Address: 200 S. Denton Tap Road, Coppell, TX 75019

Sample Evaluation Request Letter

[Date]

Stephanie Flores Executive Director of Intervention Services Coppell ISD

Dear Ms. Flores,

I am writing to formally request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child, [Child's Full Name], DOB [Date]. I am concerned about [Briefly list concerns: reading, behavior, etc.].

I look forward to receiving the Prior Written Notice or consent forms within 15 school days as required by Texas law.

Sincerely, [Your Name]

Step 2: 15-Day Response

Coppell ISD must respond within 15 school days with Prior Written Notice. They will either propose to evaluate and provide consent forms or explain why they refuse.

Step 3: Signed Consent

Evaluation cannot start without your signed, informed consent. Once received, the 45-school-day clock begins.

Step 4: ARD Meeting

After the report is finished, the committee meets within 30 calendar days to review results and determine if your child qualifies for special education.

What Tests Are Included?

Cognitive & Psychological

Assessments of IQ and functional performance conducted by LSSPs or diagnosticians.

Speech & Therapy

Evaluations for speech-language pathology, OT, or PT as suspected.

Dyslexia

Evaluations now follow IDEA procedures under HB 3928.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with the district's findings, you can request an IEE at public expense. This ensures an outside professional reviews your child's needs independently.

Early Childhood (Birth to Age 5)

For children birth to 3, contact Early Childhood Intervention (ECI) at 971-490-9055 (Dallas County) or 817-446-8000 (Denton County). For ages 3-5, contact the Coppell ISD Child Find office directly.
