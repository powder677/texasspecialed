How to Request a Special Education Evaluation in Lovejoy ISD

A comprehensive guide for families in Allen and Fairview to navigate the 15-45-30 school day evaluation timeline.

Child Find & Your Rights

Lovejoy ISD (LISD) has a federal "Child Find" mandate to identify, locate, and evaluate all children with suspected disabilities. This includes children from birth through age 21, regardless of whether they are in public school, private school, or homeschooled.

Core Rights Under Texas Law:

  • Written Requests: Parents may request a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) in writing at any time.
  • Response Window: The district must provide "Prior Written Notice" within 15 school days.
  • Qualified Professionals: Children must be assessed by specialists such as LSSPs or Diagnosticians.
  • Disagreement Rights: If you disagree with the district's report, you may request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at no cost to you.

Medical Diagnosis vs. Educational Eligibility

You do not need an outside medical diagnosis to start the process. While external reports are reviewed, Lovejoy ISD must perform its own assessment to determine if a student meets Texas eligibility criteria for special education services.

1

Submit a Written Request

Verbal requests do not officially start the legal clock; always submit a written request to ensure timelines are enforced.

2

The 15-Day Response

Lovejoy ISD has 15 school days from your request to provide one of the following:

  • Notice of Evaluation: Consent forms if they agree to test.
  • Prior Written Notice: A legal explanation if they refuse to evaluate.
  • Procedural Safeguards: Your manual of legal rights.
3

Sign Informed Consent

The 45-school-day timeline only begins once the district receives your signed consent form.

Expert Tip: Review the consent form carefully to ensure it covers every area of concern, including behavior, speech, or dyslexia.
4

The Comprehensive Evaluation (FIIE)

The district assesses the child in all areas of suspected disability. This includes:

  • Psychological and behavior assessments.
  • Academic and cognitive (IQ) testing.
  • Speech and language evaluations.
  • Dyslexia Testing: Per HB 3928, dyslexia must now be evaluated through the special education process as a specific learning disability.
5

Initial ARD Meeting

Within 30 calendar days of the report being completed, Lovejoy ISD must hold an Initial ARD meeting. The committee determines eligibility and, if the child qualifies, develops the IEP.

Absences & Summer Deadlines

If a student is absent 3 or more days, the 45-day deadline may be extended. If consent is signed 35-45 days before the final school day, the report is due by June 30th.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can RTI be used to delay an evaluation?

No. Districts cannot use Response to Intervention (RTI) to delay or deny testing for a student suspected of a disability.

How does HB 3928 affect dyslexia in Lovejoy ISD?

Dyslexia is treated as a specific learning disability under IDEA. Evaluations and specialized instruction (like Take Flight) are managed through the special education framework.

What about private school students?

LISD must evaluate children in private schools within district boundaries. Contact the Special Education department for referral protocols.
