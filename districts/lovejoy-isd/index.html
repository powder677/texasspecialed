Navigating the Lovejoy ISD Special Education Model

For families in Lucas and Fairview, Lovejoy ISD offers a small-district advantage where the Individualized Education Program (IEP) process is highly collaborative. Unlike larger neighboring districts, Lovejoy’s special education staff, based primarily at the 259 Country Club Road administrative hub, work closely with campus principals at Lovejoy High School and Sloan Creek Intermediate to ensure service delivery is consistent.

Under Board Policy EHBAA(LEGAL), Lovejoy ISD is required to identify and evaluate students with suspected disabilities. If you are requesting an initial evaluation, the district follows the Texas-mandated 45-school-day timeline once written consent is signed. For students already receiving services, the Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) committee meetings are the primary vehicle for decision-making. These meetings utilize data from MAP (Measures of Academic Progress) and STAAR performance to adjust accommodations and goals.

Specialized Programs & Services

Lovejoy ISD provides a full continuum of placements, including:

  • Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE): Located at Puster Elementary and Hart Elementary for children ages 3-5.
  • Social-Emotional Support: Targeted interventions for students with behavioral needs, overseen by licensed specialists in school psychology (LSSPs).
  • Secondary Transition: Lovejoy High School offers vocational training and "Lovejoy Life" skills programs to meet federal transition requirements under Policy EHBA(LEGAL).

If a dispute arises during an ARD meeting, parents are encouraged to follow the local grievance process outlined in Board Policy FNG(LOCAL). This process typically begins with the campus principal before escalating to Director Sacy Sibley and finally the Superintendent’s office.
