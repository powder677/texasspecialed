Navigating the Lovejoy ISD Special Education Model

For families in Lucas and Fairview, Lovejoy ISD offers a small-district advantage where the Individualized Education Program (IEP) process is highly collaborative. Unlike larger neighboring districts, Lovejoy’s special education staff, based primarily at the 259 Country Club Road administrative hub, work closely with campus principals at Lovejoy High School and Sloan Creek Intermediate to ensure high-fidelity service delivery.

Under Board Policy EHBAA(LEGAL), Lovejoy ISD must complete initial evaluations within 45 school days of receiving written parental consent. For students already receiving services, the Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) committee meetings utilize data from MAP (Measures of Academic Progress) and STAAR performance to adjust accommodations and goals.

Specialized Programs & Services

Lovejoy ISD provides a full continuum of placements, including:

  • Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE): Centralized at Puster Elementary and Hart Elementary.
  • Social-Emotional Support: Targeted interventions overseen by district LSSPs at Willow Springs Middle School.
  • Lovejoy Life Skills: Secondary transition programs at Lovejoy High School meeting requirements under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL).

If a dispute arises, parents should refer to the local grievance process outlined in Board Policy FNG(LOCAL), starting with the campus principal before escalating to Director Sacy Sibley.
