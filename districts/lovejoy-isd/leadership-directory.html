Lovejoy ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Lovejoy ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators in the Allen/Lucas area.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

Lovejoy ISD is known for its small-district feel and high academic standards. The Special Education Department focuses on providing personalized support to ensure students with disabilities can thrive within the "Lovejoy Way." Leadership here oversees a range of services including specialized instruction, speech therapy, and occupational therapy.

Key Coordinators

In Lovejoy ISD, coordinators play a vital role in ensuring that campus-level staff have the training and resources needed to support students. Because Lovejoy is a smaller district compared to its neighbors, these coordinators often work very closely with campus diagnosticians and parents to find creative solutions for complex student needs.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Following the requirements of HB 3928, the Dyslexia Coordinator ensures that students with dyslexia receive appropriate "Specially Designed Instruction." They manage the transition of many students from 504 plans into Special Education where necessary.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

The specialist provides support for students on the spectrum, focusing on social skills development, sensory needs, and communication strategies within the general education and specialized settings.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Oversees the Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) process. If you have signed consent for an evaluation, this office tracks the strict Texas timelines to ensure your child's eligibility is determined promptly.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

The go-to contact for moving families. Whether you are moving into the Allen/Lucas area or heading elsewhere, the Records Clerk ensures that IEPs and evaluation data are transferred securely and quickly.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the Lovejoy ISD Administration Building at 469-742-8000 to be directed to the correct department specialist.

Who to Contact When

Understanding the hierarchy of a school district helps parents advocate more effectively. In Lovejoy ISD, the district encourages open communication starting at the campus level before escalating concerns to the central administration.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you suspect your child has a learning disability or other health impairment, submit your request for a formal evaluation in writing to the special education office or your school principal.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Campus Diagnostician or Case Manager

Your child's specific campus is the primary site for ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) meetings. For questions about daily accommodations or schedule changes, start with your child's teacher or the campus diagnostician.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: District Records Clerk

To obtain a full copy of your child’s educational record for private medical providers or insurance, a formal request to the central office is typically the fastest route.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director of Special Education

If you feel the ARD committee is not following the IEP or if a disagreement cannot be settled at the school building, the Executive Director provides the next level of administrative review.
