Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Documentation

  • Keep a log of all phone calls and save every email correspondence.
  • Use clear subject lines including your child's name and campus.
  • Request written summaries of any verbal agreements made during meetings.

📝 Chain of Command

  • Always start communication at the campus level with the teacher or diagnostician.
  • If an issue remains unresolved, involve the campus principal before the district office.
  • The district encourages open communication within the "Lovejoy Way".
