⚠️ Important Notice About Timelines

Many complaints have strict filing deadlines. TEA complaints must be filed within one year of the alleged violation. District grievances (Policy FNG) in Lovejoy ISD typically require a Level One filing within 15 business days of the incident. Don't delay if you believe your child's rights have been violated.

File a Special Education Complaint in Lovejoy ISD

Lovejoy ISD serves approximately 4,000-4,500 students in the Lucas and Fairview areas of North Metro Dallas. Understand your local escalation process, state-level rights, and how to access mediation or due process hearings.

Quick Answer: To file a special education complaint in Lovejoy ISD, start with your campus ARD chair or principal to attempt informal resolution. If the issue remains unresolved, you must submit a formal written grievance under Board Policy FNG (Local) within 15 business days. You may also file directly with the Texas Education Agency (TEA) or request free mediation. Lovejoy ISD handles these disputes through a three-level administrative process, starting with campus-level administrators and potentially escalating to the Board of Trustees.

Common Questions About Lovejoy ISD Dispute Resolution

How do I file a special education complaint in Lovejoy ISD?

Start at campus level. Contact your child's ARD committee chair or principal first. If the issue is not resolved, submit a formal written grievance to the campus administrator. Lovejoy ISD follows Policy FNG (Local), requiring a conference within 10 days of your submission to discuss the issue. The district provides a written decision within 20 days.

What is the difference between a district complaint and a TEA complaint?

District complaints are handled internally; TEA complaints trigger state investigation. A district complaint follows the Lovejoy ISD Board of Trustees' three-level grievance path. A TEA (Texas Education Agency) complaint is a formal state-level investigation into legal violations, which must be filed within one year. Lovejoy ISD parents are encouraged to use informal local routes first, but state filing is available at any time.

What is mediation and how do I request it in Lovejoy ISD?

Mediation is a neutral compromise tool. It is a voluntary process where a TEA-provided neutral third party helps parents and Lovejoy ISD reach a mutual agreement at no cost to the parents. In Lovejoy ISD, this is often scheduled within 2-4 weeks if both parties agree to participate.

Dispute Resolution Escalation Path in Lovejoy ISD

Follow this recommended sequence to resolve special education disputes in Lucas and Fairview schools:

Level 1

Campus-Level Resolution (Start Here)

Contact: Teacher, Case Manager, or Campus Principal.

Contact for SpEd: Allison Claunch, Director of Special Education.

Timeline: Request meeting within 5-10 days of the issue.

Level 2

Formal District Grievance (FNG Local)

Method: Submit written grievance to the campus principal.

Timeline: Must file within 15 business days.

Outcome: A conference within 10 days; decision within 20 days.

Level 3

TEA Complaint or Mediation

TEA File: spedcomplaints@tea.texas.gov.

Statute of Limitations: 1 year for TEA; 180 days for OCR.

Protection Against Retaliation

Parents who advocate for their children in Lovejoy ISD are protected by federal law.

  • ✓ Lovejoy ISD cannot refuse services because you filed a complaint.
  • ✓ School staff cannot treat your child negatively due to your advocacy.
  • ✓ District must address complaints objectively regardless of history.
