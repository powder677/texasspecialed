Answer: In Plano ISD, a special education evaluation is a comprehensive assessment conducted at no cost to parents to determine if a student has a disability and requires specialized instruction. The process begins once the district receives written parental consent, triggering a 45-school-day timeline for completion, followed by an ARD meeting within 30 calendar days to determine eligibility. Parents in Plano ISD may request this evaluation at any time if they suspect their child has a disability.

This page describes evaluation procedures specific to Plano ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: Evaluation Timelines in Plano ISD

  • Response to Request: Plano ISD must respond in writing to a parent's request for evaluation within 15 school days.
  • Evaluation Completion: The district has 45 school days to complete the Full Individual Evaluation (FIE) after receiving signed consent.
  • Eligibility Meeting (ARD): An ARD meeting must be held within 30 calendar days after the written report is completed.
  • Absence Extension: If a student is absent 3 or more days during the evaluation period, the 45-day deadline may be extended by those absence days.

The Plano ISD Evaluation Process

Step 1: Initiation and Consent

To begin the process, submit a written request to the school principal. If Plano ISD agrees to evaluate, they will provide a "Prior Written Notice" and a "Consent to Evaluate" form. Evaluation cannot begin until this consent is signed and returned.

Step 2: The Full Individual Evaluation (FIE)

Qualified professionals, such as educational diagnosticians or Licensed Specialists in School Psychology (LSSP), will assess the student in all areas of suspected disability. Assessments are administered in the student's native language and cover areas such as academic achievement, cognitive functioning, and social-emotional development.

Step 3: The Eligibility ARD Meeting

Once the report is finished, the ARD committee (including the parents) meets to review the results. The committee determines if the child meets Texas criteria for a disability category and requires specialized instruction (Special Education) to make progress.

How Evaluation in Plano ISD May Differ From Other Districts

Plano ISD utilizes specific local resources and structures for their special education assessments:

  • "Lift Off to Learning" Help Line: Plano ISD provides a dedicated line (469-752-4299) for families with developmental or academic concerns for children not yet enrolled.
  • Student Support Team (SST) Integration: For enrolled students, evaluation requests often trigger a meeting of the campus SST to review current interventions and data before a formal referral is finalized.
  • Specialized Audiological Support: Unlike smaller districts that contract out, Plano ISD employs staff audiologists to assist in evaluations for students with suspected hearing impairments.
