Quick Facts: Evaluations in Round Rock ISD

  • Timeline: 15 school days to respond; 45 school days to evaluate; 30 calendar days for the ARD meeting.
  • Who can request? Parents or guardians have the right to request an evaluation at any time.
  • Cost: Evaluations are provided at public expense (free to parents).
  • Child Find: The district must identify and evaluate any child suspected of having a disability.

Common Questions About Round Rock ISD Evaluations

How do I request a special education evaluation in Round Rock ISD?

Submit a written request to your child's campus principal. If the child is preschool-age and not yet enrolled, address the letter to the District's Special Education Director.

Sample language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], date of birth [DOB], currently enrolled at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Round Rock ISD?

The "15-45-30" rule. The district has 15 school days to respond, 45 school days to conduct testing after receiving signed consent, and 30 calendar days to hold an ARD meeting to review results.

What tests are included in a Round Rock ISD evaluation?

A comprehensive FIIE. The district evaluates in all areas of suspected disability, which may include academic achievement, cognitive (IQ) testing, speech-language, and behavioral assessments.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. Under federal Child Find law, RRISD must evaluate any child suspected of having a disability based on educational performance, regardless of a medical diagnosis.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE)?

Yes. If you disagree with the district's evaluation, you have the right to request an IEE conducted by a qualified outside professional at district expense.

What happens if the school refuses to evaluate?

Prior Written Notice. If the district refuses your request, they must provide a formal notice explaining why they do not suspect a disability and provide a copy of your Procedural Safeguards.

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

An FIIE is a comprehensive assessment that determines if your child has a disability and identifies their specific educational needs. It covers 13 disability categories recognized in Texas.

13 Disability Categories in Texas Special Education

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment Specific Learning Disability Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment

How to Request an Evaluation in Round Rock ISD

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Send a formal letter to your school's principal describing your academic, social, or behavioral concerns.

Step 2: Consent and Response (15 School Days)

RRISD has 15 school days to respond in writing. If they agree to evaluate, they will provide a Consent for Evaluation form.

Step 3: Testing Window (45 School Days)

Once you sign and return the consent form, the district has 45 school days to conduct the evaluation. Note: If your child is absent for 3+ days, the timeline may be extended.

Step 4: ARD Meeting (30 Calendar Days)

The ARD committee must meet within 30 calendar days of the evaluation report's completion to review results and determine eligibility.

End-of-Year Evaluation Requests

If consent is signed late in the school year, special rules apply:

  • 35-45 School Days before year-end: Evaluation must be completed by June 30th.
  • Fewer than 35 School Days: The process may carry over into the next school year.
