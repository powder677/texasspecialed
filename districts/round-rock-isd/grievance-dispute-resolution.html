Quick Answer: To file a special education complaint in Round Rock ISD, start with your campus ARD chair or principal. If unresolved, escalate to the district level by submitting the "Level One Student/Parent Complaint Form" to the Superintendent's office under Board Policy FNG (LOCAL). You can also file directly with the Texas Education Agency (TEA) or request mediation. Round Rock ISD, serving families in the North Austin/Round Rock area, requires district-level grievances to be filed within 15 district business days of the incident and offers support through the Special Education Department at (512) 464-5140.

⚠️ Important Notice About Timelines

TEA complaints must be filed within one year of the alleged violation. Local district grievances in Round Rock ISD typically must be filed within 15 district business days of the time you knew or should have known of the issue.

Quick Facts: Dispute Resolution in Round Rock ISD

  • Your Rights: You can disagree with ARD decisions without retaliation.
  • Start Here: Informal resolution with the campus principal or department chair.
  • Escalate To: Formal Level 1 Grievance (FNG LOCAL).
  • State Option: File a TEA complaint within 1 year of the incident.
  • Legal Option: Due process hearing (legal counsel highly recommended).

Common Questions About Round Rock ISD Dispute Resolution

How do I file a special education complaint in Round Rock ISD?

Start at the campus level. Contact your child's ARD committee chair or campus principal first. If unresolved within 10-15 business days, escalate by submitting a Level One form to the Administration Building at 1311 Round Rock Avenue. Round Rock ISD requires a date-stamped receipt or certified mail to track these formal submissions.

What is the difference between a district complaint and a TEA complaint?

District complaints are handled internally; TEA complaints trigger state investigation. A district complaint follows RRISD board policy and is heard by district leadership. A TEA complaint is a formal investigation with a 60-day timeline. Parents are encouraged to contact the RRISD Special Education office at (512) 464-5140 to resolve issues locally before involving the state.

Escalation Path in Round Rock ISD

Level 1

Campus-Level Resolution

Contact your child's ARD chair or campus principal. Most issues regarding service minutes or IEP implementation can be resolved here through an informal staffing or reconvene ARD.

Level 2

District-Level Complaint

Submit to: Office of the Superintendent / Grievance Officer

Address: 1311 Round Rock Avenue, Round Rock, TX 78681

Phone: (512) 464-5000

Must be filed within 15 district business days using the Level One Student/Parent Complaint Form.
