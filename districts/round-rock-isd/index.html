Understanding Round Rock ISD Special Education Rights

When advocating for your child in Round Rock ISD, citing the specific board policy ensures your requests are handled with priority at the Lillie Delgado Administration Building.

  • Initial Evaluations: Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), Round Rock ISD must complete the Full Individual Evaluation (FIE) within 45 school days of receiving your signed consent.
  • Dispute Resolution: If consensus is not reached in an ARD, Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) outlines your right to a Level One conference with the campus principal and subsequent appeals.
  • Cameras in Classrooms: Parents may request video surveillance in self-contained units at campuses like McNeil or Westwood High under Board Policy EHBAF(LEGAL).
