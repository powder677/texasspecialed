About Round Rock ISD Special Education

Round Rock Independent School District (RRISD) is a "Destination District" located in one of the fastest-growing regions of Texas. The district serves students from Round Rock, Austin, and Cedar Park. A standout feature of RRISD's approach to special education is the Family Resource Center, located just east of the main administration building. Here, the district employs dedicated Special Education Parent Liaisons whose specific job is to bridge the communication gap between families and school personnel.

The district benefits from a very active Special Education Parent Advisory Council (SEPAC). Unlike in some districts where parent groups are informal, the RRISD SEPAC works directly with the Executive Director of Special Education to advise on policy and training needs. They hold regular meetings and offer a support network that can be invaluable if you are feeling isolated in your advocacy journey.

For students with specific needs, Round Rock ISD offers specialized support ranging from Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) for ages 3-5 to 18+ Transition Services for young adults. The district has placed a strong emphasis on "Child Find" compliance, conducting community outreach to identify children who may need services before they even enroll in kindergarten. If you have concerns about your child's development and they are not yet school-aged, you can contact the Child Find office directly to initiate an evaluation.

Navigating the district's hierarchy is easier if you follow their established protocol. RRISD encourages parents to resolve issues at the lowest possible level, starting with the campus teacher and principal. However, if those avenues are exhausted, the Parent Liaisons are your best "next step" before filing a formal grievance or calling the central administration. They can often facilitate a meeting with the appropriate Program Director to resolve disputes informally.
