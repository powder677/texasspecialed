Round Rock ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Round Rock ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators serving the Austin metro area.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

Round Rock ISD serves a rapidly growing population of over 46,000 students across Round Rock, Austin, and Cedar Park. The special education leadership team provides the structural oversight necessary to manage thousands of Individualized Education Programs (IEPs) while ensuring compliance with state and federal laws.

Key Coordinators

While the Executive Director oversees the entire department, most day-to-day program decisions and specialized support are managed by coordinators. In Round Rock ISD, these specialists bridge the gap between district-wide policy and the practical needs of your child's classroom.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Handles dyslexia evaluations and services under HB 3928. They are responsible for ensuring that students with dyslexia receive proper evidence-based instruction within the special education framework.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Supports students with autism spectrum disorders. They provide consultation to teachers on behavioral strategies, visual schedules, and social communication supports required for student success.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages initial and reevaluation requests. This office oversees the diagnosticians and psychologists who perform the Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) to determine eligibility.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

Handles IEP/ARD records requests and transfers for students moving into or out of Round Rock ISD. If you are new to the Austin area, this is your first stop to ensure records reach your new campus.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the main special education office at 512-464-5000 for referral to the appropriate person.

Who to Contact When

In Texas, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process is the vehicle for all special education decisions. Use this guide to determine who to call first based on your current needs.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you suspect your child has a disability, you should submit a written request for evaluation. The district is legally required to respond within 15 school days to provide you with a notice of evaluation or a refusal.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Your child's ARD case manager or campus special education coordinator

Campus-level issues like daily goal tracking or specific accommodation concerns should always start at the school. Most Round Rock ISD campuses have a dedicated diagnostician on-site.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

When you need copies of your child's file for a private medical provider or for personal records, the district records clerk can assist with the formal request process.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If you cannot reach an agreement at the ARD table or through the campus principal, the Executive Director's office handles formal dispute resolution and compliance oversight.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

Under Texas HB 3928, dyslexia services are now integrated into special education. If your child is transitioning from a 504 plan to an IEP, contact the coordinator for guidance on this process.
