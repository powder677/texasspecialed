Who Should I Contact?

For ARD Meeting Questions

Start with your campus ARD committee chair (usually a Diagnostician or Speech Pathologist) or special education coordinator.

Campus-Level Support

RRISD operates a campus-based ARD model. This means the majority of daily decisions and IEP implementation questions are handled by staff located physically at your child's school.

Role Responsibility Who to Call
Diagnostician/LSSP Evaluations, IEP writing, ARD facilitation Campus Main Office
Case Manager Daily goal tracking, classroom accommodations Your Child's Teacher
Speech Pathologist Communication services and ARD chair (if SLI only) Campus SpEd Dept
Campus Principal Final authority on campus resources and staffing Campus Main Office

For a full list of campus phone numbers, visit the Round Rock ISD Directory.

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use clear subject lines: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Include your child's full name and DOB in all correspondence
  • Request written follow-up for all verbal agreements

📞 Phone Contact

  • Call during business hours: 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM
  • Leave detailed voicemails with your name and student's name
  • Follow up phone calls with an "as per our conversation" email

📝 Documentation

  • Save all emails in a dedicated folder or physical binder
  • Keep a log of phone calls including date, time, and person
  • Use certified mail for formal evaluation requests
