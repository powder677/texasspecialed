Navigating the Garland ISD Special Education System

Garland ISD (GISD) utilizes a centralized administration model located at the Harris Hill Administration Building, though operational support for secondary transition is housed at the Transition Learning Center on State Street. Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district is required to identify and evaluate students with suspected disabilities. To trigger the official evaluation timeline, parents should submit a formal written request to the Campus Principal or the Special Education Team Lead at their child's specific school.

GISD offers a continuum of placements including ALE (Applied Learning Environment) for functional skill development and FBE (Functional Behavior Environment) for students requiring intensive behavioral intervention. Decisions regarding these placements are made exclusively by the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) Committee. If parents disagree with a committee decision, Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) outlines the formal grievance process, which begins with the campus administrator before escalating to the Director of Special Education.

For students transitioning to adulthood, the Project SEARCH program at local business sites provides critical real-world experience. Additionally, families with children ages 3-5 can access Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) through a centralized assessment process. For any campus-level concerns, your primary ally is the Special Education Coordinator assigned to your specific zone, who ensures that IEP (Individualized Education Program) goals are implemented with fidelity across the district's 70+ campuses.
