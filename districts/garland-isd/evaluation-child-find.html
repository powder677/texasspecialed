How to Request a Special Education Evaluation in Garland ISD

A step-by-step guide to the special education evaluation process for families in Garland, Rowlett, and Sachse.

Child Find & Your Rights

Under federal and state law, Garland ISD has a "Child Find" obligation to identify, locate, and evaluate all children with disabilities (birth through age 21) within the district who may need special education. This includes children in private schools, those who are homeschooled, and highly mobile students.

You Have the Right To:

  • Request a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for your child in writing at any time.
  • Receive a response (Prior Written Notice) from the district within 15 school days of your written request.
  • Have your child evaluated in all areas related to the suspected disability.
  • Review all evaluation data and participate in the decision-making process.
  • Request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at public expense if you disagree with the district's results.

No Diagnosis Required

You do not need a medical diagnosis from a doctor to request an evaluation. Garland ISD evaluates based on "educational eligibility," determining if a disability exists and if it creates a need for specialized instruction.

Step-by-Step Evaluation Process in Garland ISD

1

Submit a Written Request

The evaluation process formally begins with a written request. If your child is enrolled in school, submit the request to the Campus Principal or the Educational Diagnostician. For children not yet in school (ages 3-5), contact the Special Education department directly.

2

The 15-Day Response Window

Within 15 school days of receiving your request, Garland ISD must respond. They will either provide you with a Notice of Evaluation and consent forms or a Prior Written Notice explaining their refusal to evaluate.

3

Provide Written Consent

The district cannot evaluate your child without your signed Written Consent. Signing consent does not mean you have agreed to services; it only authorizes the testing.

Timeline Trigger: The district has 45 school days from the day they receive your signed consent to complete the evaluation report.

Tip: Keep a record of the date you signed the consent form to track the 45-school-day deadline.
4

The Evaluation (FIIE)

Qualified Garland ISD staff, such as Educational Diagnosticians and School Psychologists (LSSPs), will evaluate your child. The assessment includes:

  • Intellectual and cognitive development.
  • Academic levels in reading, writing, and math.
  • Social, emotional, and behavioral health.
  • Dyslexia Screening: Following Texas HB 3928, dyslexia evaluations are now conducted through the special education process.
5

Initial ARD Committee Meeting

Within 30 calendar days of the report being completed, an ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) committee meeting is held. You are a required member of this committee. Together, you will review the data to determine eligibility and develop an IEP if services are needed.

Texas Timeline Requirements (15-45-30)

Garland ISD must follow the strict 15-45-30 school day sequence mandated by Texas law:

15 School Days

Written request to Consent or Refusal.

45 School Days

Signed consent to Completed Evaluation.

30 Calendar Days

Completed Evaluation to ARD Meeting.

What if Timelines are Missed?

If Garland ISD fails to meet these deadlines, they are in violation of state and federal law. Parents should:

  • Email the Campus Principal and Educational Diagnostician for an immediate status update.
  • Contact the Garland ISD Special Education Department at 972-487-3300.
  • Consult with SPEDTex at 1-855-773-3839 for advocacy support.

Frequently Asked Questions for Garland Families

How much does the evaluation cost?

Evaluations provided by Garland ISD are conducted at no cost to parents as part of the district's obligation to provide a Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE).

What if my child attends a private school in Garland?

Garland ISD is responsible for "Child Find" for all students within its boundaries, including those in private schools or who are homeschooled.

What is an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE)?

If you disagree with the district's evaluation, you have the right to request an IEE. This is an evaluation by an outside professional, paid for by Garland ISD.
