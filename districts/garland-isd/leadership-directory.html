Garland ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Garland ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators serving Garland, Rowlett, and Sachse.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Who Should I Contact?

For ARD Meeting Questions

Start with your child's Campus Diagnostician or Case Manager.

They handle student-specific daily accommodations and annual scheduling.

For Evaluation Requests

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director.

Submit a formal written request if you suspect your child has a disability.

For Complaints or Grievances

Start: Campus Principal.

Escalate to: Executive Director of Special Education if resolutions are not met at the campus level.

For General Questions

Special Education Administration Office.

Phone: 972-487-3300.

Address: 720 Stadium Drive, Garland, TX 75040.

Executive Leadership

Garland ISD (GISD) is a "Choice of School" district serving over 51,000 students. The leadership team ensures that IEPs are implemented with high fidelity regardless of the campus chosen.

Key Department Coordinators

Coordinators act as subject matter experts for Garland ISD, supporting specialized transportation and service delivery in the "Choice of School" environment.

Evaluation Coordinator

Focus: Primary contact for questions regarding Full and Individual Initial Evaluations (FIIE) status.

Contact: Call 972-487-3300

Dyslexia Coordinator

Focus: Manages identification and specialized instruction compliance under HB 3928.

Contact: Call 972-487-3300

Autism Specialist

Focus: Guidance on behavior and social skills; assists with the Texas Autism Supplement.

Contact: Call 972-487-3300

Records Clerk

Focus: Secure storage and transfer of IEP and ARD documentation for outside providers or moves.

Contact: Call 972-487-3300

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the department to be connected with the staff member for your specific school feeder pattern.

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Include your child's name, DOB, and campus in every email
  • Request written responses to verbal conversations

📝 Documentation

  • Save all IEP and ARD documentation provided by the Records Clerk.
  • Take notes during phone calls (date, time, person, summary).
  • Request confirmation of verbal agreements in writing.
