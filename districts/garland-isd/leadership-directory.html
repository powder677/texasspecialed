Executive Leadership

Garland ISD (GISD) is a "Choice of School" district, which adds a layer of complexity to special education services for its 51,000+ students. The special education leadership team is responsible for ensuring that regardless of which campus a family chooses, the student's IEP is implemented with high fidelity. When campus-level resolutions regarding services or placements are not meeting your child's needs, these executive directors provide the district-level oversight necessary to ensure FAPE (Free Appropriate Public Education).

Key Coordinators

In Garland ISD, coordinators act as subject matter experts who support specific campuses and program types. They are instrumental in the "Choice of School" environment, helping to coordinate specialized transportation and out-of-zone service delivery when a student's chosen campus requires additional support resources.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages identification and specialized instruction for students with dyslexia. They ensure compliance with HB 3928, moving dyslexia services under the Special Education umbrella for more intensive support.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Provides expert guidance on behavior, communication, and social skills programming. They assist ARD committees in developing the Texas Autism Supplement to address the unique needs of students on the spectrum.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Oversees the district's LSSPs and Educational Diagnosticians. They are the primary contact if you have questions about the status of a Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) or reevaluation.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

Manages the secure storage and transfer of confidential student records. This office is the first stop for parents requesting an official copy of an IEP or ARD document for outside providers.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the Special Education department at 972-487-3300 to be connected with the staff member responsible for your child's specific school feeder pattern.

Who to Contact When

Effective advocacy in GISD requires knowing where to start. In Texas, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process is the only legal way to modify your child's special education programming.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you suspect your child has a disability, you should submit a formal written request for evaluation. Garland ISD has specific timelines to follow once this written request is received.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Campus Diagnostician or Case Manager

For student-specific questions regarding daily accommodations, teacher communication, or scheduling your next ARD, your child's current school campus is the most direct point of contact.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

If you are moving out of Rowlett, Sachse, or Garland, or if you need records for a private medical evaluation, contact the central records clerk to request a copy of the student's file.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If a situation cannot be resolved at the campus level with the Principal, the Executive Director of Special Education provides the next tier of administrative review for formal complaints.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

Recent changes in Texas Law mean that students with dyslexia now receive "specially designed instruction" through Special Education. Contact this office if you have questions about transitioning from a 504 plan.
