Arlington ISD Special Education Leadership & Contact Directory

Direct contacts for special education services, ARD meetings, evaluations, and support in Arlington ISD, serving over 54,000 students in the DFW metroplex.

Who Should I Contact?

For ARD Meeting Questions

Start with your child's ARD case manager or campus special education coordinator.

If unresolved: Contact the district coordinator assigned to your child's program.

For Evaluation Requests

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator

Phone: 682-867-4611

Submit written requests to both the campus principal and the central special education office.

For Complaints or Disputes

Start: Campus Principal

Escalate to: Executive Director of Special Education

Program Coordinators

Arlington ISD coordinators manage specialized programming and support based on student needs and grade levels.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Focus: Oversight of dyslexia evaluations and services under HB 3928, ensuring specialized instruction within the special education framework.

Autism Specialist

Focus: Behavioral strategies, sensory supports, and implementation of the Texas Autism Supplement.

Evaluation Coordinator

Focus: Management of Full and Individual Initial Evaluations (FIIE) and coordination of district diagnosticians.

Records Clerk

Focus: Processing IEP and ARD documentation requests for student transfers or medical providers.

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a specific subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]".
  • Include your child's full name and campus in all correspondence.
  • Follow up phone conversations with a written email summary for your records.

📞 Documentation Hierarchy

  • Always start at the campus level with the teacher or case manager.
  • Submit all formal requests, such as evaluation requests, in writing.
  • Maintain a log of dates, names, and summaries of all district contacts.
