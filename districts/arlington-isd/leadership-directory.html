Arlington ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Arlington ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators serving families in Arlington and the DFW metroplex.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

Arlington ISD (AISD) supports a massive student population of over 54,000 across North Texas. The Special Education Department is structured to provide specialized services ranging from early childhood intervention to post-secondary transition. The executive leadership team manages the district's compliance with the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA) and provides administrative support to campus principals and special education teams.

Key Coordinators

While the Executive Director oversees the entire department, most day-to-day program decisions and specialized support are managed by coordinators. In Arlington ISD, coordinators are often assigned by grade level or specific disability programs to ensure that every student's individual needs are met within the ARD process.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Handles dyslexia evaluations and services under HB 3928, ensuring compliance with the updated Texas Dyslexia Handbook and the shift toward specialized instruction in Special Education.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Supports students with autism spectrum disorders, providing guidance on behavioral strategies, sensory needs, and the implementation of the Texas Autism Supplement.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages initial and reevaluation requests, overseeing the Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) timeline and coordinating the district's team of diagnosticians and LSSPs.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

Handles IEP/ARD records requests and transfers for students moving into or out of Arlington ISD. Essential for parents who need documentation for medical providers or insurance.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the main special education office at 682-867-4611 for referral to the appropriate person for your child's specific school or program.

Who to Contact When

In Texas, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process is the vehicle for all special education decisions. Use this guide to determine the best point of contact for your specific situation in Arlington ISD.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

When you suspect your child has a disability and needs support, you should submit a written request for evaluation to both the campus principal and the AISD central special education office.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Your child's ARD case manager or campus special education coordinator

For student-specific questions regarding accommodations or progress on IEP goals, your child's home campus is always the first and most effective stop.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

If you are moving or need records for an outside provider, contact the district's special education records office directly to request a copy of your child's file.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If issues cannot be resolved at the campus level with the Principal or Diagnostician, the Executive Director provides the next level of administrative oversight for formal dispute resolution.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

Under Texas HB 3928, dyslexia services are now integrated into the Special Education framework. Contact the coordinator to discuss the transition from Section 504 if applicable.
