Quick Facts: Evaluations in Arlington ISD

  • Child Find: The district must locate and evaluate all children with suspected disabilities.
  • Response Window: 15 school days from your written request to a district response.
  • Testing Timeline: 45 school days to complete the evaluation after you sign consent.
  • ARD Timeline: 30 calendar days from the finished report to the first meeting.
  • Cost: Evaluations are free to parents under FAPE (Free Appropriate Public Education).

Common Questions About Arlington ISD Evaluations

How do I request a special education evaluation in Arlington ISD?

Submit a written request to the campus principal. If your child is enrolled, this is the most direct method. You should include your child's name, grade, and specific areas of concern.

Example: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child under the IDEA Child Find mandate."

How long does the process take in Arlington?

It follows a 15-45-30 sequence. The district has 15 school days to respond to your request, 45 school days to test after you sign consent, and 30 calendar days to meet for the ARD.

What tests are included?

Qualified professionals conduct a comprehensive FIIE. Assessments usually cover cognitive ability, academic levels in core subjects, and social-emotional behavior. Following Texas HB 3928, dyslexia screening is also part of this special education framework.

Can I get an evaluation for my 3-year-old?

Yes. Arlington ISD’s Child Find obligation starts at birth. For children ages 3-5 not yet in school, contact the Special Education department directly at 682-867-7465.

What if I disagree with the district's results?

You can request an IEE. An Independent Educational Evaluation allows for an outside professional to assess your child at the district's expense if you disagree with their FIIE.

Does a medical diagnosis count?

It is only part of the evaluation. A medical diagnosis (like ADHD or Autism) does not automatically qualify a child; the school evaluation must prove an "educational need" for specialized instruction.

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

An FIIE is the comprehensive assessment used by Arlington ISD to determine if a student meets the Texas eligibility criteria for special education. It aims to identify the specific disability and whether it impacts the student’s ability to learn.

How to Request an Evaluation in Arlington ISD

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Deliver your request to the Campus Principal or the Special Education Executive Director.

Key Contact:

Dr. Michael Hill, Executive Director of Special Education

Address: 1203 W. Pioneer Pkwy, Arlington, TX 76013

Phone: 682-867-7465

Download Free Request Template

Step 2: Sign Written Consent

Arlington ISD cannot begin testing without your signed permission. Once signed, the 45-school-day timer begins.

Step 3: Evaluation & ARD

Specialists like Educational Diagnosticians will evaluate your child. Within 30 calendar days of the report, you will meet for an ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) meeting.
