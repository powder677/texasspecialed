How to Request a Special Education Evaluation in Arlington ISD

A step-by-step guide to navigating the special education evaluation process for families in Arlington ISD and the DFW Metroplex.

Child Find & Your Rights

Under federal and state law, Arlington ISD has a "Child Find" obligation to identify, locate, and evaluate all children with disabilities (birth through age 21) residing within the district who may need special education. This mandate includes children in private schools, those who are homeschooled, and highly mobile students.

You Have the Right To:

  • Request a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for your child in writing at any time.
  • Receive a response (Prior Written Notice) from the district within 15 school days of your written request.
  • Have your child assessed in all areas of suspected disability by qualified professionals.
  • Receive a copy of your Procedural Safeguards which outline your legal protections.
  • Request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at district expense if you disagree with the district's results.

Educational Need vs. Medical Diagnosis

In Arlington ISD, a medical diagnosis from a doctor is only one piece of the puzzle. The school evaluation determines if the child meets Texas eligibility criteria and if the disability creates a specific need for specialized instruction.

Step-by-Step Evaluation Process in Arlington ISD

1

Submit a Written Request

The process formally begins with a written request for evaluation. If your child is currently enrolled in an Arlington ISD school, deliver this request to the Campus Principal. For children not yet in school, contact the Special Education department directly.

Ensure your request includes the specific language required by Texas law to start the legal 15-day countdown.

2

The 15-Day Response Window

Within 15 school days of receiving your request, Arlington ISD must either provide you with a Notice of Evaluation and consent forms or a Prior Written Notice explaining their refusal to evaluate.

3

Provide Written Consent

Arlington ISD cannot evaluate your child without your signed Written Consent. Signing consent does not mean you have agreed to special education services; it only allows the testing to occur.

Timeline Trigger: The district has 45 school days to complete the evaluation report from the day they receive your signed consent.

Tip: Always request a receipt or delivery confirmation when returning your consent form to track the 45-school-day deadline.
4

The Comprehensive Evaluation (FIIE)

Qualified Arlington ISD staff, such as Educational Diagnosticians and School Psychologists (LSSPs), will evaluate your child. The assessment includes:

  • Cognitive and intellectual development.
  • Academic levels in reading, writing, and math.
  • Social, emotional, and behavioral data.
  • Dyslexia Screening: Following Texas HB 3928, dyslexia must be evaluated as a specific learning disability under the special education framework.
5

Initial ARD Committee Meeting

Within 30 calendar days of the completed evaluation report, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) committee must meet. As a parent, you are a required and equal member of this committee. You will review the data together to determine eligibility and develop an IEP if services are needed.

Texas Timeline Requirements (15-45-30)

Arlington ISD must follow the strict 15-45-30 school day sequence mandated by Texas law:

15 School Days

Written request to Consent or Refusal.

45 School Days

Signed consent to Completed Evaluation.

30 Calendar Days

Completed Evaluation to ARD Meeting.

What if Timelines are Missed?

If Arlington ISD fails to meet these deadlines, they are in non-compliance with state and federal law. Parents should:

  • Email the Campus Principal and Evaluation Coordinator for an immediate status update.
  • Contact the Arlington ISD Special Education Department at 682-867-7465.
  • Consult with SPEDTex, the Texas Special Education Information Center.

Frequently Asked Questions for Arlington Families

Is there a cost for the evaluation?

No. Evaluations provided by Arlington ISD are conducted at no cost to parents as part of the district's obligation to provide a Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE).

What if my child attends a private school in Arlington?

Arlington ISD is responsible for "Child Find" for all students within its boundaries, including those in private schools or who are homeschooled.

What is an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE)?

If you disagree with the district's evaluation, you have the right to request an IEE. This is an evaluation by an outside professional, paid for by Arlington ISD.
