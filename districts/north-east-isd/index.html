Navigating NEISD Specialized Services

North East ISD (NEISD) provides a highly structured continuum of services centered at the Tesoro Drive administration building. For families navigating the Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) process, NEISD is unique for its Alternative Learning Environment (ALE)—specialized units for students needing functional academics—and the Redirection program for intensive behavioral support.

Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must complete initial evaluations within 45 school days of signed consent. NEISD is also proactive in its "Child Find" efforts, offering specific screening clinics for preschool-aged children (ages 3-5) through the Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) program. At the secondary level, transition planning is a core focus, with the district utilizing the Academy of Creative Education (ACE) for non-traditional pathways.

If you are experiencing a disagreement with a campus committee, NEISD utilizes Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) for dispute resolution. Parents are encouraged to contact the Special Education Program Coordinator assigned to their child's specific instructional arrangement (ALE, Redirection, or Resource) at the central office before initiating formal mediation.
