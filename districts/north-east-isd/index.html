Policy & Program Authority in North East ISD

Navigating special education in North East ISD requires understanding the specific chain of command at the 8961 Tesoro Dr administrative office. While initial evaluations begin at the campus level—such as Winston Churchill High School or Johnson High School—complex programmatic decisions for specialized placements like the Alternative Learning Environment (ALE) or the Redirection program are overseen by central Program Coordinators.

Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), North East ISD must complete evaluations within 45 school days of receiving written parental consent. For preschool-aged children, the district centralizes intake through Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) screening clinics to meet Child Find mandates. Families should note that under Board Policy FNG(LOCAL), any grievances regarding IEP implementation should first be addressed with the campus principal before escalating to the Tesoro Drive administration.

Specialized Instructional Arrangements

North East ISD utilizes several district-specific programs for students with high support needs:

  • ALE (Alternative Learning Environment): Focused on functional academics and life skills for students with significant cognitive deficits.
  • Redirection Program: A behavioral support model focused on teaching pro-social replacement behaviors.
  • Family Engagement: NEISD employs specific Family Specialists at many campuses to assist parents in navigating ARD committee meetings.
