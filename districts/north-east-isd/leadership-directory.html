North East ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for North East ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators serving San Antonio families.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

North East ISD (NEISD) is a prominent district in San Antonio, serving over 57,000 students. The special education leadership team ensures that the district's programs—from Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) to transition services—are implemented with fidelity and comply with federal and state mandates. When campus-level support is exhausted, these directors provide the necessary oversight to resolve complex educational concerns.

Key Coordinators

In North East ISD, central office coordinators act as subject matter experts for specific service areas. They provide guidance to ARD committees and ensure that campus staff are utilizing evidence-based strategies to support student progress in the Least Restrictive Environment (LRE).

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Oversees the identification and instruction for students with dyslexia under HB 3928, ensuring access to specialized instruction within the special education framework.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Provides support for students on the autism spectrum, helping campus teams implement social-emotional supports and the required Texas Autism Supplement strategies.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages the team of Educational Diagnosticians and LSSPs who conduct Full and Individual Initial Evaluations (FIIE) to determine special education eligibility.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

The point of contact for requesting IEP and ARD records. This is essential for parents transferring into NEISD or providing documentation to outside specialists.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the main special education office at 210-407-0000 to be directed to the person overseeing your child's campus cluster.

Who to Contact When

Effective advocacy starts with contacting the right person. In Texas, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process is the formal meeting where all special education programming decisions are made. Use this guide to navigate North East ISD's structure.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you believe your child has a disability, submit a formal written request for evaluation to the district's special education administration and your child's school principal.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Campus Diagnostician or Case Manager

Your child’s campus team is the primary resource for questions regarding classroom accommodations, progress on goals, or scheduling an ARD meeting.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

When you need copies of previous evaluations or IEP documents for medical providers or a move, contact the central records office directly.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If you cannot reach an agreement with the campus ARD committee or principal, the Executive Director oversees the formal district-level resolution process.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

Recent Texas legislative changes mean that dyslexia is now primarily served under special education. Contact this office for help with the transition from Section 504.
