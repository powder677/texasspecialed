Quick Facts: Evaluations in North East ISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full (Individual) Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, teachers, or school staff can refer a child.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to completed evaluation in NEISD.
  • Cost: Free to parents. The district covers all assessment costs.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting within 30 days of the completed evaluation report.

Request a Special Education Evaluation in North East ISD

Essential guidance for San Antonio families. Learn how to start the evaluation process, understand timelines, and advocate for your child's needs in North East Independent School District.

Common Questions About North East ISD Evaluations

```

How do I request a special education evaluation in North East ISD?

Submit a written request to your campus principal. You can also email the North East ISD Special Education Department. Be sure to include your child's name, school, and the specific areas of concern (academic, social, or behavioral).

Sample request language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], date of birth [DOB], currently enrolled at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in North East ISD?

45 school days maximum. Once you sign the official consent form, North East ISD has 45 school days to complete all testing. This timeline pauses for district holidays and summer breaks.

What tests are included in a North East ISD evaluation?

Varies by suspected disability. Common assessments used by NEISD include cognitive testing (IQ), academic achievement tests (reading/math), and social-emotional scales. If dyslexia is suspected, specific literacy assessments are included per Texas HB 3928.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. North East ISD cannot require a medical diagnosis as a prerequisite for an evaluation. If you suspect your child has a disability that impacts their learning, the district must conduct its own evaluation under IDEA.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) in North East ISD?

Yes, if you disagree with the district's results. If you believe the NEISD evaluation was flawed or incomplete, you can request an IEE at public expense. The district will provide a list of qualified independent evaluators in the San Antonio area.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

Initial ARD meeting within 30 calendar days. Once the report is written, an ARD committee (which includes you) will meet to review the data and determine if your child qualifies for an Individualized Education Program (IEP). Learn about ARD meetings →

```

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

A FIIE is a comprehensive assessment required by federal law to determine eligibility for special education. In North East ISD, this process answers three critical questions:

  • Does the child have a disability? Based on the 13 categories recognized under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA).
  • Does the disability impact educational performance? Simply having a diagnosis is not enough; it must show an impact in the classroom.
  • What services are needed? The evaluation identifies specific areas where your child needs specialized instruction.
```

13 Disability Categories in Texas Special Education

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment (Includes ADHD) Specific Learning Disability (Includes Dyslexia) Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment
```

How to Request an Evaluation in North East ISD

```

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Submit your request to the campus principal or the North East ISD Special Education Office.

Where to Send Your Request:

Method: Email or Written Letter

Contact: Dr. David Moore, Executive Director

Email: specialeducation@neisd.net

Address: 8961 Tesoro Dr., San Antonio, TX 78217

Sample Evaluation Request Letter


```

[Date]

Dr. David Moore
North East ISD Special Education
8961 Tesoro Dr.
San Antonio, TX 78217

Dear Dr. Moore,

I am writing to formally request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child, [Child's Full Name], date of birth [DOB], currently enrolled in [Grade] at [School Name].

I am concerned about my child's progress in [Reading/Math/Behavior/Social Skills]. I believe [Child's Name] may have a disability that requires special education services under IDEA.

Please provide me with consent forms and begin the evaluation process as soon as possible. I understand that North East ISD has 45 school days from my signed consent to complete the evaluation.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[Your Phone]
Download Fillable Template
```

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Within 15 school days, North East ISD must respond. They will either give you consent forms to sign or a notice explaining why they won't evaluate. The 45-day clock does not start until you sign the consent form.

Step 3: Evaluation Period (45 School Days)

Once consent is signed, the district's specialists (Diagnosticians, LSSPs, SLPs) will begin testing. This includes observations, academic tests, and reviewing records.

Step 4: Receive Evaluation Report

You have the right to receive the written FIE report before the ARD meeting. We recommend requesting it at least 5 school days in advance so you have time to review the data.

```

The Evaluation Timeline in North East ISD

```
Day 1

Parent Submits Request

15 School Days

District Responds

District provides consent forms for you to sign.

45 School Days

Evaluation Period

From the date you sign consent to the completed report.

30 Calendar Days

Initial ARD Meeting

The committee meets to determine eligibility.

Note: If the 45th school day falls during the summer, the timeline may be extended. However, North East ISD generally attempts to complete spring requests before the school year ends.

```

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

North East ISD uses a variety of standardized tools to understand your child's learning profile:

```

Cognitive/IQ

Measures intellectual ability, memory, and processing speed using tests like the WISC-V.

Academic

Tests reading, writing, and math skills (WIAT-4) to see if your child is performing at grade level.

Psychological

LSSPs evaluate emotional regulation, behavior, and social interactions.

Speech/Language

SLPs assess how your child understands language and expresses themselves verbally.

```

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with North East ISD’s evaluation, you have the right to an IEE. This is an evaluation by a private professional paid for by the district.

How to Request an IEE in NEISD

Write to the Special Education Director stating that you disagree with the district's evaluation and are requesting an IEE at public expense. The district must respond by either granting the request or taking you to a due process hearing to prove their evaluation was sufficient.

Learn About Dispute Resolution

Early Childhood Evaluations (Ages 3-5)

North East ISD provides Child Find services for preschoolers. If your 3 or 4-year-old shows delays in speech, motor skills, or social development, you do not need to wait for Kindergarten.

Contact the NEISD Preschool Referral Line: 210-407-0000
