Answer: In North East ISD, an ARD meeting is the legally required Texas special education process where parents and San Antonio district staff determine a student's eligibility, services, and placement. Parents must receive at least five school days’ written notice, maintain the right to audio record the meeting, and serve as equal members of the decision-making committee.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to North East ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: North East ISD ARD Meetings

  • Notice Timeline: Minimum of 5 school days’ written notice is required.
  • Recording Rights: Parents may audio record the meeting without school permission under Texas law.
  • Parent Role: You are a legally required, equal member of the ARD committee.
  • Annual Requirement: The committee must meet at least once every 12 months.
  • Initial ARD Timing: Must occur within 30 days of a completed Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).

Common North East ISD ARD Questions

Who attends an ARD meeting at an NEISD campus?

The committee typically includes the parent, a special education teacher, a general education teacher, a district representative (usually a Principal), and an evaluator. Depending on the campus, a dedicated ARD Facilitator may also be present to manage documentation.

Can I request an ARD meeting in North East ISD?

Parents in North East ISD may request an ARD meeting at any time to discuss concerns regarding their child's IEP or progress. The district must hold the meeting or provide a written explanation for refusal.

What if I disagree with the ARD committee's recommendations?

If agreement is not reached, parents may request a "10-day recess" to gather more data or explore alternatives before finalizing the IEP. You also have the right to sign in disagreement and explore mediation or grievance options.

How to Prepare for a North East ISD ARD Meeting

Step 1: Review Draft Documents

Ask your NEISD case manager for a draft of proposed goals at least one week before the meeting.

Step 2: Document Parent Concerns

Prepare a written statement of your concerns and vision for your child to be included in the official deliberations.

Step 3: Organize Supporting Data

Gather recent work samples, progress reports, and any private evaluations to share with the NEISD committee.

How ARD Meetings in North East ISD May Differ From Other Districts

North East ISD employs specific staffing and procedural patterns that San Antonio families should note:

  • Campus ARD Facilitators: Many NEISD campuses utilize specialized facilitators whose primary role is managing the complex paperwork and legal timelines, often distinct from the classroom teacher.
  • REED Integration: In North East ISD, the "Review of Existing Evaluation Data" (REED) process is often bundled closely with the annual ARD to streamline triennial reevaluations.
  • Virtual/In-Person Flexibility: NEISD consistently offers robust virtual attendance options to accommodate working parents, a practice that is highly standardized across the district's campuses.
