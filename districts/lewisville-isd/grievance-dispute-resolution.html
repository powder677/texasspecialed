Quick Answer: To file a special education complaint in Lewisville ISD, parents should first attempt to resolve the issue with their campus ARD chair or principal. If informal resolution is unsuccessful, you can file a formal Level One Grievance under Board Policy FNG (Local) by emailing the LISD Office of Legal Services at grievances@lisd.net. LISD, which serves over 50,000 students in the North Dallas area, requires this formal notice within 15 district business days of the incident.

Understanding the dispute resolution pathways in Lewisville Independent School District. Whether you are in Flower Mound, Highland Village, or Lewisville, this guide explains your rights under FNG (Local) policies and TEA state mandates.

Quick Facts: Dispute Resolution in Lewisville ISD

  • Primary Contact: Tina Clark, Executive Director of Special Education.
  • District Grievance Method: Formal Level 1 Form submitted to LISD Legal Services.
  • Phone Support: (469) 713-5203 for Special Education questions.
  • State Option: TEA Complaint (One-year filing limit).
  • Local Support: LISD Special Education Family Center offers Tuesday evening support groups.

Common Questions About LISD Dispute Resolution

Does Lewisville ISD have a parent liaison for special education?

While some districts have a dedicated "Parent Liaison" role, LISD provides family support through the Special Education Family Center located at 400A High School Dr.. They offer solution-focused family counseling and parenting resource groups on Tuesday evenings to help families navigate the special education system. Parents can also reach out to the Greater Lewisville Special Education PTSA for community-led resource sharing.

What happens during an LISD Level One Grievance conference?

Once you submit your form to grievances@lisd.net, an administrator will contact you to set up a Level One conference. During this meeting, you can explain your concerns regarding your child's ARD services or placement. LISD will then provide a written response within 10 district business days. If you are dissatisfied, you have 10 days to appeal to Level Two.

LISD Dispute Resolution Escalation Path

Level 1

Campus Resolution (Recommended First Step)

Contact: Campus Principal or Diagnostician.

Goal: Immediate, informal resolution of service delivery or IEP issues.

Level 2

Formal District Grievance (FNG Local)

Submit to: Office of Legal Services.

Form: Student/Parent Level One Grievance Form.

Deadline: 15 District business days.

Level 3

Texas Education Agency (TEA) or Mediation

TEA Complaint: Filed state-wide for legal non-compliance.

Mediation: A neutral third party helps negotiate a solution.
