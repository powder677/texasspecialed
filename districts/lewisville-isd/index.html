Navigating LISD Specialized Services

Lewisville Independent School District (LISD) operates its special education department primarily out of the Bolin Administrative Center on West Main Street. LISD is well-regarded for its specialized program structure, but understanding the terminology is essential for parents. Key programs include Communications (Autism/Social Communication), Focus (Behavioral Support), and Life Skills (Functional Academics).

For families of older students, LISD provides extensive transition services through Project SEARCH and the Lewisville ISD Collegiate Academy. The district emphasizes "Future Ready" skills, and under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must trigger the 45-school-day evaluation window upon receipt of signed parental consent for an initial Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).

A key tip for navigating LISD is to utilize the "Zone" structure. The district is divided into high school feeder patterns (Marcus, Flower Mound, Hebron, The Colony, and Lewisville). Special Education Coordinators are assigned to these specific zones. If you have a conflict at the campus level that the Campus Principal cannot resolve, contacting the Coordinator for your high school feeder pattern is the required next step before escalating to Dr. Michele Janda. Formal disputes are processed via Board Policy FNG(LOCAL).
