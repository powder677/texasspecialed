Lewisville ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Lewisville ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators serving Lewisville, Flower Mound, Highland Village, The Colony, and Carrollton.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

Lewisville ISD (LISD) is a sprawling district in the DFW metroplex serving over 48,000 students. The Special Education department is led by a team of dedicated professionals who oversee the implementation of individualized programming across more than 60 campuses. When you need support beyond the campus level—whether for complex ARD disputes or policy clarifications—the executive leadership team provides the final tier of district-wide oversight.

Key Coordinators

In Lewisville ISD, specialized coordinators provide the technical expertise needed to support diverse learner profiles. They work closely with campus administrators and teachers to ensure that the district remains at the forefront of special education best practices and adheres to all Texas Education Agency mandates.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages dyslexia identification and services following the HB 3928 transition. They ensure students receive evidence-based, specially designed instruction within the special education framework.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Provides strategic support for students on the autism spectrum, assisting campus teams with sensory strategies, behavioral support, and the completion of the required Texas Autism Supplement.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Oversees the team of Educational Diagnosticians and LSSPs. This office is responsible for tracking Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) requests and maintaining state-mandated timelines.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

The primary point of contact for families needing official copies of IEP or ARD documents. Vital for families moving between DFW districts or seeking outside medical consultations.

Note: If specific names are not listed above, please call the main Special Education office at 469-713-5203 to be connected with the coordinator assigned to your child's specific school zone.

Who to Contact When

Advocating for your child in a large district like LISD is most effective when you know the proper chain of command. Remember that in Texas, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) committee is the legal decision-making body for all special education matters.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you suspect your child has a disability that impacts their learning, submit a formal, written request for evaluation to the district’s special education administration office.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Your child's Case Manager or Campus Diagnostician

For student-specific concerns regarding daily accommodations, goal progress, or to request a meeting, your child's home campus is always the first stop.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

If you are relocating or need your child's educational file for a private specialist, contact the records clerk to request a complete copy of the student's special education file.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If issues cannot be resolved with your campus principal or diagnostician, the Executive Director of Special Education provides administrative review and conflict resolution.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

Under the updated Texas Dyslexia Handbook, many services have moved to special education. Contact this office if you are transitioning from a 504 plan to an IEP.
