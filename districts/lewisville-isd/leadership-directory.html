Tips for Effective Communication with LISD

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Include your child's name and DOB in every email to help staff locate records quickly.
  • Request a "Read Receipt" or follow up if you do not receive a reply within 2 business days.

📝 Chain of Command

  • Level 1: Teacher / Case Manager
  • Level 2: Campus Diagnostician or Assistant Principal
  • Level 3: Campus Principal
  • Level 4: District Zone Coordinator or Executive Director
