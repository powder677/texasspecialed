Quick Facts: Evaluations in Lewisville ISD

  • What is it? FIIE = Full Individual Initial Evaluation.
  • Who can request? Parents, legal guardians, or district staff.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to report.
  • Cost: Free to parents; assessments provided at no cost.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting within 30 calendar days of report.

Request a Special Education Evaluation in Lewisville ISD

Navigating the evaluation process in Lewisville, Flower Mound, and surrounding communities. Learn how to initiate a request, understand the 45-day timeline, and advocate for your child in Lewisville Independent School District.

Common Questions About Lewisville ISD Evaluations

```

How do I request a special education evaluation in Lewisville ISD?

Submit a written request to your campus principal or the Special Education department.. State clearly that you suspect your child has a disability and request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE). Emailing both the principal and the Special Education office is recommended to ensure a recorded receipt.

How long does the evaluation process take in Lewisville ISD?

The standard timeline is 15-45-30.. Upon receiving your request, LISD has 15 school days to provide notice and request consent. After consent is signed, the district has 45 school days to conduct testing. Finally, an ARD meeting must be held within 30 calendar days of report completion.

What tests are included in a Lewisville ISD evaluation?

Assessments are tailored to individual needs.. These may include cognitive testing (IQ), academic achievement tests (reading, math), speech-language assessments, and behavioral observations. Under HB 3928, dyslexia evaluations must follow IDEA special education protocols.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. Lewisville ISD's Child Find mandate requires them to identify and evaluate any student suspected of a disability regardless of prior medical diagnosis. School evaluations focus on educational eligibility rather than medical diagnosis.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) in Lewisville ISD?

Yes, if you disagree with the district's results.. You can request an IEE at public expense, conducted by a qualified professional not employed by the district.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

An Initial ARD meeting is held within 30 calendar days.. The ARD committee, including you, reviews results to determine if your child is eligible for services. A copy of the report should be provided to you at least five school days before this meeting.

How do I get an evaluation for my 3-5 year old?

Contact the LISD Child Find office. For children aged 2 years 9 months to 5 years not yet in school, contact the Child Find Secretary at 469-948-8515.

```

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

An FIIE is a comprehensive assessment conducted to determine if a child has a disability and to specify their educational needs. It must assess all areas related to the suspected disability.

How to Request an Evaluation in Lewisville ISD

```

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Send a formal letter to your principal and the Special Education director..

Where to Send Your Request:

Method: Email or Certified Mail

Contact: SSAB (Special Services Administration Building)

Email: spedquestions@lisd.net

Phone: 469-713-5203

Address: 400A High School Dr., Lewisville, TX 75057

Sample Evaluation Request Letter


```

[Date]

[Principal Name] / Special Education Director
Lewisville ISD
[School Address]

Dear [Name],

I am writing to formally request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child, [Child's Full Name], date of birth [DOB], currently enrolled at [School Name].

I have concerns regarding [academic progress / behavior / speech]. I suspect my child may have a disability that requires special education services.

Please provide me with the necessary consent forms and procedural safeguards. I understand that the district has 15 school days to respond to this request.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[Phone/Email]
Download Fillable Template
```

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Within 15 school days, LISD must provide a Prior Written Notice and an opportunity to sign consent. Testing cannot begin without your written consent.

Step 3: Evaluation Period (45 School Days)

Once consent is received, the district has 45 school days to complete the written report. This timeline may be extended if the student is absent for three or more days.

Step 4: ARD Meeting (Within 30 Days)

The ARD committee must meet within 30 calendar days of report completion. If the 30th day falls in summer, the meeting must generally occur by the 15th school day of the following year.

```

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Lewisville ISD uses a variety of tools to evaluate educational needs:

Cognitive/IQ

Tests: WISC-V, KABC-II

Measures: Reasoning and problem-solving.

Academic

Tests: WJ-IV, WIAT-4

Measures: Skills in reading, math, and writing.

Speech

Tests: CELF-5, GFTA-3

Measures: Language and articulation.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with an LISD evaluation, you may request an IEE at public expense. The district must then either grant the IEE or file for a due process hearing to prove its evaluation was appropriate.

Contact: Special Education Director at SSAB.

Lewisville ISD Evaluation Specifics

Private/Homeschool Evaluations

If your child is in a private school in LISD boundaries, you may refer them to the LISD campus they would otherwise attend. Contact the Special Education office at 469-713-5203 for guidance.
