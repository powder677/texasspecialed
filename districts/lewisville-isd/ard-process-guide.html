Answer: In Lewisville ISD, an ARD meeting is the legally mandated Texas special education process where parents and district staff collaborate to determine eligibility, services, and placement. Parents in Lewisville ISD are equal committee members who must receive at least five school days’ written notice before a meeting and maintain the right to audio record proceedings or request a 10-day recess if consensus is not reached.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to Lewisville ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: Lewisville ISD ARD Meetings

  • Notice Timeline: Minimum of 5 school days' written notice required.
  • Recording Rights: Parents may audio record with prior notice to the campus administrator.
  • Parent Role: You are a required, equal member of the ARD committee.
  • Frequency: Meetings must occur at least once per year (Annual ARD).
  • Draft Access: Lewisville ISD typically provides draft goals and PLAAFPs prior to the meeting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an ARD meeting take in Lewisville ISD?

A typical ARD meeting in Lewisville ISD lasts approximately 60 minutes, though complex initial or transition meetings may require more time. The committee must stay until all required agenda items are addressed or a recess is called.

Can I bring an advocate to a Lewisville ISD ARD?

Parents have the right to bring an advocate or outside provider to any ARD meeting in Lewisville ISD. The district requests advance notice if an advocate or attorney will be attending to ensure appropriate district representation is present.

What happens if I disagree with the ARD committee?

If consensus cannot be reached, Lewisville ISD protocol allows for a 10-day recess to gather more data or explore alternatives. Parents may sign "Disagree" on the final document while allowing agreed-upon services to move forward.

How to Prepare for a Lewisville ISD ARD Meeting

  1. Review the Draft IEP: Examine the draft PLAAFPs and goals provided by the LISD team at least 3-5 days before the meeting.
  2. Document Parent Concerns: Prepare a written statement of your concerns to be included verbatim in the "Parent Input" section of the ARD document.
  3. Verify Evaluation Data: Contact the campus Diagnostician (the "Diag") if you have questions about recent testing or the Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).
  4. Organize Outside Data: Gather any private therapy reports or medical diagnoses you wish the Lewisville ISD committee to consider.

How ARD Meetings in Lewisville ISD May Differ From Other Districts

Lewisville ISD utilizes specific staffing patterns and resources that distinguish its ARD process from neighboring North Texas districts:

  • SDI Strategists: Unlike districts that use general "Resource Teachers," LISD often utilizes Specially Designed Instruction (SDI) Strategists to oversee goal implementation.
  • The Special Education Family Center: LISD maintains a dedicated facility at 400-A High School Dr. to support parents outside of the formal ARD meeting environment.
  • "The Spot" Lending Library: This unique LISD resource allows parents to preview sensory tools or educational kits before requesting them as accommodations in an ARD.
  • In-Home Training (IHT) Focus: LISD has a robust process for evaluating the need for In-Home Training for students who struggle to generalize skills between school and home.
