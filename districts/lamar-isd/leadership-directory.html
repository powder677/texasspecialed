Campus Special Education Coordinators

For day-to-day questions about your child's services, contact your campus special education coordinator or ARD committee chair.

Lamar CISD operates a campus-based ARD team model. Each elementary, middle, and high school campus has a designated Diagnostician or Case Manager responsible for your child's IEP implementation. For a specific campus contact list, visit the Lamar CISD Special Education page.

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Include your child's name, DOB, and campus in every email.
  • Keep initial emails brief and request specific action steps.
  • Request written confirmation of any verbal agreements made via phone.

📞 Phone Contact

  • Call during business hours: Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM.
  • Leave detailed voicemails with your name, child's name, and a specific callback number.
  • Follow up every phone call with a "summary email" to document the conversation.
