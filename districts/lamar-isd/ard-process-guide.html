Answer: In Lamar CISD, an ARD meeting is the legally required Texas special education process where parents and district staff determine a student's eligibility, services, and placement. Under Texas law, Lamar CISD parents must receive at least five school days’ written notice, are mandatory equal members of the committee, and have the right to audio record all proceedings.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to Lamar CISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: ARD Meetings in Lamar CISD

  • Notice Timeline: Minimum 5 school days' written notice required.
  • Recording Rights: Parents may audio record; notification to the campus is requested for logistics.
  • Parent Role: Mandatory, equal member of the ARD committee.
  • Annual Requirement: The committee must meet at least once every 12 months.
  • Initial ARD Timing: Must occur within 30 calendar days of a completed Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).

How ARD Meetings in Lamar CISD May Differ From Other Districts

Lamar CISD utilizes specific staffing roles and technology policies that impact the ARD experience for families in Rosenberg and Richmond:

  • ARD Committee Managers: Unlike many districts where classroom teachers handle all logistics, Lamar CISD employs dedicated ARD Committee Managers to facilitate meetings and ensure procedural compliance.
  • Skyward Integration: The district heavily utilizes Skyward Family Access for data review; parents are encouraged to audit grades and attendance records here prior to the PLAAFP discussion.
  • Electronic Device Policy (2025-2026): Following the 2025-2026 device update, any necessary student use of personal technology for educational or medical reasons must be explicitly documented as an accommodation within the IEP.
  • Adult Transition Program (ATP): Lamar CISD provides a specialized pathway for students aged 18-21, with the ARD committee specifically determining placement into these community-based transition services.

How to Prepare for an ARD Meeting in Lamar CISD

1. Review Data via Skyward

Access Skyward Family Access to review recent progress monitoring and attendance data that will be used to draft the IEP.

2. Request a Draft IEP

Request a "Draft IEP" from your campus ARD Committee Manager at least 3-5 days before the meeting to review proposed goals in advance.

3. Document Parent Concerns

Prepare a written list of your concerns; under Texas procedural safeguards, the district must document and consider parent input during the meeting.

Common Lamar CISD ARD Questions

Can I bring an advocate to a Lamar CISD ARD?

Yes, parents have the legal right to bring advocates or individuals with special knowledge of the child. The district typically requests advance notice so they can ensure the meeting room is adequate for the group.

What happens if I disagree with the ARD committee?

If consensus is not reached, Lamar CISD will offer a 10-day recess. This allows parents and the district to gather more data before meeting a second time to reach an agreement.
