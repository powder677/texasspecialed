Navigating Lamar CISD Specialized Services

Lamar CISD covers a massive geographic area, serving the diverse communities of Rosenberg, Richmond, and Fulshear. Because of this growth, the district uses a highly structured staffing model. Unlike smaller districts where teachers run meetings, Lamar CISD employs dedicated ARD Committee Managers (Facilitators) to oversee compliance and run Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) meetings. This ensures procedural consistency across campuses.

The district places a heavy emphasis on data accessibility through Skyward Family Access. Parents are strongly encouraged to review attendance, grades, and progress reports in Skyward prior to any ARD meeting. For students aged 18-21 who require post-secondary support, the district operates an Adult Transition Program designed to foster employability and independent living skills within the local community.

If you suspect your child has a disability, the "Child Find" process is initiated through the campus Counselor or Diagnostician. Once consent is signed, Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL) mandates that the Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) be completed within 45 school days. Parents needing to resolve disputes should follow the chain of command: starting with the campus ARD Manager, then the Principal, before escalating to the Executive Director at Brazos Crossing. Formal grievances are filed under Board Policy FNG(LOCAL).
