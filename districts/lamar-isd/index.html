Navigating Lamar CISD Special Programs

Lamar Consolidated ISD implements a comprehensive service model for students with disabilities under the authority of Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL). This policy mandates that the district identify and evaluate all students suspected of having a disability within 45 school days of receiving written parental consent.

The district is recognized for the Pegasus Program, an inclusive early childhood initiative where students with disabilities learn alongside typically developing peers. Unlike many districts that isolate preschool services, Lamar CISD integrates these services at various elementary campuses to promote social development from age 3.

For secondary students, the 18+ Adult Transition Program (ATP) focuses on post-secondary independence. Under the guidance of Transition Coordinators, students receive community-based instruction designed to meet the mandates of the Texas Transition and Employment Guide.

Dispute Resolution: If an ARD Committee fails to reach a consensus, Lamar CISD Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) governs the grievance process. Parents are encouraged to first contact their assigned Special Education Coordinator at the campus level before escalating concerns to the Brazos Crossing administrative team.
