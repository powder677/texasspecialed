Quick Facts: Evaluations in Lamar CISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full (Individual) Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, teachers, or school staff can refer a child.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to completed evaluation in Lamar CISD.
  • Cost: Free to parents. The district covers all testing costs.
  • Next Step: Initial ARD meeting within 30 days of the completed report.

Request a Special Education Evaluation in Lamar CISD

Essential guidance for Rosenberg and Richmond families. Learn how to start the evaluation process, understand timelines, and advocate for your child's needs in Lamar Consolidated Independent School District.

Common Questions About Lamar CISD Evaluations

How do I request a special education evaluation in Lamar CISD?

Submit a written request to the Special Education department or your campus principal. You should email or mail a formal letter to the Executive Director of Special Education at the Lamar CISD Special Education Office. Written requests are vital as they trigger legal timelines under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA).

Sample request language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], date of birth [DOB], currently enrolled at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Lamar CISD?

45 school days maximum for the evaluation. Once you sign the consent form, Lamar CISD has 45 school days (not counting weekends, holidays, or summer break) to complete all assessments and provide you with a written report. Following this, the district has an additional 30 calendar days to hold the ARD meeting.

What tests are included in a Lamar CISD evaluation?

The evaluation is comprehensive and varies by suspected disability. Common assessments used by Lamar CISD specialists include: cognitive/IQ testing (such as the WISC-V), academic achievement tests (Woodcock-Johnson), adaptive behavior scales, and specialized speech/language evaluations. The district must assess your child in all areas of suspected disability.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. You do not need a medical diagnosis (like ADHD or Autism) to request a school evaluation. Lamar CISD has a "Child Find" duty to evaluate any child who may have a disability that impacts their learning, regardless of whether they have a prior medical diagnosis.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) in Lamar CISD?

Yes, if you disagree with the district's findings. If you believe Lamar CISD's evaluation was incorrect or incomplete, you can request an IEE. This is an evaluation conducted by an outside professional at the district's expense. You should submit this request in writing to the Special Education Director.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

An ARD meeting is held within 30 days. After the report is finished, Lamar CISD will convene the Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) committee. As a parent, you are a required member of this committee. You will review the results together and decide if your child meets Texas eligibility criteria for special education. Learn about ARD meetings →

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

A FIIE (often referred to as an FIE in Lamar CISD) is a formal assessment process. It isn't just one test, but a collection of data and assessments designed to answer three critical questions:

  • Does the child have a disability? Does the student meet the criteria for one of the 13 eligibility categories?
  • Is there an educational need? Does the disability cause the child to struggle in the general education classroom?
  • What services are needed? What specific instruction, accommodations, or therapies are required for the child to make progress?

13 Disability Categories in Texas Special Education

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment Specific Learning Disability Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment

In Lamar CISD, these evaluations are conducted by a multidisciplinary team. Depending on your child's needs, this may include Educational Diagnosticians, Licensed Specialists in School Psychology (LSSP), Speech-Language Pathologists (SLP), and Occupational Therapists (OT).

Step 1: Submit Written Request

While you can talk to a teacher about your concerns, a written request is what starts the legal clock in Lamar CISD.

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Within 15 school days, Lamar CISD will contact you. They will either provide you with "Notice of Proposed Evaluation" and a consent form, or a "Prior Written Notice" explaining why they do not believe an evaluation is necessary at this time.

Crucial: Testing cannot begin until you sign that consent form.

Step 3: The 45-Day Evaluation Period

Once Lamar CISD receives your signed consent, the 45-school-day timeline begins. During this time, specialists will observe your child, conduct one-on-one testing, and gather information from you and your child's teachers.

Step 4: Receive and Review the Report

Lamar CISD must provide you with a copy of the Full Individual Evaluation (FIE) report. We recommend requesting this report at least 5 business days before your scheduled meeting so you have time to read the findings and prepare your questions.

The committee meets to determine eligibility and create an IEP if needed.

Note: School days do not include weekends, district holidays (like Spring Break), or summer vacation. If you sign consent late in the spring semester, the timeline may carry over into the following school year.

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Lamar CISD uses standardized tools to ensure evaluations are fair and accurate. Depending on your concerns, the evaluation may include:

Intelligence (IQ)

Tests: WISC-V, KABC-II

Purpose: To understand how your child processes information and their verbal/non-verbal reasoning skills.

Achievement

Tests: WIAT-4, Woodcock-Johnson

Purpose: To measure current levels in reading, writing, and math compared to other students their age.

Social-Emotional

Tests: BASC-3, Conners-4

Purpose: To look at behaviors, anxiety, attention, and emotional regulation in the school setting.

Communication

Tests: CELF-5, GFTA-3

Purpose: Conducted by an SLP to evaluate speech sounds, vocabulary, and social language use.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

In Lamar CISD, if you disagree with the results of a district evaluation, you have the right to an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE). This means an outside expert, not employed by the school, conducts their own testing.

When you request an IEE at public expense, Lamar CISD must either pay for it or file for a due process hearing to show that their own evaluation was sufficient. Most parents request an IEE when they feel the school missed a specific diagnosis or didn't capture the full extent of the child's needs.

Early Childhood Evaluations (Ages 3-5)

Does your preschooler struggle with speech or reach milestones later than peers? Lamar CISD provides Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) evaluations for children ages 3 to 5 who are not yet in kindergarten.

You do not have to wait for your child to turn 5 to get help. If your child qualifies after an evaluation, they can receive services like speech therapy or specialized preschool classes directly through Lamar CISD at no cost to you.

Contact for 3-5 year olds: 832-223-0000
