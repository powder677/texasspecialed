Highland Park ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Highland Park ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators in the Dallas area.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

Highland Park ISD is committed to providing a high-quality education that meets the unique needs of every student. The Special Education Department, often referred to within the district as Learner Services, oversees the implementation of individualized education programs (IEPs) and ensures that students receive the specialized instruction and related services they are entitled to under federal and state law.

Key Coordinators

While the Executive Director provides the strategic vision, Highland Park ISD utilizes a team of specialized coordinators to manage specific programs and compliance areas. In a district with such high academic expectations, these coordinators ensure that the "specially designed instruction" required for students with disabilities is both rigorous and supportive.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Following the significant changes in HB 3928, the Dyslexia Coordinator manages the identification and service delivery for students with dyslexia within the Special Education framework. They are a critical resource for parents transitioning from Section 504 to an IEP for reading services.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Provides expert guidance on behavioral interventions, communication tools, and environmental modifications for students on the autism spectrum. They often consult directly with campus ARD committees to support classroom implementation.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

This coordinator manages the "Child Find" process and oversees the Full and Individual Initial Evaluations (FIIE). If you are seeking an initial evaluation for your child, this office ensures the district adheres to the legal 45-school-day timeline for completion.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

Responsible for the secure transfer and maintenance of special education records. If you are moving into the Park Cities or need to provide documentation to a private psychologist, this is your primary administrative contact.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the Learner Services main office at 214-780-3000. Due to the district's size, some staff members may serve multiple campus roles.

Who to Contact When

Effective advocacy starts with knowing who to call. In Texas, the ARD process is designed to be collaborative, but it can be complex. Use this guide to navigate the Highland Park ISD administration efficiently.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you suspect your child has a disability that impacts their learning, submit a formal, dated request for evaluation in writing to the special education office. This triggers the legal timeline for the district's response.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Campus Diagnostician or Speech Pathologist

For questions regarding your child's specific IEP goals, classroom accommodations, or upcoming ARD dates, your child's campus-based team is always the first point of contact.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

Under FERPA, parents have the right to inspect and review all educational records. Contact the central records clerk to request a digital or physical copy of your child's most recent FIE and IEP.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director of Special Education

If you have a disagreement with an ARD committee decision that cannot be resolved at the campus level, contact the Executive Director to discuss mediation or formal grievance procedures.
