Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Include your child's full name and campus in every email.
  • Request a "read receipt" for time-sensitive requests (like evaluation requests).
  • Keep a digital folder of all outgoing and incoming district emails.

📞 Documentation

  • Follow up every phone call with a brief "summary email" to the district staff member.
  • Log the date, time, and name of anyone you speak with regarding services.
  • If a verbal agreement is made, request it be documented in the "Deliberations" section of the next ARD.
