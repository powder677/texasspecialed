Quick Answer: In Highland Park ISD, special education services are centrally managed by Director of Special Education Laurie Gagne at the HPISD Administration Building. The district strictly adheres to Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL) for Child Find, mandating that initial evaluations (FIIE) be completed within 45 school days of receiving signed parental consent.

Navigating HPISD Specialized Services

Special education in Highland Park ISD is built on a foundation of rigorous academic standards. Because HPISD is a relatively small district with highly concentrated resources, initial evaluation requests are typically processed directly through the Campus Principal or the Campus Special Education Team Lead at schools such as Bradfield Elementary or Highland Park High School. Once a written request is submitted, the district must provide a Notice of Rights and seek consent to trigger the 45-school-day evaluation window required by Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL).

The district provides a full continuum of placements, ranging from inclusion support to Specialized Classrooms for students needing a modified curriculum. In HPISD, the "HP Way" emphasizes high-fidelity implementation of the Individualized Education Program (IEP). For students with behavioral needs, the district utilizes Board Policy FNG(LOCAL) as the framework for parents to address concerns or disagreements with the ARD committee's decisions. Decisions regarding a change of placement are always made by the ARD Committee, which includes the Campus Administrator serving as the district representative.

For secondary students, Highland Park High School provides specialized transition services focused on post-secondary success in higher education or competitive employment. Parents should ensure that Transition Plans are data-driven and align with the district's high college-readiness benchmarks. If you are navigating a dispute, the first step is always an informal conference with Director Laurie Gagne at the Westchester Drive office before initiating formal mediation or a due process hearing under Texas Education Agency guidelines.
