About Highland Park ISD Special Education

Highland Park ISD (HPISD) is renowned for its tradition of academic excellence and deep community involvement. For parents navigating the special education system in this high-achieving Dallas enclave, the priority is often ensuring that specialized support matches the district's rigorous standard of instruction. HPISD is committed to providing a Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE) to all students with disabilities, focusing on "inclusive excellence" that allows students to access the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS) alongside their peers whenever possible.

A hallmark of Highland Park ISD special education is the district's sophisticated approach to literacy and dyslexia support. Given the community’s high expectations, the district has invested heavily in evidence-based reading interventions and specialized teacher training. Furthermore, HPISD offers a comprehensive range of "Continuum of Services," including speech therapy, occupational therapy, and physical therapy, as well as highly specialized programs for students with autism or significant cognitive disabilities who require a more modified curriculum or life-skills focus.

In recent years, Highland Park ISD has made significant strides in improving its transition planning for students moving from middle school to high school and beyond. The district emphasizes a data-driven ARD process, where student progress is closely monitored to ensure that accommodations and modifications are effectively bridging the gap between a student's current abilities and their educational goals. Parents are encouraged to be active members of the ARD committee, acting as experts on their child's history and needs during the development of the Individualized Education Program (IEP).

What sets Highland Park ISD apart is the local emphasis on the "HP Way"—a commitment to excellence that extends into the special education classroom. Whether your child is just beginning the Child Find evaluation process or is preparing for graduation, you should expect a collaborative, professional environment. However, the complexity of Texas special education law means you must stay informed. Understanding your procedural safeguards and how to effectively advocate during an ARD meeting is essential to ensuring your child receives the full benefit of the resources HPISD has to offer.
