Request a Special Education Evaluation in Highland Park ISD

A step-by-step guide for families in Dallas and the Park Cities to secure a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) under federal and Texas law.

Quick Facts: HPISD Evaluations

  • Child Find: HPISD is legally required to identify and evaluate children with suspected disabilities, including those in private schools or homeschooled.
  • The 15-Day Rule: The district must respond to your written request within 15 school days.
  • No Medical Diagnosis Needed: Testing determines "educational eligibility," which is separate from a clinical diagnosis.
  • IEE Rights: You may request an Independent Educational Evaluation at district expense if you disagree with HPISD's findings.

Common Questions About Highland Park ISD Evaluations

How do I request an evaluation in Highland Park ISD?

Submit a formal written request to your campus principal or the Executive Director of Special Education, Dr. Alicia Miller. Written requests trigger the legal 15-school-day response window.

How long does Highland Park ISD have to complete testing?

Once you sign the consent form, the district has 45 school days to complete the Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE). Note: Summer and school holidays do not count toward these "school days".

What assessments are included in the FIIE?

HPISD assessments typically cover cognitive processing, academic achievement (reading, math, writing), social-emotional health, and any specialized areas like speech or motor skills.

Can the school use RTI to delay an evaluation?

No. HPISD cannot use "Response to Intervention" (RTI) as a reason to delay or deny an evaluation if a disability is suspected.

How did HB 3928 change dyslexia testing in HPISD?

HB 3928 requires that all dyslexia evaluations now be conducted under the IDEA (Special Education) framework, ensuring full legal protections for the student.

How to Request an Evaluation in Highland Park ISD

Step 1: The Written Request

To ensure your request is documented and legally binding, send it to:

Executive Director: Dr. Alicia S. Miller

Phone: 214-780-3000

Address: 7015 Westchester Dr., Dallas, TX 75205

Sample Request Language:

Dear Dr. Miller,

I am writing to formally request a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child, [Child's Name], under the Child Find mandate of IDEA. I am concerned about my child's progress in [Area of Concern].

Please provide me with a Prior Written Notice and consent forms within the 15-school-day timeline required by Texas law.

Step 2: Reviewing the Consent Form

The 45-school-day testing clock only starts after HPISD receives your signed consent. Ensure the "areas of suspected disability" section on the form is comprehensive.

Step 3: Receiving the Report & ARD Meeting

After testing is finished, HPISD has 30 calendar days to hold an Initial ARD meeting. You should receive a copy of the report before this meeting to review the findings.
