How to Request a Special Education Evaluation in Humble ISD

A complete guide to navigating the special education evaluation process for families in Humble, Atascocita, and Kingwood.

Child Find & Your Rights

Under the federal Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA), Humble ISD has a "Child Find" mandate to identify, locate, and evaluate all children with disabilities (birth through age 21) within district boundaries. This responsibility covers all children, including those attending private schools or being homeschooled.

You Have the Right To:

  • Request a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) at any time.
  • Receive a response (Prior Written Notice) from Humble ISD within 15 school days of your written request.
  • Have your child evaluated in all areas of suspected disability.
  • Access a copy of the Procedural Safeguards which outline your legal rights.
  • Request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at public expense if you disagree with the district's results.

No Medical Diagnosis Required

In Humble ISD, you do not need a clinical diagnosis from a doctor to trigger an evaluation. The district evaluates based on educational need—determining if a disability exists and if it specifically requires specialized instruction.

Step-by-Step Evaluation Process in Humble ISD

1

Submit a Written Request

The process formally begins with a written request. If your child is school-aged (3-21), contact the Campus Principal or Educational Diagnostician at your neighborhood school. For children birth through age 2, contact the Special Education department directly.

2

The 15-Day Response Window

Within 15 school days of receiving your request, Humble ISD must provide a Prior Written Notice. This document states whether the district agrees to evaluate or refuses your request with a detailed explanation.

3

Sign Written Consent

If the district moves forward, you must sign a Consent for Evaluation. This authorizes specific assessments. The legal 45-school-day timeline does not begin until the district receives this signed form.

Tip: Keep a copy of the dated consent form to track the 45-school-day deadline.
4

The Evaluation (FIIE)

Trained professionals, including Educational Diagnosticians and LSSPs, will evaluate your child in specific areas. Assessment includes:

  • Intellectual/Cognitive development.
  • Academic levels in core subjects.
  • Social, emotional, and behavioral performance.
  • Dyslexia Screening: Under Texas HB 3928, dyslexia evaluations are now conducted through the special education process, using evidence-based protocols.
5

Initial ARD Meeting

Within 30 calendar days of the completed report, an Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) committee meeting is held. You are a required member. Together, you review the data to determine eligibility and develop an Individualized Education Program (IEP) if needed.

Texas Timeline Requirements (15-45-30)

Humble ISD must follow the standardized Texas 15-45-30 sequence:

15 School Days

From request to Consent or Refusal.

45 School Days

From consent to completed Evaluation.

30 Calendar Days

From Evaluation to ARD Meeting.

Compliance Issues

If Humble ISD exceeds these timelines, they are in violation of state law. Parents should:

  • Email the Campus Principal for an immediate status update.
  • Contact the Humble ISD Special Education Department at 281-641-8410.
  • Utilize SPEDTex, the Texas special education information center, at 1-855-773-3839.

Frequently Asked Questions for Humble Families

Can my child receive an evaluation during the summer?

While the "school day" clock pauses during summer, you can still make a referral by calling the Special Education department at 281-641-8410.

What is an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE)?

If you disagree with Humble ISD's results, you can request an IEE. This is an evaluation done by a professional not employed by the district, paid for by the district.

Does the timeline change for dyslexia?

No. Under HB 3928, dyslexia evaluations follow the same 15-45-30 sequence as all other special education assessments.
