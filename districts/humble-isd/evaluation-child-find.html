Quick Facts: Evaluations in Humble ISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full (Individual) Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, teachers, or school staff can refer a child.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to completed evaluation.
  • Cost: Free to parents; district covers all testing.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting within 30 days of completed evaluation.

Common Questions About Humble ISD Evaluations

How do I request a special education evaluation in Humble ISD?

Submit a written request to your child's campus principal or diagnostician. For school-aged children (3-21), contact the campus directly; for younger children, contact the Special Education department.

Sample language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], DOB [Date], at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Humble ISD?

45 school days maximum for the testing. Once consent is signed, the district has 45 school days to provide a report. This is followed by a 30-calendar-day window to hold the ARD meeting.

What tests are included in a Humble ISD evaluation?

Testing is tailored to suspected disabilities. Common assessments include cognitive development, academic core subjects, and social-emotional performance. Under HB 3928, dyslexia evaluations are also included.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. You do not need a clinical medical diagnosis to trigger an evaluation. Humble ISD evaluates based on educational need and suspected disability categories.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE)?

Yes, if you disagree with the district's results. An IEE is an evaluation by a professional not employed by Humble ISD, paid for at public expense.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

An ARD meeting is held within 30 days. The committee, including you, will review results to determine eligibility and develop an Individualized Education Program (IEP) if services are needed.

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

A FIIE (sometimes called FIE) is a comprehensive assessment to determine if a child qualifies for special education under IDEA. It determines:

  • Does the child have a disability? One of the 13 categories recognized in Texas.
  • Does it impact education? Does the child require specially designed instruction?
  • What supports are needed? Data from the FIIE informs the development of the IEP.

13 Disability Categories in Texas Special Education

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment Specific Learning Disability Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment

Contact your neighborhood school's principal or diagnostician in writing.

I am writing to formally request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child, [Child's Name], DOB [Date], currently in [Grade].

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Humble ISD must provide a response within 15 school days. If they agree, you will sign a Consent for Evaluation.

Step 3: Evaluation Period (45 School Days)

The 45-school-day clock begins once the district receives your signed consent.

The Evaluation Timeline in Humble ISD

Day 1: Parent submits written request.

~15 School Days: District provides consent forms or refusal.

Clock Starts: Parent signs consent; 45-school-day testing window begins.

Day 45: Written evaluation report completed.

Within 30 Days: ARD meeting held to review eligibility.

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Humble ISD uses specialized staff to evaluate cognitive development, academic levels, and behavioral performance.

Cognitive/IQ

Measures intellectual development and problem-solving skills.

Academic

Evaluates reading, writing, and math core competencies.

Social-Emotional

Screens for behavioral and performance health indicators.

After the Evaluation: The ARD Meeting

The Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) committee meeting is held within 30 calendar days of report completion.

✓ Eligible for Special Education

If the committee determines a disability exists and requires specialized instruction, an IEP is developed.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with Humble ISD's results, you may request an IEE at public expense. This is conducted by a professional outside the district.

Humble ISD Evaluation Specifics

Evaluation in the Summer

The school-day clock pauses, but referrals can still be made by calling 281-641-8410.
