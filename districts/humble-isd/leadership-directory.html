Humble ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Humble ISD special education administrators, coordinators, and support staff serving Humble, Atascocita, and Kingwood families.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Who Should I Contact?

For ARD Meeting Questions

Start with your child's Campus Diagnostician or Special Education Teacher.

They manage student-specific IEP goals and day-to-day service delivery.

For Evaluation Requests

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Campus Principal.

Submit a formal written request if you suspect your child has an educational disability requiring specialized instruction.

For Complaints or Disputes

Start: Campus Principal.

Escalate to: Executive Director of Special Education if concerns regarding FAPE are not resolved at the campus level.

For General Questions

Special Education Administration Office (BBTC).

Phone: 281-641-8326.

Address: 10203 Birchridge Drive, Humble, TX 77338.

Executive Leadership

Humble ISD (HISD) serves over 48,000 students in the Northeast Houston area. The Special Education leadership team provides the administrative structure to ensure state and federal compliance across all 47+ campuses.

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]"
  • Always copy the campus principal on formal requests sent to the special education office.
  • Request a "Read Receipt" or written confirmation for time-sensitive evaluation requests.

📝 Documentation

  • Keep a binder with your child's most recent FIE and IEP documents.
  • If you receive a verbal commitment during an ARD meeting, ensure it is written into the "Deliberations" section.
  • Follow up every phone call with a brief email: "Per our conversation today..."
