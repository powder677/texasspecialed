Humble ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Humble ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators serving families in Humble, Kingwood, and Atascocita.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

Humble ISD serves a diverse student population of over 48,000 in the Northeast Houston area. The Special Education department is focused on providing inclusive environments and specialized support that allow every student to thrive. The executive leadership team is responsible for district-wide compliance with the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA) and provides the ultimate level of support for parents navigating the ARD process.

Key Coordinators

In Humble ISD, program coordinators manage the day-to-day operations of specialized services. They serve as a vital link between the central administration and campus-level teams, ensuring that instructional strategies and behavioral supports are implemented effectively for students across the district.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Oversees the identification and specialized instruction for students with dyslexia. They ensure Humble ISD remains compliant with HB 3928 and the latest Texas Dyslexia Handbook requirements.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Provides support and specialized training for staff working with students on the autism spectrum. They assist in developing individualized strategies and ensuring the Autism Supplement is addressed in ARD meetings.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Manages the district's diagnosticians and LSSPs. This office oversees the Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) process and ensures that evaluation timelines are strictly met.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

Handles the secure transfer and release of special education records. This is the primary point of contact for parents who need copies of their child's IEP or ARD documents for outside providers.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the Humble ISD Special Education office at 281-641-8000 for a referral to the administrator overseeing your child's campus cluster.

Who to Contact When

Navigating special education in a large district like Humble ISD requires knowing which office to approach for specific needs. In Texas, all programming and placement decisions are made through the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you suspect your child has a disability and requires specialized support, you should submit a formal, written request for an evaluation to the district’s special education administration.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Your child's ARD case manager or campus diagnostician

For student-specific questions regarding progress, accommodations, or to schedule a new ARD meeting, your child's home campus is always the first point of contact.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

If you are moving or need your child's educational file for a private medical evaluation, contact the records department directly to request a complete copy of the student's file.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If issues cannot be resolved at the campus level with the Principal or Diagnostician, the Executive Director of Special Education provides the next tier of administrative oversight.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

Recent changes to Texas law mean that dyslexia is now served under the special education framework. Contact this office if you have questions about transitioning from a 504 plan to specialized instruction.
