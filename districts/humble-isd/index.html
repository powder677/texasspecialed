About Humble ISD Special Education

Humble ISD is recognized for its innovative approach to special education, particularly in transition services. The district's department, referred to as "Educational Support Services," operates with a philosophy that emphasizes inclusive practices and community integration. Unlike many districts that rely solely on a central director, Humble ISD utilizes a robust network of Campus Specialists and ARD Specialists who are stationed directly at schools to provide immediate support to families and staff.

A flagship program within Humble ISD is Mosaic, the district’s 18+ transition program. Located near the Community Learning Center, Mosaic is designed for young adults with disabilities who have completed their high school credit requirements but need further support in independent living and employability. The program is unique in its design, often utilizing community partnerships to provide real-world vocational training rather than simulated classroom tasks. If your child is approaching graduation age, discussing a transition to Mosaic at your ARD meeting is a critical step.

For our youngest learners, Humble ISD offers strong Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) services. The district’s "Child Find" process for preschoolers is streamlined through their Early Childhood Department. If you are moving to the district with a child aged 3-5 who has a disability, you will likely interface with the assessment team at the **Instructional Support Center** before being assigned to a campus. The district is also aggressive about "In-Home Training" assessments for students with autism, recognizing that behavior support often needs to extend beyond the school day.

Navigating the chain of command in Humble ISD is relatively straightforward. If you have an issue, your first contact should be the campus ARD Specialist. If that does not resolve the concern, the district employs "Cluster Coordinators" who oversee groups of schools (feeder patterns). This localized leadership model helps resolve disputes faster than waiting for a response from the central administration building on Birchridge Drive.
