Navigating Humble ISD Specialized Services

Humble ISD is recognized for its innovative approach to special education, particularly in transition services. The district's department, referred to as "Educational Support Services" (ESS), operates with a philosophy that emphasizes inclusive practices and community integration. Unlike many districts that rely solely on a central director, Humble ISD utilizes a robust network of Campus Specialists and ARD Specialists who are stationed directly at schools to provide immediate support to families and staff.

A flagship program within Humble ISD is Mosaic, the district’s 18+ transition program. Currently utilizing the Community Learning Center, Mosaic is moving to a permanent, purpose-built facility off Timber Forest Drive in Fall 2025. This program is designed for young adults with disabilities who have completed their high school credit requirements but need further support in independent living and employability. The program utilizes community partnerships to provide real-world vocational training rather than simulated classroom tasks. If your child is approaching graduation age, discussing a transition to Mosaic at your ARD meeting is a critical step.

For our youngest learners, Humble ISD offers strong Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) services. The district’s "Child Find" process is overseen by Dr. Ashley Clark at the Eggers Instructional Support Center on Magnolia Cove Drive. If you are moving to the district with a child aged 3-5 who has a disability, you will likely interface with the assessment team here before being assigned to a campus. Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must complete the Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) within 45 school days of receiving written parental consent.

Navigating the chain of command in Humble ISD is relatively straightforward. If you have an issue, your first contact should be the campus ARD Specialist or Principal. If that does not resolve the concern, the district employs "Cluster Coordinators" who oversee groups of schools (feeder patterns). This localized leadership model helps resolve disputes faster than waiting for a response from the central administration building on Birchridge Drive. Formal grievances are handled under Board Policy FNG(LOCAL).
