Navigating the Humble ISD Special Education System

Navigating special education in Humble ISD requires an understanding of their decentralized support model. While the central office is at the BBTC (Board, Business, and Technology Center) on Birchridge Drive, the district utilizes specialized Campus ARD Specialists at schools like Atascocita High School and Kingwood Park High to facilitate meetings. Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must complete evaluations within 45 school days; however, Humble ISD guidelines emphasize that the process begins officially only when written consent is submitted to the campus principal or the Instructional Support Center assessment team.

The district's flagship Mosaic Program provides transition services for students aged 18-22, focusing on employability and independent living. Decisions regarding placement into Mosaic or specialized programs like SPEAK (for students with autism) are determined by the ARD committee. If a dispute arises, parents should first reference Board Policy FNG(LOCAL), which outlines the multi-level grievance process starting with the Cluster Coordinator assigned to your school's feeder pattern before escalating to the Executive Director.

For early learners, the Child Find mandate is executed through the Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) assessment team. Parents of preschoolers (ages 3-5) should contact the district's evaluation lead directly to initiate the 60-day timeline (30 days for evaluation plus 30 days for the initial ARD). Humble ISD also offers specialized "In-Home Training" assessments for students on the autism spectrum, a service that must be data-driven and requested during a formal ARD committee meeting.
