Answer: In Humble ISD, an ARD meeting is the legally required Texas special education meeting where parents and district staff determine a student's eligibility, services, and placement. Parents in Humble ISD must receive at least five school days’ written notice, are considered equal members of the committee, and have the right to audio record all proceedings under Texas law.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to Humble ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: ARD Meetings in Humble ISD

  • Notice Timeline: Minimum 5 school days' written notice required.
  • Recording Rights: Parents may audio record; notification is standard practice.
  • Parent Role: Mandatory, equal member of the ARD committee.
  • Annual Requirement: Required at least once every 12 months.
  • Initial ARD Timing: Must occur within 30 days of a completed FIE.

How ARD Meetings in Humble ISD May Differ From Other Districts

Humble ISD utilizes specific organizational structures that parents should be aware of during the ARD process:

  • Facilitated ARD Approach: For complex cases, Humble ISD often utilizes a "Facilitated ARD" model, where a neutral party manages communication to maintain focus on student data and goals.
  • Virtual Accessibility: The district maintains robust virtual meeting options via Zoom or Microsoft Teams, allowing for flexible parent participation from home or work.
  • Diagnostic Staffing: In Humble ISD, the "Diagnostician" (often called the 'Diag') typically leads the interpretation of Full Individual Evaluation (FIE) data during the meeting.

Common Humble ISD ARD Questions

Can I bring an advocate to a Humble ISD ARD?

Yes, parents have the legal right to bring advocates or experts to any Humble ISD ARD meeting. If bringing an attorney, Humble ISD typically requires advance notice so their legal counsel can also attend.

What happens if I disagree with the ARD committee?

If consensus is not reached, a "10-day recess" is typically offered to allow the committee to gather more data before reconvening. Parents may sign "Agree" to specific parts of the IEP while noting formal disagreement on others.

How to Prepare for an ARD Meeting in Humble ISD

1. Review Current Data

Collect recent report cards, progress reports, and STAAR or MAP scores to see if current goals are being met.

2. Request a Draft IEP

Request a "Draft IEP" from your Humble ISD case manager at least one week before the meeting to avoid seeing proposed goals for the first time during the ARD.

3. Document Concerns

Write down your top 3-5 concerns and specific questions for the diagnostician or teachers to ensure they are addressed.
