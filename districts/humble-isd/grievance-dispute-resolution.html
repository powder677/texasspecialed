File a Special Education Complaint in Humble ISD

Serving families in Humble, Kingwood, and Atascocita. Understand your rights and the local escalation process for special education disagreements.

Quick Answer: To file a special education complaint in Humble ISD, start with your campus ARD chair or principal. If unresolved, you must file a formal written grievance (Policy FNG Local) with the principal within 15 district business days. For legal violations, you can file directly with the Texas Education Agency (TEA) within one year. Humble ISD's special education department is led by Chief Henry Phipps and Director Shawna Hunt.

Humble ISD Local Grievance (FNG LOCAL)

If informal meetings with campus staff fail, Humble ISD uses a formal three-level process:

Level 1: Campus Principal

Submit a formal complaint form to the principal. Timeline: Within 15 business days of the incident. The principal must hold a conference and provide a written response.

Level 2: Superintendent’s Designee

If unsatisfied, appeal to the Superintendent's office within 10 business days of the Level 1 response.

Level 3: Board of Trustees

The final local step is a hearing before the Humble ISD School Board.
