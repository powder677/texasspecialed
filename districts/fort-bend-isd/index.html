Fort Bend ISD Special Education Resources & Support

Quick Answer: In Fort Bend ISD, special education services are overseen by Executive Director Deidra Lyons-Lewis at the Lexington Blvd Administration Building. The district adheres to Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), requiring Full Individual and Initial Evaluations (FIIE) to be completed within 45 school days of receiving written parental consent.

Serving 8,116 students across Sugar Land and Missouri City with specialized instruction and ARD committee leadership.

Navigating the Fort Bend ISD Continuum of Care

Fort Bend ISD provides a diverse array of specialized placements tailored to student needs. For those requiring the most therapeutic environment, the district utilizes the M.R. Wood Center for Learning. On comprehensive campuses like Clements High School or First Colony Middle School, you will find localized programs including FLASH (Functional Living and School Health) and ABC (Academic Behavior Class). These programs are managed by specific Program Managers; for instance, any concerns regarding behavioral data should be directed to the Program Manager for Behavior before escalating to the Director level.

Under Board Policy FNG(LOCAL), parents have a structured right to resolve grievances. Fort Bend ISD emphasizes the "Chain of Command," starting with the Campus Assistant Principal who typically serves as the ARD Administrator. If the ARD committee cannot reach a consensus, the district’s Operating Procedures encourage a 10-day recess to gather additional data before reconvening, a practice mandated by 19 TAC § 89.1050(g).

The district’s Special Education Parent Advisory Committee (SEPAC) provides a platform for West Fort Bend families to influence district-wide training. This committee is a vital resource for understanding how LRE (Least Restrictive Environment) decisions are made in Sugar Land, ensuring that "Change of Placement" moves—such as moving from inclusion to a self-contained FLASH classroom—are supported by rigorous documentation and current evaluation data.
