How to Request a Special Education Evaluation in Fort Bend ISD

Navigating the evaluation process for students in Sugar Land, Missouri City, and surrounding Fort Bend ISD communities.

Child Find & Your Rights

Under federal and state law, Fort Bend ISD maintains a "Child Find" mandate to identify and evaluate any child residing within the district who is suspected of having a disability. This includes children in private schools, those who are homeschooled, and even infants and toddlers through coordination with early intervention programs.

You Have the Right To:

  • Request a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for your child at any time.
  • Receive a response from Fort Bend ISD within 15 school days of your written request.
  • Review all evaluation data and participate in the decision-making process.
  • Receive "Prior Written Notice" if the district refuses your request for evaluation.
  • Access an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) if you disagree with the district's results.

Wait, No Diagnosis?

Correct. In Fort Bend ISD, you do not need a clinical diagnosis from a doctor to trigger an evaluation. The school evaluates to see if the child meets one of the 13 disability categories under IDEA and if that disability creates a need for specialized instruction.

Step-by-Step Evaluation Process in Fort Bend ISD

1

Submit a Written Request

The process starts when you submit a written request to the school principal or the special education department. While a verbal request is valid, written requests provide a clear paper trail for the 15-school-day response deadline.

2

The 15-Day Review

Within 15 school days of receiving your request, Fort Bend ISD must either give you the opportunity to sign consent for an evaluation or provide a written notice explaining why they refuse to evaluate.

3

Sign Written Consent

If the district moves forward, you will receive a Notice of Evaluation and a Consent for Evaluation form. The evaluation cannot start until you sign and return this form.

Note: Signing consent for evaluation is NOT the same as signing consent for services. You are only agreeing to the testing.

Timeline Alert: The 45-school-day deadline for the district to finish the evaluation starts the day they receive your signed consent.
4

The Evaluation (FIIE)

Fort Bend ISD will conduct a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation. This is a multi-disciplinary assessment that may include:

  • Intellectual/Cognitive assessment.
  • Academic achievement in reading, writing, and math.
  • Language and communication skills.
  • Physical and motor abilities.
  • Behavioral and emotional data.
  • Dyslexia: Following Texas HB 3928, dyslexia is evaluated as a specific learning disability under the IDEA framework.
5

Initial ARD Meeting

Once the evaluation is written, Fort Bend ISD must convene an Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) committee meeting within 30 calendar days. You are a required member of this committee. The committee will use the evaluation data to determine if your child qualifies for special education.

Texas Timeline Requirements (15-45-30)

The "Special Education Clock" in Texas follows a specific 15-45-30 sequence:

15 School Days

From written request to Consent or Refusal.

45 School Days

From signed consent to completed Evaluation.

30 Calendar Days

From completed Evaluation to ARD Meeting.

What if Fort Bend ISD Misses a Deadline?

If the 45-school-day window is exceeded, the district is out of compliance with state law. Parents should:

  • Email the Campus Evaluation Coordinator and Principal for an immediate status update.
  • Contact the FBISD Special Education Department at 281-634-1143.
  • Consult with SPEDTex, the Texas Special Education Information Center.

Common Questions About Evaluations in Fort Bend ISD

Can my child be evaluated during the summer?

The "school day" clock generally pauses during the summer break. However, if you sign consent late in the spring, the district may have specific requirements to complete it, or the timeline will resume on the first day of the new school year.

What is an "IEE"?

An Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) is an evaluation conducted by a qualified examiner who is not employed by Fort Bend ISD. You have the right to request an IEE at public expense if you disagree with the district's evaluation.

My child is in 504. Do I need a new evaluation for SpEd?

Yes. While 504 evaluations provide data, eligibility for special education (IDEA) requires a more comprehensive Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) to determine the need for specialized instruction.
