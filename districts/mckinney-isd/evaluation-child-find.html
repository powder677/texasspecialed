Quick Facts: Evaluations in Prosper ISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full Individual Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, teachers, or staff can refer a child.
  • Timeline: 45 school days from signed consent to report.
  • Cost: Free to parents; district covers testing.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting within 30 days of completed report.

Request a Special Education Evaluation in Prosper ISD

Step-by-step guidance for Prosper families. Learn how to initiate a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE), understand timelines, and advocate for your child in Prosper Independent School District.

Common Questions About Prosper ISD Evaluations

```

How do I request a special education evaluation in Prosper ISD?

Submit a written request to your campus principal and the Special Education department.. For students not yet enrolled in LISD (private or homeschool), email special-education@prosper-isd.net.

Sample language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], DOB [Date], currently enrolled at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Prosper ISD?

45 school days maximum for testing.. Once consent is signed, the district has 45 school days to complete testing. An ARD meeting is then scheduled within 30 calendar days to review results.

What tests are included in a Prosper ISD evaluation?

Testing depends on the suspected disability.. Common assessments include cognitive testing (WISC), academic achievement (Woodcock-Johnson), speech-language samples, and social-emotional scales.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. You do not need a doctor's diagnosis to request testing. Under Child Find, Prosper ISD must evaluate any child suspected of a disability that impacts their education.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE)?

Yes, if you disagree with the district's results.. An IEE is an evaluation by a third party at public expense. Contact the Director of Special Education to initiate this request.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

ARD meeting within 30 calendar days.. The committee reviews the FIE report to determine eligibility and (if eligible) develop an IEP. You must receive the report at least 5 days before the meeting.

```

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

A FIIE (sometimes called FIE) is a comprehensive assessment to determine eligibility for special education. It assesses:

  • Disability Identification: Does the child meet criteria for one of the 13 IDEA categories?.
  • Educational Impact: Does the disability require specialized instruction?.
  • Service Recommendations: What supports are needed for student success?.
```

13 Disability Categories in Texas Special Education

Autism Deaf-Blindness Deafness Emotional Disturbance Hearing Impairment Intellectual Disability Multiple Disabilities Orthopedic Impairment Other Health Impairment Specific Learning Disability Speech/Language Impairment Traumatic Brain Injury Visual Impairment
```

How to Request an Evaluation in Prosper ISD

```

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Email the campus principal or the Executive Director of Special Education.

Where to Send Your Request:

Method: Email / Mail

Contact: Lecya Janak, Executive Director of Special Education

Email: lgjanak@prosper-isd.net

Address: 605 E. 7th St., Prosper, TX 75078

Sample Evaluation Request Letter


```

[Date]

Lecya Janak
Executive Director of Special Education
Prosper ISD

Dear Ms. Janak,

I am writing to formally request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child, [Child's Name], DOB [Date], currently in [Grade] at [School].

I am concerned about [academic/behavioral challenges]. I believe they may have a disability that requires special education services under IDEA. Please provide me with consent forms as soon as possible.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
Download Fillable Template
```

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Within 15 school days, the district provides consent forms or a written refusal explaining why testing isn't warranted.

Step 3: Evaluation Period (45 School Days)

Once you sign consent, testing must be completed within 45 school days.

Step 4: Receive Report & ARD Meeting

You must receive the report at least 5 days before the ARD meeting, which must occur within 30 days of report completion.

```

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Assessments are conducted by licensed specialists:

Cognitive/IQ

Who: LSSP or Diagnostician.

Measures: Problem-solving and reasoning.

Academic

Who: Educational Diagnostician.

Measures: Reading, math, and writing vs. grade level.

Speech

Who: Speech-Language Pathologist.

Measures: Articulation and language skills.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with Prosper ISD's testing, you can request an IEE at public expense. Contact Lecya Janak to submit a formal disagreement and IEE request.
