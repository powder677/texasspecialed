Understanding the McKinney ISD Special Populations Model

McKinney ISD's special education department is distinctive for its centralized Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE) model. Unlike districts that scatter preschool programs across every elementary campus, McKinney ISD houses the majority of its 3-to-5-year-old services at the Lawson Early Childhood School. This allows for concentrated resources focused entirely on early developmental outcomes.

Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must complete a Full Individual Evaluation (FIE) within 45 school days of receiving written parental consent. For families with behavioral concerns, the district utilizes the P.O.W.E.R. Parenting series, providing specific strategies that align with the Structured Teach classrooms and Functional Academics environments utilized at campuses like Evans Middle School and Faubion Middle School.

At the secondary level, McKinney ISD high schools employ a "Co-Department Chair" structure. One chair focuses on high-fidelity instruction while the other manages legal compliance, ensuring that Individualized Education Programs (IEPs) are both academically rigorous and procedurally sound. If a dispute arises, the district facilitates the process via Board Policy FNG(LOCAL), which encourages resolution at the campus level before escalating to the Greer Annex administration.
