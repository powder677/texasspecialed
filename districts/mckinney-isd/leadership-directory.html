Direct contacts for special education services, ARD meetings, evaluations, and administrative support in McKinney Consolidated ISD.

Who Should I Contact?

For ARD Meeting Questions

Start with your child's ARD case manager or the campus special education coordinator.

Escalation: Contact the Executive Director if campus-level issues remain unresolved.

For Evaluation Requests

Key District Coordinators

McKinney ISD uses specialized coordinators to manage day-to-day operations and high-stakes program mandates:

Dyslexia Coordinator

Focus: Compliance with HB 3928, evidence-based instruction, and ARD specialist participation.

Contact: 469-302-4000

Autism Specialist

Focus: Training staff on sensory strategies, behavioral interventions, and the Texas Autism Supplement.

Contact: 469-302-4000

Evaluation Coordinator

Focus: Monitoring Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) timelines (45-school-day window).

Contact: 469-302-4000

Records Clerk

Focus: Handling secure record requests and transfers under FERPA regulations.

Contact: 469-302-4000

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [Campus]".
  • Include your child's name and DOB in every email for quick record retrieval.
  • Request written summaries of any verbal agreements made during phone calls or meetings.

📝 Chain of Command

  • Level 1: Child's Teacher or Case Manager.
  • Level 2: Campus Diagnostician or Assistant Principal.
  • Level 3: Campus Principal.
  • Level 4: District Office / Executive Director.
