File a Special Education Complaint in McKinney ISD

Navigate the grievance and dispute resolution process in McKinney Independent School District. Learn your rights under Board Policy FNG (Local) and the pathways for state-level escalation.

Quick Answer: To file a special education complaint in McKinney ISD, begin with your campus principal or the Special Populations Department. If informal resolution fails, you must file a formal Level One Student/Parent Resolution Form under Board Policy FNG (Local) within 15 district business days of the incident. MISD typically provides a written decision within 10 business days after the grievance conference.

Quick Facts: Dispute Resolution in McKinney ISD

  • Leadership: Brynne Riley, Executive Director of Special Populations.
  • District Phone: (469) 302-4000.
  • Grievance Policy: Board Policy FNG (Local).
  • TEA Limit: One year from the date of violation.
  • OCR Limit: 180 days from the date of discrimination.

Common Questions About MISD Special Education Complaints

How do I file a grievance with McKinney ISD?

To file a formal grievance, you must submit the "Student/Parent Resolution Form - Level One" to the campus principal or the Office of Legal Affairs at 1 Duvall Street. This must be done within 15 district business days. A conference will be scheduled, and a written Level 1 Decision will be issued within 10 business days of that meeting.

What happens if I disagree with a Level 1 Decision in McKinney ISD?

If you are unsatisfied with the initial decision, you can appeal to Level 2 (Superintendent's designee) and then Level 3 (Board of Trustees). Each appeal stage must be filed within 10 district business days of receiving the previous decision.

Can I file a complaint directly with the TEA?

Yes. If you believe McKinney ISD has violated a state or federal special education law, you can email a completed TEA Model Complaint Form to spedcomplaints@tea.texas.gov. You are required to send a copy to the MISD Superintendent at the same time. TEA investigates and issues a decision within 60 days.

Recommended Escalation Pathway in MISD

Step 1

Informal Campus Resolution

Attempt to resolve issues through your child's ARD chair, campus principal, or by contacting the Special Populations department at (469) 302-4000.

Step 2

Formal Level One Grievance

Submit written forms to 1 Duvall Street within 15 district business days. Request a date-stamped receipt if hand-delivering.

Step 3

TEA Complaint or Mediation

If local resolution fails, file with the TEA for legal violations or request a free mediator to facilitate a compromise.
