Answer: In McKinney ISD, an ARD meeting is the legally required Texas special education process where parents and district staff determine a student's eligibility, services, and placement. Parents must receive at least five school days’ written notice, maintain the right to audio record the meeting, and serve as equal members of the decision-making committee.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to McKinney ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: McKinney ISD ARD Meetings

  • Notice Timeline: Minimum of 5 school days’ written notice is required.
  • Recording Rights: Parents may audio record the meeting under Texas law.
  • Parent Role: You are a legally required, equal member of the ARD committee.
  • Annual Requirement: The committee must meet at least once per year to update the IEP.
  • Initial ARD Timing: Must occur within 30 calendar days of a completed evaluation report.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does McKinney ISD provide draft IEPs before the meeting?

McKinney ISD typically provides draft goals and data for parent review prior to the scheduled ARD meeting. While not strictly required by law, this practice allows parents to prepare questions and feedback in advance.

What if I disagree with the McKinney ISD committee's decision?

If consensus is not reached, McKinney ISD must offer a 10-day recess to allow both parties to gather more information. Parents may sign "Disagree" on the document and pursue dispute resolution options if necessary.

How do I record an ARD meeting in McKinney ISD?

Under Texas law, you have the right to audio record an ARD meeting. McKinney ISD policy requests that parents notify the campus facilitator 48 hours in advance if they intend to record or bring an attorney.

How to Prepare for a McKinney ISD ARD Meeting

Step 1: Request Draft Data

Ask your McKinney ISD case manager for draft goals and PLAAFPs at least 5 school days before the meeting.

Step 2: Review Parent Portal Data

Check recent progress reports and work samples via the McKinney ISD Parent Portal (HAC) to identify any areas of concern.

Step 3: Draft Your Concerns

Write a formal "Parent Concerns" statement that you want included verbatim in the official ARD deliberations.

How ARD Meetings in McKinney ISD May Differ From Other Districts

McKinney ISD utilizes specific organizational structures that parents should be aware of during the ARD process:

  • Area Coordinators: For complex cases, McKinney ISD often involves central office Area Coordinators who provide oversight to ensure campus compliance across the district.
  • Collaborative "Brief" ARDs: McKinney ISD frequently uses "Brief ARDs" for minor updates to accommodations or services, which can be faster than a full annual review.
  • Campus Facilitator Role: Most McKinney campuses have a dedicated staff member to manage the ARD calendar, ensuring that parent notifications are sent exactly in accordance with district timelines.
