Common Questions About Frisco ISD Dispute Resolution

How do I file a special education complaint in Frisco ISD?

Exhaust informal steps first. Contact the teacher, then the assistant principal, and finally the principal. If these conversations do not resolve the issue, contact the School Leadership Department. Formal grievances are then filed through the online Request Center or by emailing complaintresolution@friscoisd.org. FISD assigns a tracking number to each submission for administrative review.

Who is the Chief of Specialized Learning in FISD?

Dr. Garrett Jackson serves as the Chief Specialized Learning Services Officer. His department oversees the implementation of instructional and related services across all FISD campuses, including centralized classroom locations.

What is the timeline for a TEA complaint?

One calendar year. A TEA complaint must allege a violation of IDEA or state rules that occurred within one year of the filing date. TEA has 60 calendar days to investigate and issue a decision. In Frisco ISD, parents are encouraged to bring concerns to an ARD meeting before filing with TEA.

Frisco ISD Complaint System Details

Does FISD require informal resolution?

Yes. Frisco ISD specifically instructs parents to use the formal complaint process only after completing informal steps with campus and district administrators. This process is structured to prioritize immediate dialogue at the school site before escalating to legal or state-level procedures.

How are records handled during a dispute?

If you need special education records to support your complaint, you must submit a request to the student's campus or through the Special Education Records Clerk at 469-633-6900. Records are retained for a limited time after services end.
