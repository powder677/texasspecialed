Child Find & Your Rights

Under the federal Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA), Frisco ISD has a legal "Child Find" obligation to identify, locate, and evaluate all children with disabilities within district boundaries. This includes children attending private schools or those who are homeschooled.

You Have the Right To:

  • Request a Full Individual and Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for your child at any time.
  • Receive a response (Prior Written Notice) within 15 school days of your written request.
  • Expect an evaluation in all areas of suspected disability, including academics, behavior, and motor skills.
  • Provide outside medical or clinical data for the district to consider.
  • Request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) at district expense if you disagree with their results.

No Diagnosis Needed

You do not need a formal medical diagnosis (such as ADHD or Autism) to trigger an evaluation in Frisco ISD. The district assesses for "educational eligibility," which focuses on whether a disability exists and if it creates a need for specialized instruction.

Step-by-Step Evaluation Process in Frisco ISD

1

Submit a Written Request

For students currently enrolled, submit your request to the Campus Principal or Counselor. If your child is not yet school-aged or is in private school, contact the Special Education Department directly.

2

Review & Notice

Within 15 school days, Frisco ISD must provide a Prior Written Notice. This document will state if they agree to evaluate or if they are refusing your request with a specific explanation.

3

Written Consent

If the district moves forward, you must sign a Consent for Evaluation. The legal "clock" does not start until the district receives this signed document.

Timeline Alert: Frisco ISD has 45 school days from this point to complete the evaluation report.
4

The Evaluation

Specialists such as Educational Diagnosticians or LSSPs (Licensed Specialists in School Psychology) will observe and test your child. This includes:

  • Cognitive and processing abilities.
  • Academic levels in core subjects.
  • Social-emotional and behavioral health.
  • HB 3928 Compliance: Dyslexia is now evaluated as a disability under the special education framework in Texas.
5

Initial ARD Meeting

Within 30 calendar days of the report being finished, an ARD meeting is held to discuss eligibility. You will help decide if your child requires an Individualized Education Program (IEP).

Texas Timeline (15-45-30)

Frisco ISD must adhere to the standardized Texas evaluation sequence:

15 School Days

From request to Consent or Refusal.

45 School Days

From consent to completed Evaluation.

30 Calendar Days

From Evaluation to ARD Meeting.

Compliance Issues

If Frisco ISD exceeds these timelines, they are in violation of state law. Parents should:

  • Email the Campus Evaluation Coordinator for a status update.
  • Contact the District Special Education Leadership.
  • Utilize SPEDTex for additional guidance.

Frisco ISD Evaluation FAQ

Can my child receive an evaluation if they are in private school?

Yes. Under Child Find, Frisco ISD is responsible for evaluating all students within its boundaries, regardless of the type of school they attend.

What is an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE)?

If you disagree with Frisco ISD's evaluation, you can request an IEE. This is an evaluation done by a professional not employed by the district, paid for by the district.

Does the timeline change for dyslexia?

No. Under HB 3928, dyslexia evaluations follow the same 15-45-30 school day timeline as all other special education assessments.
