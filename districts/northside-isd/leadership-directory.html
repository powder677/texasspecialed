Northside ISD Special Education Leadership & Contact Directory

Direct contacts for special education services, ARD meetings, evaluations, and support in Northside ISD, serving families across Northwest San Antonio and Bexar County.

Who Should I Contact?

For ARD Meeting Questions

Start with your child's Teacher/Case Manager or the Campus Special Education Coordinator.

If unresolved: Contact the Area Special Education Coordinator responsible for your specific campus cluster.

For Evaluation Requests

Contact: Northside ISD Assessment Coordinator

Phone: 210-397-8500

Email: special.education@nisd.net

For Complaints or Disputes

Start: Campus Principal

Escalate to: Executive Director of Special Education

Campus Special Education Contacts

Northside ISD utilizes a campus-based model for daily ARD management. Your first point of contact should always be the staff members directly serving your student.

Staff to Request at Campus Level:

  • Case Manager: The teacher responsible for your student's IEP.
  • Campus Diagnostician: Manages the ARD paperwork and testing.
  • Campus Principal: Oversees all staff and student safety at the school.

NISD operates over 120 campuses. For a specific campus coordinator's email, use the NISD Staff Directory or call the main office at 210-397-8500.

Tips for Effective Communication

📧 Email Best Practices

  • Use a clear subject line: "ARD Request - [Student Name] - [ID Number]"
  • Always include your child's legal name and campus.
  • Request a "Read Receipt" for sensitive requests like initial evaluations.
  • Follow up verbal agreements with an email stating: "Per our conversation today..."

📞 Phone Contact

  • Office hours: Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM.
  • If leaving a voicemail, state your name, student's name, and a specific concern.
  • Log the date and time of all calls made to the district office.

📝 Documentation

  • Save all IEP documents and Progress Reports in a digital or physical folder.
  • Keep "Prior Written Notice" (PWN) documents for any service changes.
  • If hand-delivering requests to 5900 Evers Rd, ask for a date-stamped copy.
