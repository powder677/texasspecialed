Northside ISD Special Education Leadership & Contacts

Direct contact information for Northside ISD special education staff, coordinators, and administrators serving families in the San Antonio area.

Last updated: February 2026 | Report outdated information

Executive Leadership

As the largest school district in the San Antonio area, Northside ISD (NISD) maintains a robust Special Education Department to support over 100,000 students. The leadership team is responsible for ensuring that all students with disabilities receive a Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE) in accordance with state and federal law.

Key Coordinators

In a district of Northside's size, the central office is structured to provide specialized support for various programs. Most parent concerns regarding specific services or evaluation timelines are addressed by these program-specific coordinators who oversee campus-level implementation.

Dyslexia Coordinator

Contact main office

Oversees dyslexia identification and services under HB 3928. They ensure that students with dyslexia receive appropriate specially designed instruction (SDI) through the special education framework.

Autism Specialist

Contact main office

Provides support for students on the autism spectrum, focusing on behavioral interventions, communication supports, and the implementation of autism-specific supplements in the ARD process.

Evaluation Coordinator

Contact main office

Coordinates the team of Licensed Specialists in School Psychology (LSSPs) and Educational Diagnosticians. They track compliance with FIIE (Full and Individual Initial Evaluation) timelines.

Records Clerk

Contact main office

The primary point of contact for obtaining copies of your child's IEP, ARD paperwork, or evaluation reports when transferring into or out of Northside ISD.

Note: If specific coordinator names are not listed, contact the main special education office at 210-397-8500 for referral to the appropriate person for your child's specific campus or program.

Who to Contact When

Navigating the special education system in a large district can feel overwhelming. In Texas, the ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) process is the vehicle for all special education decisions. Use this guide to find the right person for your needs.

For Initial Evaluations

Contact: Evaluation Coordinator or Executive Director

If you suspect your child has a learning disability or needs specialized support, submit a written request for a Full and Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) to the district office.

Learn more about the evaluation process →

For ARD Meeting Questions

Contact: Campus Diagnostician or Case Manager

For questions regarding your child’s specific classroom accommodations or progress on IEP goals, always start at the campus level where services are delivered.

Prepare for your ARD meeting →

For IEP Records Requests

Contact: Records Clerk

When switching to a private school or moving out of the San Antonio area, the Records Clerk ensures your child's legal special education documents are transferred securely.

For Complaints or Grievances

Contact: Executive Director

If concerns cannot be resolved with the campus principal or the special education department's area coordinator, the Executive Director oversees the formal grievance process.

View formal grievance process →

For Dyslexia Services (HB 3928)

Contact: Dyslexia Coordinator

With recent changes in Texas law, dyslexia is now managed primarily through special education. If your child is transitioning from a 504 plan, contact this office for guidance.
