Answer: In Northside ISD, an ARD meeting is the legally required Texas special education process where parents and San Antonio district staff determine a student's eligibility, services, and placement. Parents must receive at least five school days’ written notice, maintain the right to audio record the meeting, and serve as equal members of the decision-making committee.

This page describes ARD procedures specific to Northside ISD and may not reflect practices in other Texas school districts.

Quick Facts: Northside ISD ARD Meetings

  • Notice Timeline: Minimum of 5 school days’ written notice is required.
  • Recording Rights: Parents may audio record the meeting (Texas is a "one-party consent" state).
  • Parent Role: You are a legally required, equal member of the ARD committee.
  • Annual Requirement: The committee must meet at least once per year.
  • Initial ARD Timing: Must occur within 30 calendar days of a completed Full Individual Evaluation (FIE).

Common Northside ISD ARD Questions

Who attends an ARD meeting at a Northside ISD campus?

The committee typically includes the parent, special education teacher, general education teacher, a district representative (usually a campus administrator), and an evaluator. Northside ISD also frequently utilizes dedicated ARD Facilitators or Special Education Coordinators to manage documentation.

Can I request an ARD meeting in Northside ISD?

Parents in Northside ISD may request an ARD meeting at any time to address specific concerns regarding their child's IEP. The district must respond to your request by scheduling the meeting or providing written notice of refusal.

Does Northside ISD provide draft IEPs before the meeting?

While not strictly required by law, Northside ISD staff typically provide draft goals and PLAAFPs for parent review prior to the meeting. This practice is intended to allow for a more collaborative discussion during the formal committee meeting.

How to Prepare for a Northside ISD ARD Meeting

Step 1: Review Current Data

Review the current IEP, progress reports, and recent work samples from your child's NISD campus at least 2 weeks before the meeting.

Step 2: Document Concerns

Create a list of your top concerns and draft a parent statement to be included in the official ARD deliberations.

Step 3: Request Drafts

Request a draft of the proposed IEP from your campus ARD facilitator one week before the meeting to prepare questions.

How ARD Meetings in Northside ISD May Differ From Other Districts

As San Antonio's largest district, Northside ISD utilizes specific organizational structures that parents should note:

  • ARD Facilitator Model: NISD often employs dedicated campus-based ARD Facilitators whose primary role is to ensure procedural compliance and documentation, distinct from the student's teacher.
  • Transition Specialization: For students aged 14+, Northside ISD frequently involves Transition Coordinators earlier than smaller districts to help with post-secondary goal setting.
  • Evaluation Capacity: Due to its size, NISD may have rotating evaluators (Diagnosticians or LSSPs) who cover multiple campuses, meaning the evaluator at your ARD may not work daily at your child's school.
