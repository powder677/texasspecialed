Quick Facts: Evaluations in Northside ISD

  • What is it? FIE/FIIE = Full (Individual) Initial Evaluation to determine eligibility.
  • Who can request? Parents, teachers, or school staff can refer a child.
  • Timeline: 60 calendar days from signed consent to completed evaluation.
  • Cost: Free to parents; district covers testing.
  • Next Step: ARD meeting within the 60-day window to review results.

Request a Special Education Evaluation in Northside ISD

Essential guidance for San Antonio families. Learn how to start the evaluation process, understand timelines, and advocate for your child's needs in Northside Independent School District.

Common Questions About Northside ISD Evaluations

```

How do I request a special education evaluation in Northside ISD?

Submit a written request to the Executive Director of Special Education. You may send it to 5900 Evers Rd, San Antonio, TX 78238.

Sample language: "I am formally requesting a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child [Name], date of birth [DOB], currently enrolled at [School]."

How long does the evaluation process take in Northside ISD?

60 calendar days maximum. Once Northside ISD receives your signed written consent, they have 60 calendar days to complete the process.

What tests are included in a Northside ISD evaluation?

Assessments vary by suspected disability. Testing can include academic achievement, speech and language, psychological/cognitive assessments, and dyslexia screening under HB 3928.

Does my child need a diagnosis before requesting an evaluation?

No. You do not need a doctor's diagnosis to start the evaluation process in Northside ISD.

Can I request an Independent Educational Evaluation (IEE) in Northside ISD?

Yes. If you disagree with the district's evaluation, you have the right to request an IEE at public expense.

What happens after the evaluation is complete?

Initial ARD meeting. The ARD committee will meet within the 60-day window to review reports and determine eligibility for services.

```

What is a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE)?

A FIIE is a comprehensive assessment to determine eligibility for special education. It assesses whether your child has a disability and if that disability impacts their educational performance.


How to Request an Evaluation in Northside ISD

```

Step 1: Submit Written Request

Send a written evaluation request to the district.

Where to Send Your Request:

Primary Contact: Executive Director of Special Education

Phone: 210-397-8500

Address: 5900 Evers Rd, San Antonio, TX 78238

Sample Evaluation Request Letter


```

[Date]

Executive Director of Special Education
Northside ISD
5900 Evers Rd
San Antonio, TX 78238

To the Director of Special Education,

I am writing to formally request a Full Individual Initial Evaluation (FIIE) for my child, [Child's Full Name], date of birth [DOB], currently enrolled at [School Name].

I am concerned about my child's progress in [Academic Area/Behavior/Speech]. I believe [Child's Name] may have a disability that requires special education services under IDEA.

Please provide me with the necessary consent forms to begin this process. I understand that the evaluation timeline begins once you receive my signed consent.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
Download Fillable Template
```

Step 2: District Response & Consent

Northside ISD must provide a response (Prior Written Notice) within 15 school days of your request. If they agree to evaluate, they will provide a Consent for Evaluation form.

Step 3: Evaluation Period (60 Calendar Days)

The 60 calendar day timeline begins once the district receives your signed written consent. Assessments are conducted in all areas of suspected disability.

Step 4: Initial ARD Meeting

Within the 60-day window, the district must complete evaluations and schedule an ARD meeting to review results and determine eligibility.

```

What Tests and Assessments Are Included?

Evaluations in Northside ISD may include:

Academic Testing

Reading, math, and writing assessments.

Speech & Language

Evaluations of communication and speech skills.

Psychological

Cognitive or IQ testing and behavioral assessments.

Specialized Screenings

Dyslexia screening under Texas HB 3928 protocols.

Independent Educational Evaluations (IEE)

If you disagree with Northside ISD's evaluation, you have the right to request an IEE at public expense. This is an evaluation conducted by a professional not employed by the district.
