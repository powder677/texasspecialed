Understanding the Northside ISD Special Education Model

Northside ISD is the largest district in San Antonio, managing services through a "Cluster System" directed by the Northside Learning Center. For parents navigating the process, the primary point of authority is the Campus Special Education Coordinator stationed at each neighborhood school. The district provides specialized placements including the Applied Learning Environment (ALE) for cognitive support and the Behavior Mastery Center (BMC) for intensive behavioral intervention.

Under Board Policy EHBA(LEGAL), the district must complete initial evaluations within 45 school days of signed consent. For students whose needs exceed comprehensive campus resources, Northside operates separate facilities like the Holmgreen Center and the Reddix Center. These programs are governed by the same state timelines but offer higher staffing ratios for therapeutic and functional support.

If a dispute arises during the Admission, Review, and Dismissal (ARD) process, families are encouraged to resolve issues at the campus level before escalating to the Evers Road administrative team via Board Policy FNG(LOCAL). The district also integrates family support through the Northside Family Engagement Center, which provides wraparound services for students with disabilities.
